A dungeon set beneath the waves near Thavnair, where the legendary treasure vault of Satrap Alzadaal III awaits. Players explore ancient ruins filled with mechanical guardians and aquatic threats.

## Ambujam

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Big Wave | **Raidwide** | Raidwide damage that inflicts bleed DoT; healers attend to DoT |
| Tentacle Dig | **AoE** | Summons tentacles that move and create large AoEs where they land; move to north/south corners |
| Toxic Fountain | **AoE** | Three sets of circle AoEs explode in sequence; stand in unexploded circles, then move |

**Notes:** Tentacle Dig inflicts poison and vulnerability stacks if hit. Move to the corners to avoid the tentacles' landing zones. Watch the sequence of Toxic Fountain carefully.

## Armored Chariot

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rail Cannon | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Diffusion Ray | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raidwide; healers prepare AoE healing |
| Graviton Cannon | **Spread** | AoEs on each party member; spread out |
| Articulated Bits | **AoE** | Boss moves to center with electricity puddle; Death Claws at edges fire sequential line AoEs. Also casts conal AoE where shields point |

**Notes:** During Articulated Bits, watch both the Death Claws' firing order and the boss's shield direction. Stand in gaps between the laser beams while avoiding the shield cone.

## Kapikulu

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Billowing Bolts | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Spin Out | **AoE** | Seizes all players causing spinning; move in curves to avoid boss attacks |
| Magnitude Opus | **Stack** | Stack marker; stay together near targeted player |
| Rotary Gale | **Spread** | Spread apart to distribute damage evenly |
| Spike Traps | **AoE** | Floor traps activate periodically; inflict bleed if stepped on |
| Crewel Slice | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |

**Notes:** Watch for the spike traps that periodically become active. During Spin Out, you'll move in arcs, so plan your path accordingly to avoid hazards.
