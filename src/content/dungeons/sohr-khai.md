The sacred refuge of the great wyrm Hraesvelgr, located high above the Churning Mists.

## Chieftain Moglin

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Captain Mogsun | **Adds** | Kill this add to prevent tethering to the boss - Moglin periodically revives him |
| Moogle Revival | **Adds** | Knock moogles out of purple puddles before Moglin finishes reviving them |
| Floating Weapon | **AoE** | Cross-shaped attacks from a floating weapon - stay away from it |
| Pom Flare | **AoE** | Large AoE from Pomguard Pomfryer - priority target |

**Notes:** 
- Focus on killing Captain Mogsun quickly
- Watch for moogles in purple puddles and knock them out
- Prioritize Pomguard Pomfryer when it appears

## Poqhiraj

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Burning Bright | **AoE** | Lightning line attack hitting all players in line of sight - move perpendicular to avoid |
| Wall Destruction | **AoE** | Hitting walls destroys them and creates electrified floors |
| Touchdown | **AoE** | Boss flies up and drops with proximity damage - move to opposite side of arena |
| Gallop | **Knockback** | Spreading line markers after landing - move away from center to avoid knockback |
| Cloud Call | **AoE** | Targets random players with Dark Clouds casting Lightning Bolt circle attacks |

**Notes:** 
- Avoid destroying walls if possible to maintain safe zones
- Move far from proximity markers when boss flies up
- Dark Clouds inflict Vulnerability Up - dodge their attacks

## Hraesvelgr

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wyrmclaw | **Tankbuster** | High-damage attack on the highest enmity player |
| Hallowed Wings | **AoE** | One wing glows - a line attack hits that side; stay on the opposite side |
| Holy Storm | **Raidwide** | Damages all players |
| Hallowed Dive | **AoE** | Line attack through middle followed by circle attacks on sides |
| Akh Morn | **Stack** | Stack marker on a random player - all unmarked players stack to split damage |

**Notes:** 
- Watch which wing glows to know which side is safe
- For Hallowed Dive: dodge the line first, then move into its path to avoid the circle attacks
- Akh Morn hits multiple times - stay stacked
