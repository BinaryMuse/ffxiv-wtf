The first dungeon in FFXIV, located in Western La Noscea. A simple introduction to dungeon mechanics.

## Chopper

The first boss is a straightforward coeurl enemy.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Charged Whisker | **AoE** | Frontal cone attack - avoid standing in front |

**Notes:** Tank should face boss away from party.

## Captain Madison

A pirate captain who summons adds during the fight.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Add Spawns | **Adds** | Summons scurvy dogs throughout the fight |

**Notes:** Kill adds as they spawn. Captain will try to open a gate for reinforcements - interact with the Inconspicuous Switch to close it.

## Denn the Orcatoothed

The final boss, a sahagin priest.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hydroball | **AoE** | Frontal cone AoE that can inflict Silence |
| Add Spawns | **Adds** | Summons Unnatural Ripples that spawn sahagin adds if not killed |

**Notes:** 
- Prioritize killing Unnatural Ripples before they spawn adds
- Avoid standing in front of the boss during Hydroball
