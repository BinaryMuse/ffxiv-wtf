The first dungeon of Shadowbringers, set in a village overrun by sin eaters in Lakeland. Players must fight through the corrupted settlement to confront the Lightwarden.

## Forgiven Dissonance

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Pillory | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Path of Light | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Gibbet Cage | **AoE** | Point-blank circle around boss; move away |
| Wooden Horse | **Cleave** | Large frontal cone AoE; get behind boss |
| Brazen Bull | **AoE** | Orbs spawn and create criss-cross column AoEs; find safe zones between columns |
| Thumbscrew | **AoE** | Divebomb column AoE targeting safe zone edges |

**Notes:** The Brazen Bull patterns can overlap with Thumbscrew, requiring quick repositioning to safe spots.

## Tesleen, the Forgiven

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| The Tickler | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Scold's Bridle | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Fevered Flagellation | **Spread** | Players marked 1-4; boss attacks in order. Spread to avoid splash damage |
| Exorcise | **Stack** | Four puddles appear; one player gets stack marker. Stack together, then spread immediately after to avoid overlapping fifth puddle |

**Notes:** Watch for the number markers during Fevered Flagellation and ensure you're not near the player being targeted next.

## Lightwarden Philia

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Headcrusher | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Scavenger's Daughter | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Chain Down | **Adds** | Players get chained; attack chains quickly or die |
| Pendulum | **AoE** | Two-part proximity AoE; move away from impact points |
| Left/Right Knout | **Cleave** | >180-degree arc cleave based on raised arm; comes in pairs |
| Fierce Beating | **AoE** | Sequential circle AoEs; keep moving |

**Notes:** Pay close attention to which arm Philia raises—this indicates which side of the arena will be hit (>180-degree arc). Left/Right Knout comes in pairs. Chain mechanic requires fast DPS.
