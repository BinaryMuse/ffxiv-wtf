A dungeon set in the sacred Dravanian mountain Sohm Al, exploring the path to the Churning Mists.

## Raskovnik

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Acid Rain | **AoE** | Circular AoEs target all players - spread out and dodge |
| Phytobeam | **AoE** | Line attack at random players - move out quickly |
| Sweet Scent | **Adds** | Summons Dravanian Hornets that move toward the boss |
| Devour | **AoE** | Boss eats hornets - kill them before this or boss gains damage buff |
| Spit | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage requiring healing |

**Notes:** 
- One player gets marked and pulled to center, then hit with point-blank AoE - run away immediately
- Kill the hornets before the boss can devour them

## Myath

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Third Leg Forward | **Tankbuster** | High damage on primary threat target |
| Frontal Cone | **Cleave** | Large cone AoE in front - move out of the way |
| Primordial Roar | **Adds** | Boss spawns colored slimes and throws them at marked players |

**Slime Mechanics:**
- **Red slime (Blood of the Mountain)**: All players stack on marked target to soak damage
- **Blue slime (Rheum of the Mountain)**: Marked player spreads away from group
- **Green slime (Chyme of the Mountain)**: **Priority add** - ALL players DPS it down before its cast completes or the group wipes

**Notes:** The green slime is a wipe mechanic - focus it immediately when it spawns.

## Tioman

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Chaos Blast | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Comet | **AoE** | Drops comets around the arena - avoid impact zones |
| Dark Star | **AoE** | Avoid the marked zones |
| Heavensfall | **AoE** | Large AoE attacks to dodge |

**Notes:** 
- Tioman is the consort of Nidhogg
- Watch for wing mechanics that spawn during the fight
- Stay mobile and avoid overlapping AoE zones
