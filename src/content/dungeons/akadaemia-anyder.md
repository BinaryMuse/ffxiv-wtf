An Amaurotine research facility dedicated to creating concepts and creatures. Players explore the underwater academy while confronting the powerful creations within, including the Guardian Force Quetzalcoatl.

## Cladoselache & Doliodus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Protolithic Puncture | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Marine Mayhem | **Raidwide** | Repeated party damage until one shark dies |
| Tidal Guillotine (land) | **AoE** | Large circle around boss on land; move away |
| Tidal Guillotine (water) | **AoE** | Shark jumps out creating circle AoE where it surfaces |
| Pelagic Cleaver (land) | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE |
| Pelagic Cleaver (water) | **Cleave** | Larger frontal cone from water; move to sides |
| Aquatic Lance | **Spread** | Random player marked with circle AoE; spread away |

**Notes:** Two sharks alternate between arena and water. Attacks change based on location—water attacks are generally larger. Kill one to stop Marine Mayhem damage.

## Marquis Morbol

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Arbor Storm | **Raidwide** | Party-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Sap Shower | **Spread** | All players marked with orange circles; spread to avoid overlap |
| Extensible Tendrils | **AoE** | Boss slams tendrils down 5 times; stand in "armpits" between tendrils |
| Bile Vomit | **Cleave** | Unmarked cone immediately after 5th tendril slam; stay to boss's side |
| Blossom | **AoE** | Purple glowing flowers are AoE zones; avoid standing on them |

**Notes:** After the fifth Extensible Tendrils hit, immediately move to the boss's side to avoid Bile Vomit. The combo is quick.

## Quetzalcoatl

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Shockbolt | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Thunderbolt | **Raidwide** | Heavy party-wide damage; healers top everyone up |
| Thunderstorm | **Spread** | Players and arena targeted by circle AoEs; purple orbs left behind give damage buff |
| Shocking Plumage | **AoE** | Four cone AoEs from boss in all directions; stand between cones |
| Winding Current | **Knockback** | Boss teleports south and knocks players north through growing orb AoEs; get into boss hitbox |

**Notes:** Collect purple orbs from Thunderstorm for damage buff. During Winding Current, the only safe zone is inside the boss's hitbox—cross the arena quickly.
