An ancient Amdapori holy place in the South Shroud that has been corrupted by voidsent. One of the first level 50 dungeons available.

## Psycheflayer

The fight begins with a Lunatic Priest who summons the true boss at 50% HP.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mind Melt | **Raidwide** | Drains MP from targets - hide behind pillars to avoid |
| Stone Adds | **Adds** | Priority targets that spawn throughout the fight |
| Disease | **AoE** | One add inflicts Disease - requires Esuna to remove |
| Rock Elemental | **Adds** | Massive elemental spawns late - avoid its huge red circle AoE |

**Notes:** 
- Kill the Lunatic Priest to 50% to spawn Psycheflayer
- Prioritize stone adds throughout the fight
- Hide behind pillars during Mind Melt to avoid MP drain
- When the large elemental spawns, move outside its AoE or stack against a pillar

## Demon Wall

A famous DPS check - the wall pushes forward and wipes the party if it reaches the end.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Void Zones | **AoE** | Cast on the floor alternating middle and sides - cause damage and Slow |
| Repel | **Knockback** | Knocks the party backward - face directly toward the boss |
| Liquefy | **AoE** | Creates puddles on the ground |

**Notes:** 
- This is a hard DPS check - burn the boss before it pushes you off
- Void zones alternate between center and sides - move accordingly
- Face directly toward the boss during Repel to control knockback direction
- Healers must be prepared for consistent unavoidable damage

## Anantaboga

The final boss, a large voidsent dragon.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Imminent Catastrophe | **Gaze** | Look away from the boss to avoid Doom |
| Tail Drive | **Cleave** | Rear cone attack - don't stand behind |
| Plague Dance | **AoE** | Circular AoE around the boss |
| Add Spawns | **Adds** | Spawns void creatures throughout the fight |

**Notes:** 
- Tank should position the boss carefully for party safety
- Look away during Imminent Catastrophe to avoid Doom debuff
- Avoid standing directly behind the boss due to Tail Drive
