A dungeon set in the once-verdant forest of Vanaspati in Thavnair, now twisted and corrupted by the Final Days. Players must navigate through the apocalyptic landscape and confront the Terminus beasts that have manifested from the people's despair.

## Terminus Snatcher

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Last Gasp | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Note of Despair | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Mouth Off | **AoE** | Spawns mouths around arena; stand on closed mouths to avoid open mouth explosions |
| Lost Hope | **AoE** | Inflicts Temporary Misdirection; movement inputs are reversed |
| What is Left/Right | **Cleave** | Half-room cleave; dodge to safe side while avoiding Mouth Off explosions |

**Notes:** During Lost Hope, your movement directions are reversed. Use rapid movement inputs to quickly determine which direction you're actually moving. Combine this awareness with Mouth Off patterns.

## Terminus Wrecker

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Total Wreck | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Meaningless Destruction | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Poison Heart | **Stack** | Stack marker; group up to split damage |
| Unholy Water | **AoE** | Creates water bubbles that stun if touched (except during fire spray) |
| Aether Spray (Fire) | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE; enter water bubbles for protection |
| Aether Spray (Water) | **Knockback** | Knockback from center; avoid being knocked into bubbles or arena edges |

**Notes:** Aether Spray has two forms. Fire version requires you to stand in the water bubbles for protection. Water version is a knockback—position to avoid being pushed into bubbles or the edge.

## Svarbhanu

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Flames of Decay | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Aetherial Disruption | **AoE** | Arena splits into four lanes with blue triangles or red circles; screen glows with matching symbols showing unsafe lanes |
| Crumbling Sky | **AoE** | Projectiles create stacked AoE damage zones |
| Cosmic Kiss | **Knockback** | Knockback that may push into blast zones; position carefully |
| Gnashing of Teeth | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |

**Notes:** During Aetherial Disruption, look at the symbols on your screen edges. Match the symbols (blue triangles or red circles) to identify which lanes will explode, then move to safe lanes.
