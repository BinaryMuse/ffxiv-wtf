A poisonous cave in Coerthas Central Highlands filled with toxic Morbol creatures and deadly gold-tinted pools. Infamous for its difficulty.

## Locksmith

A Morbol that inflicts stacking poison debuffs.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Gold Lung | **Raidwide** | Stacking debuff dealing damage over time - cannot be Esuna'd |
| Morbol Fruit | **AoE** | Eat fruit around the arena to remove Gold Lung stacks |
| Bad Breath | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack with multiple debuffs |

**Notes:** 
- Gold Lung stacks and deals increasing damage - eat Morbol Fruit to clear stacks
- Wait until 2 stacks before eating fruit as they respawn slowly
- Tank should position near a fruit; healer should stand near the left fruit
- Avoid the yellow Goldbile puddles throughout the arena

## Coincounter

A cyclops with no attack telegraphs - must watch the boss directly.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 10-Tonze Swipe | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack - no telegraph, watch positioning |
| 100-Tonze Swipe | **Cleave** | Heavier frontal cone attack - stay behind the boss |
| 100-Tonze Swing | **AoE** | Extremely powerful attack - run away immediately |
| Glower | **AoE** | Random AoE beam on a party member - can be interrupted |
| Eye of the Beholder | **Raidwide** | Aggro wipe with purple electricity |

**Notes:** 
- No red indicator circles - watch the boss's animations and facing
- Always stay behind the boss to avoid Swipe attacks
- DPS should hold back after aggro wipe so tank can regain control
- Interrupt Glower if possible

## Miser's Mistress

Another Morbol with an even more dangerous stacking debuff.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Burr Burrow | **Raidwide** | Stacking debuff dealing direct damage |
| Morbol Fruit | **AoE** | Eat fruit to remove debuff stacks |
| Bad Breath | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack with multiple debuffs |
| Hooked Burrs | **Adds** | Spawns add seedlings during the fight |

**Notes:** 
- Unlike Locksmith, this debuff deals direct damage per stack
- Morbol Fruits respawn much slower - wait for 3-4 stacks before eating
- Multiple phases add complexity to the fight
- Coordinate fruit usage with your party to avoid running out
