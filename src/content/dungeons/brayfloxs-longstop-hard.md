The Illuminati goblins have invaded Brayflox's home with magitek weapons. A hard mode featuring goblin technology and bomb mechanics.

## Illuminati Commander

A goblin commander who summons fixating adds.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Goblin Adds | **Adds** | Spawns adds that fixate on individual players |
| Frontal Attacks | **Cleave** | Standard frontal cleave attacks |

**Notes:** 
- Adds will fixate on specific players and chase them
- Manage add positioning while fighting the boss
- Kill adds when possible to reduce party damage

## Magitek Vangob G-III

A goblin-piloted magitek machine with zone control.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Blue Zones | **AoE** | Blue areas appear on the ground - avoid at all costs |
| Magitek Attacks | **AoE** | Various AoE patterns to dodge |

**Notes:** 
- Primary mechanic is avoiding blue zones
- Keep mobile and watch for zone spawns
- Straightforward fight focused on positioning

## Gobmachine G-VI

The final boss, a large magitek with bomb mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Bomb Spawns | **Adds** | Bombs spawn and must be pushed away from center |
| Giant Bomb | **Adds** | Large bomb spawns that must be killed |
| AoE Push | **AoE** | Use AoE attacks to push bombs away |
| Explosion | **Raidwide** | Bombs explode if not handled properly |

**Notes:** 
- Push bombs away from the center using attacks (AoEs work well)
- Kill the giant bomb when it spawns
- Coordinate bomb placement as a team
- Don't let bombs pile up in the center
