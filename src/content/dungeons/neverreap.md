A floating island inhabited by the Vanu Vanu beastmen, accessible after completing the Heavensward main scenario.

## Nunyenunc

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Filoplumes | **Cleave** | Frontal cone damage - tank should face boss away from party |
| Feather Squall | **AoE** | AoE attack that must be avoided |
| Disappear | **Adds** | Boss disappears and a misty area appears - find and destroy Nunyenunc's Shadow |
| Nunyeninc Feathers | **Adds** | Destroy immediately - they explode and inflict Vulnerability Up |

**Notes:** 
- When the boss disappears, quickly locate and kill the Shadow
- Prioritize destroying feathers before they explode

## Canu Vanu

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Morrowcloud | **AoE** | Targets a random player with Bleed - healers should cleanse |
| Sacred Totem | **AoE** | Marks players who drop totems |
| Totem Chant | **Adds** | Creates a purple circle - move any totems out of the circle before cast finishes |

**Notes:** 
- If a totem stays inside the purple circle, the boss becomes immune
- Destroy the resulting Canu Vanu's Totem to remove immunity

## Waukkeon

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Divine Mist | **Adds** | Random marks spawn Divine Mist adds |
| Divine Gale | **Adds** | Boss spawns four to five Divine Gale adds and rushes around the arena |
| Turbine | **Knockback** | Party-wide knockback after adds are destroyed - stack near boss to avoid falling off |
| Tornadoes | **AoE** | Moving AoEs that cause damage and knockback - avoid them |

**Notes:** 
- Kill Divine Mists first, then Divine Gales
- After all adds are destroyed, stack near the boss for Turbine
- Watch for moving tornadoes throughout the fight
