A mansion floating in the void, home to the reclusive mage Beq Lugg. Players explore the enchanted estate filled with magical creatures and animated furniture to uncover its secrets.

## Seeker of Solitude

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Shadowbolt | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Immortal Anathema | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Dark Pulse | **Stack** | Stack marker on random player; group together |
| Dark Well | **Spread** | All players marked with circle AoEs; spread apart |
| Tribulation | **AoE** | Dirt puddles spawn; brooms sweep and explode near puddles. Avoid puddles and broom paths |

**Notes:** During Tribulation, start on the side with puddles far from approaching brooms, then follow behind the middle broom after it passes.

## Leannan Sith

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Storm of Color | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Ode to Lost Love | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Direct Seeding | **AoE** | Seeds fall on grassy squares; pick up and drop on dry cracked earth before Gardener's Hymn |
| Ode to Far Winds | **Spread** | Circle markers in area plus player markers; find separate safe spots |
| Ode to Fallen Petals | **AoE** | Donut attack; move into melee range of boss |
| Gardener's Hymn | **Adds** | Seeds left on grass become Lover's Ring enemies |

**Notes:** Later Direct Seeding includes knockback—place seeds where they'll be knocked onto dry earth, not grass.

## Lugus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Captive Bolt | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Culling Blade | **Raidwide** | Damages all; spawns new flammable furniture if needed. Avoid SW, SE corners and N center |
| Scorching Left/Right | **Cleave** | Half-room cleave; watch boss's sword position and swirling mist |
| Otherworldly Heat | **Spread** | Players marked; cross-shaped AoEs appear at player locations. Stay diagonal and away; avoid burning furniture |
| Mortal Flame | **AoE** | Players inflicted with fire debuff; transfer to furniture or die |
| Fire's Domain | **Spread** | Players marked 1-4 with swords; boss dashes to each in order with cone attack. Stay far, near walls |

**Notes:** Preserve furniture for Mortal Flame mechanic. During Fire's Domain, the dash damage decreases with distance, and cone attacks can burn furniture—stay near N/S walls.
