Ishgardian forces attempt to reclaim the vigil with adventurer assistance. Features unique mechanics including cannon combat.

## Gorynich

A three-headed dragon with dangerous add mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rake | **Tankbuster** | Single-target attack on the tank |
| Lion's Breath | **AoE** | Cone AoE at a random player |
| Swinge | **Cleave** | Large cone AoE after boss walks to wall - spread out |
| Vigil Heir | **Adds** | Adds that buff boss if killed near it |

**Notes:** 
- A dedicated DPS must drag Vigil Heir adds to the opposite side of the room
- If adds die near Gorynich, he gains damage and haste buffs
- Spread out during Swinge
- Tank faces boss away from party

## Cuca Fera

A unique encounter fought entirely with cannons.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Barrier | **AoE** | Boss creates a protective shield |
| Room-wide Blast | **Raidwide** | Must be interrupted with cannons |
| Fiery Spin | **AoE** | Spinning fire attack |
| Iron Kiss | **AoE** | Cannon attack - damages boss and adds (reflected if barrier active) |
| Spindly Fingers | **AoE** | Cannon attack - interrupts blast and destroys barrier |

**Notes:** 
- Use cannons positioned in the room corners to fight the boss
- Use Iron Kiss to deal damage (don't use when barrier is active)
- Use Spindly Fingers to interrupt room-wide blast and destroy barrier
- Easiest boss when mechanics are understood

## Giruveganaus

A dragon that cannot be tanked traditionally - attacks are random.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Shoulder Tackle | **Cleave** | Boss lowers right shoulder before charging |
| Fire Blast | **Cleave** | Boss rises on hind legs - stand behind to avoid |
| Tail Swipe | **Cleave** | Boss raises tail - stand on right side to dodge |
| Fire Breath | **Raidwide** | No tells, unavoidable, but low damage |
| Infirmity | **Raidwide** | All attacks apply this debuff, reducing magical healing |
| Second Dragon | **Adds** | At 50% HP, a second Giruveganaus spawns |

**Notes:** 
- This is a dodge-heavy fight - watch the dragon's animations
- All attacks apply Infirmity (reduced healing received)
- When second dragon spawns at 50%, kill the original first
- Watch for animation tells:
  - Right shoulder lower = Shoulder Tackle
  - Rises on hind legs = Fire Blast (get behind)
  - Tail raises = Tail Swipe (stand on right side)
