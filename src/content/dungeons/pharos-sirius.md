A lighthouse on the shores of Western La Noscea corrupted by the Calamity's aether. Features challenging mechanics for a level 50 dungeon.

## Symond the Unsinkable

An undead pirate captain with adds that cannot be tanked normally.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Corrupting Spit | **Adds** | Adds apply stacking Corrupted Crystal debuff - 3 stacks kills the tank |
| Crystalline Shot | **AoE** | Growing yellow AoE that expands three times and applies debuff |
| Giga Slash | **AoE** | Large AoE with knockback - tank against wall |

**Notes:** 
- DPS must handle adds - tank cannot tank them due to stacking debuff
- Tank should position against the wall for Giga Slash knockback
- Add waves spawn based on boss HP percentage
- Slower DPS actually helps manage add spawns better

## Zu

A large bird surrounded by eggs in the arena.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Egg Hatching | **Adds** | Eggs glow grey/purple when close to hatching - destroy them |
| Damage Buff | **Raidwide** | Destroying eggs gives boss stacking +8 damage buff |
| Add Spawns | **Adds** | Hatched eggs become adds that deal moderate-high damage |

**Notes:** 
- DPS should destroy glowing eggs before they hatch
- Keep the boss's damage buff stacks below +10 or it becomes dangerous
- Tank should grab threat on any hatched adds
- The boss itself does minimal damage without stacks

## Tyrant

A corrupted creature encountered on the lighthouse stairs.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Aether Detonation | **AoE** | Corrupted crystals drop and periodically explode |
| Creeping Darkness | **AoE** | Frontal cone attack |

**Notes:** 
- Avoid standing near corrupted crystals when they're about to explode
- Keep moving to avoid crystal hazards

## Siren

The final boss - a healer check with charm mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Song of Torment | **Tankbuster** | Applies bleeding effect to the tank |
| Lunatic Voice | **Raidwide** | AoE applying Reduced Immunity - healers must dispel immediately |
| Dreamy Voice | **AoE** | Single-target charm - if cast on healer, often wipes the party |
| Aerial Charge | **AoE** | Siren flies up and charges in a straight line - watch the outer ring |
| Aerial Charm | **AoE** | Siren appears in center - only safe spot is directly under her |

**Notes:** 
- Kill Sergeant adds first - they can stun party members
- Dispel Reduced Immunity from Lunatic Voice immediately
- When Siren flies: outer ring = line charge, center = charm AoE (stand under her)
- Dreamy Voice on the healer is often a wipe condition
