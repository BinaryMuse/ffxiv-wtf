Abandoned mines in Western Thanalan infested with hostile creatures. The Immortal Flames need adventurers to clear out the hecatoncheires that have taken over.

## Kottos

A gigas that spawns after defeating waves of Spriggan adds.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Grand Slam | **Tankbuster** | High damage to area around tank - tank move away from party |
| Lumbering Leap | **AoE** | Boss jumps to marked location, then random puddles erupt |
| Colossal Slam | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE - keep boss facing away from party |

**Notes:** Tank should face the boss away from the party and stay separated from DPS/healer.

## Ichorous Ire

A slime that cannot be damaged by normal attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Blasting Device | **AoE** | Use Improved Blasting Device to damage the boss |
| Spriggan Adds | **Adds** | Kill Spriggans to protect the Blasting Cap |

**Notes:** 
- The boss must be damaged 3 times with the Blasting Device to defeat it
- Blasting Cap spawns and places a red AoE - boss must be standing in it when detonated
- Kill Spriggan adds quickly to protect the Blasting Cap

## Gyges the Great

A large hecatoncheir that breaks open boulders to summon adds.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Colossal Slam | **Cleave** | Small frontal cone AoE |
| Gigantic Swing | **AoE** | Donut AoE - safe spot is directly under the boss |
| Gigantic Smash | **AoE** | Opposite pattern - move away from boss |
| Stone Servants | **Adds** | Boss breaks boulder to spawn adds - prioritize killing them |

**Notes:** 
- Kill Stone Servant adds quickly before they break the second boulder
- If second boulder breaks, more adds spawn and can overwhelm the party
