An ancient Far Eastern temple housing the tamate-bako, a powerful geomancy relic guarded by divine auspices.

## Otengu

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clout of the Tengu | **Raidwide** | Moderate group damage - heal and shield |
| Yama-kagura | **AoE** | Column AoE targeting random player - dodge |
| Tengu Embers | **AoE** | Embers spawn and spiral - avoid or get Separation debuff |
| Ember Explosion | **AoE** | Embers stop and explode in circular AoEs |
| Wile of the Tengu | **Gaze** | Turn away or get Hysteria debuff causing uncontrolled running |
| Might of the Tengu | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on tank |

**Notes:**
- Simple tank and spank - avoid AoEs and gaze attacks
- Getting hit by embers applies stacking Separation debuff
- Turn away during Wile of the Tengu to avoid Hysteria

## Daidarabotchi

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ground Pound | **AoE** | No telegraph - watch which arm boss raises to determine side |
| Cone AoE | **AoE** | Multiple players targeted by thin cone AoEs |
| Circular AoE | **Spread** | Blue markers drop circular AoEs - avoid other marked players |
| Earth Spikes | **AoE** | DPS targeted by following multi-hit circular AoEs - avoid others |
| Mythmaker | **AoE** | Lake spawns in center and grows - standing too long kills you |

**Notes:**
- Boss is stationary and attacks with arms
- Watch which arm is raised to know which side gets hit
- The lake from Mythmaker grows over time - don't stand in it

## Qitian Dasheng

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| The Short End | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on tank |
| Both Ends (Normal) | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE if boss has normal staff - run out |
| Both Ends (Glowing) | **AoE** | Donut AoE with knockback if staff glows - stay in melee |
| Mount Huaguo | **Raidwide** | Moderate group damage |
| Monkey Magicks | **Adds** | Servant of the Sage adds spawn - kill quickly |
| Second Heaven | **Raidwide** | Damage based on Duty Gauge from surviving adds |
| Splitting Hairs | **Adds** | Boss clones - kill both together or survivor respawns clone |
| The Long End | **AoE** | Players tethered to boss, circular AoEs, then column |
| Five-fingered Punishment | **Stack** | Stack marker on one player |

**Notes:**
- Arena edge is dangerous - avoid knockbacks into it (Bleed + Vuln debuffs)
- Watch for normal vs glowing staff: normal = out, glowing = in
- Kill Servant adds fast to minimize Second Heaven damage
- During Splitting Hairs, kill both clones together
