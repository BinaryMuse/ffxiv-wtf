The workshop of the legendary Sharlayan archon Matoya, filled with her magical experiments. Players navigate through enchanted brooms, living mud, and porxie familiars in this whimsical dungeon.

## Mudman

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hard Rock | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Stone Age | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable party-wide damage |
| Peat Pelt | **AoE** | Four mud balls tether to players; position at drainage systems so balls roll into drains |
| Falling Rock | **Stack** | Stack marker; group together for heavy hit |
| Brittle Breccia | **AoE** | Expanding 3/4 circle AoE; stand behind boss or in already-hit areas |

**Notes:** During later Peat Pelt casts, some drains are blocked by mudmen—avoid sending balls to blocked drains or they'll bounce back.

## Nixie

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Crash-Smash | **Tankbuster** | Spawns four icicles tethered to tank; others avoid tether paths |
| Shower Power | **AoE** | Three of four water pipes activate with line attacks; stand in front of inactive pipe |
| Pitter-Patter | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Cloud Platform | **AoE** | Boss jumps to cloud above; use water sprouts to launch up, defeat Nixies, return before knockback |

**Notes:** Later phases combine Shower Power with circle AoEs—identify the inactive pipe while spreading for circle markers.

## Mother Porxie

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tender Loin | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage; shields recommended |
| Huff and Puff | **Knockback** | Large knockback; safe zone is small area directly in front of boss |
| Minced Meat | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Open Flame | **AoE** | Fire puddles spawn around arena |
| Suckling Frenzy | **Adds** | Porxie adds spawn; AoE them down |
| To a Crisp | **Spread** | Players marked with spread markers |

**Notes:** For Huff and Puff, the only safe spot is a small area directly in front of the boss in the center—stand there to avoid being knocked into hazards.
