The seat of Garlean power in Doma, now under siege as the liberation forces push to reclaim their homeland.

## Magitek Rearguard

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Garlean Fire | **AoE** | Column AoE targeting a player that hits multiple times |
| Magitek Bits | **Adds** | Spawns adds that telegraph column AoEs across the arena |
| Rearguard Mines | **AoE** | Mines spawn on arena sides and travel across - don't touch them |

**Notes:**
- Tank should face the boss away from the party
- Mines cannot be killed and explode if touched
- Dodge the column AoEs from Magitek Bits

## Magitek Hexadrone

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Circle of Death | **AoE** | Point-blank circular AoE around the boss |
| 2-Tonze Magitek Missile | **Stack** | Stack marker requiring all players to group together |
| Magitek Bits | **Knockback** | Column AoEs with orange tethers that knock players back |
| Magitek Missiles | **AoE** | Pillars spawn - all players must stand near one to mitigate damage |

**Notes:**
- Avoid excessive movement during Magitek Bits phases
- Stand near pillars when they spawn to survive Magitek Missiles
- Stack together for the 2-Tonze Magitek Missile

## Hypertuned Grynewaht

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Chainsaw | **Cleave** | Long frontal attack lasting several seconds - tank must move away |
| Delay-Action Charge | **Tankbuster** | Untelegraphed attack requiring tank movement |
| Prey | **AoE** | Marks targeted players - avoid trapping the group |
| Sawblades | **AoE** | Spawn around arena edges with AoE patterns |
| Magitek Bits | **Knockback** | Knockback tethers similar to previous fight |

**Notes:**
- Stay mobile and watch for overlapping attack patterns
- Tank should move away from the party during Chainsaw
- Players marked with Prey should position away from others
