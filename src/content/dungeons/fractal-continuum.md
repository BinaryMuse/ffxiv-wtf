An Allagan museum facility in Azys Lla containing preserved specimens and malfunctioning exhibits.

## Phantom Ray

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rapid Sever | **Tankbuster** | High damage on primary threat target |
| Double Sever | **AoE** | Alternating cone AoEs hitting front/back then left/right quarters |
| Double Sever II | **AoE** | Same cones plus multiple random circular AoEs around the arena |

**Notes:** 
- High HP boss with many simultaneous AoEs
- Stay mobile and dodge the quarter-arena attacks

## Minotaur

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 11-tonze Swipe | **Cleave** | Frontal cleave with **no visual telegraph** - tanks keep boss facing away |
| 111-tonze Swipe | **Knockback** | Circular point-blank AoE with knockback |
| Disorienting Groan | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE followed by boss charging random players |
| 10-tonze Slash | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack after charging |
| 1111-tonze Swipe | **Adds** | **Wipe mechanic** - must be interrupted (see notes) |

**Notes:** 
- **No visual telegraphs** - watch the cast bar carefully
- To interrupt 1111-tonze Swipe: interact with Biomass Incubators around the arena to summon a Continuum Conservator
- Kill the Conservator add immediately or the boss will consume it and gain buffs

## The Curator

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clockwork Alarum | **Adds** | Switch DPS to these adds immediately when they spawn |
| Glowing Tiles | **AoE** | Avoid standing on glowing floor tiles |
| Bomb Debuff | **AoE** | Players with this debuff should stay away from others until it expires |

**Notes:** 
- Prioritize killing adds over damaging the boss
- Don't stand on glowing tiles
- Spread out if you have the bomb debuff
