The first dungeon of Endwalker, set in a floating tower that serves as the domain of the Magus Sisters. Players ascend the tower to confront these powerful mages who serve as enforcers for the Telophoroi.

## Minduruva

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Manusya Bio | **Tankbuster** | Inflicts poison; use cooldowns and cleanse with Esuna |
| Manusya Blizzard III | **AoE** | Conal AoE radiating from boss; move to safe side |
| Manusya Fire III | **AoE** | Donut-shaped AoE; stay in melee range of boss |
| Manusya Thunder III | **AoE** | Multiple AoEs in cardinal directions; stand diagonally |
| Manusya Bio III | **Cleave** | Half-arena frontal cleave; get behind boss |
| Dhrupad | **Raidwide** | Hits all non-tank players with elemental damage; applies uncleansable DoT |
| Transmute | **AoE** | Creates orbs that travel to markers; memorize which spell each orb will cast |

**Notes:** Transmute requires memorizing which orb corresponds to which spell via the buff icons. Fire = get close, Blizzard = avoid cone, Thunder = avoid marked areas.

## Sanduruva

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Isitva Siddhi | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Prapti Siddhi | **AoE** | Fires line AoEs at each party member in succession; keep moving |
| Manusya Berserk | **AoE** | Spawns five orbs that explode; stand against wall in gap between orbs |
| Explosive Force | **AoE** | Detonates the orbs from Manusya Berserk; getting hit inflicts Berserk |
| Prakamya Siddhi | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE around boss; move away |

**Notes:** Watch for the safe spot against the wall during Explosive Force. Being hit by orbs causes you to involuntarily attack the boss.

## Cinduruva

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Samsara | **Raidwide** | Heavy group-wide damage; healers prepare |
| Delta Attack | **AoE** | Combines Fire, Blizzard, and Thunder mechanics from earlier fights |
| Dhrupad | **Raidwide** | All three sisters channel damage on non-tank players |
| Manusya Reflect | **AoE** | Sisters rotate through spells; dodge based on visual cues |

**Notes:** The final fight combines all three Magus Sisters' mechanics. Watch for familiar ability tells from the earlier encounters and react accordingly.
