An ancient city of the Amdapori civilization deep in the South Shroud, now overrun by voidsent and the demon Diabolos.

## Decaying Gourmand

A massive morbol-like creature that devours players.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Beatdown | **Cleave** | Frontal AoE with knockback |
| Moldy Phlegm | **AoE** | Creates poison puddles dealing damage over time |
| Devour | **Adds** | Marks prey, pulls them into Voracious Jaw - destroy the jaw to save them |
| Voracious Jaw | **Adds** | Trapped player takes continuous damage until freed |

**Notes:** 
- When a player is marked as prey, they will be devoured
- Other players must quickly destroy the Voracious Jaw to free them
- Avoid standing in poison puddles
- Tank should face boss away due to Beatdown knockback

## Arioch

A flying demon that summons wamoura adds.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ranch Wamoura | **Adds** | Boss is untargetable while adds are alive |
| Debuff Stacks | **Tankbuster** | Killing wamouras applies stacks - boss targets highest stack player |
| Frontal Attacks | **Cleave** | Various frontal cone attacks |

**Notes:** 
- Only the tank should attack and kill the adds
- Each wamoura kill applies a debuff stack to the attacker
- Boss targets the player with highest stacks - tank maintains aggro this way
- Kill new adds as the debuff wears off to maintain aggro

## Diabolos

The final boss, a powerful voidsent demon lord.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ruinous Omen | **Raidwide** | Massive group damage - open matching Diabolical Gates to avoid |
| Nightmare | **AoE** | Interruptible attack that sleeps players |
| Gravity Orb | **AoE** | Avoid placing near gates |
| Camisado | **Tankbuster** | Heavy single-target damage on tank |
| Noctoshield | **AoE** | Boss becomes shielded |
| Ultimate Terror | **Gaze** | Gaze attack - look away to avoid fear |

**Notes:** 
- For Ruinous Omen, players must open two Diabolical Gates with matching colors/symbols
- Gates must be opened before the cast finishes or the party takes heavy damage
- Interrupt Nightmare to prevent party-wide sleep
- Don't place Gravity Orbs near the gates
