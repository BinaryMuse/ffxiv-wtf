Ancient Ronkan ruins hidden within the Rak'tika Greatwood. Players explore the crumbling temple complex while confronting guardians that protect the secrets of a fallen civilization.

## Lozatl

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Stonefist | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Lozatl's Scorn | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage |
| Heat Up | **AoE** | Half of boss's body glows; move to the non-glowing side |
| Lozatl's Fury | **AoE** | Avoid the boss's glowing/fiery side |
| Ronkan Statue | **AoE** | Glowing purple statue indicates half of arena will be hit; move to opposite side |

**Notes:** Watch which side of the boss or which statue is glowing to determine safe zones. The pattern alternates between boss-based and statue-based mechanics.

## Batsquatch

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ripper Fang | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Soundwave | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage |
| Subsonics | **AoE** | Multiple circle AoE markers appear; avoid all |
| Falling Stalactites | **AoE** | Stalactites fall; with two, go opposite side. With three, stand near base and watch lean direction |

**Notes:** For the stalactite mechanic with three stalactites, observe which direction they're leaning—they will fall in that direction.

## Eros

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rend | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Glossolalia | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage |
| Hound out of Heaven | **AoE** | Tether appears; run away until tether turns purple to reduce damage |
| Inhale/Heaving Breath | **Knockback** | Boss inhales creating poison puddles, then knockback; position so you won't be knocked through puddles |
| Confession of Faith | **AoE** | Watch glowing statue heads; middle glowing = go to sides, sides glowing = go to middle |

**Notes:** During the Inhale mechanic, be mindful of poison puddle placement so the subsequent knockback doesn't push you through them.
