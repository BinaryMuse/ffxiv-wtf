A ghostly voyage through haunted waters aboard a spectral ship, where sailors lost to the sea have become restless spirits.

## Lugat

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Amorphous Applause | **Cleave** | 180-degree frontal AoE - move behind the boss |
| Hydroball | **Stack** | Stack marker on a player - group up to split damage |
| Sea Swallows All | **AoE** | Pulls all players to center, followed by Concussive Oscillation |
| Concussive Oscillation | **AoE** | Circular AoEs after the pull - run out immediately |
| Overtow | **Knockback** | Knockback on a single player, often followed by Hydroball |

**Notes:**
- After getting pulled in by Sea Swallows All, run out quickly to avoid the follow-up AoEs
- Return to the group quickly after Overtow knockback for the stack marker

## The Governor

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Shadowflow | **AoE** | Alternating cone AoEs - stand in safe zones between them |
| Bloodburst | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage requiring heals |
| Enter Night | **AoE** | Tethers a player with shadows - run away to break the tether |
| Shadowsplit | **Adds** | Boss spawns shadow adds that drop circular pools in safe zones |

**Notes:**
- Watch the shadow add movement to determine safe positioning before Shadowflow
- Run far away from the boss to break Enter Night tethers quickly
- Don't stack debuffs from failing to break tethers

## Lorelei

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Morbid Advance | **AoE** | Forces your character to walk forward - position facing safety |
| Morbid Retreat | **AoE** | Forces your character to walk backward - position with back to safety |
| Virgin Tears | **AoE** | Large AoEs dropped on the ground, often combined with Advance/Retreat |
| Somber Melody | **Raidwide** | Party-wide AoE damage |
| Ill Will | **Tankbuster** | Heavy-hitting attack on tank |

**Notes:**
- Pay attention to whether it's Advance (walk forward) or Retreat (walk backward)
- Position yourself so forced movement takes you away from AoEs
- Before this boss, interrupt/silence Fleshless Captive's Seductive Scream to avoid its Flood combo
