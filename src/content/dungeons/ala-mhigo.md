The climactic assault on the occupied city of Ala Mhigo, culminating in a confrontation with the Garlean Viceroy himself.

## Magitek Scorpion

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Electromagnetic Field | **Raidwide** | Party-wide unavoidable damage requiring heals |
| Target Search | **AoE** | Large circular AoEs follow players, then lock in place and explode |
| Tail Laser | **AoE** | Line AoEs in front and behind boss, dividing the arena |

**Notes:**
- Move out of Target Search indicators once they stop moving
- Stand to the sides of the boss to avoid Tail Laser
- Target Search often occurs with Tail Laser, limiting available space

## Aulus mal Asina

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mana Burst | **Raidwide** | Party-wide AoE damage requiring heals |
| Order to Charge | **AoE** | Summons Magitek Bits that perform circle AoEs |
| Order to Fire | **AoE** | Summons Magitek Bits that perform donut AoEs |
| Magitek Disruptor | **AoE** | Stuns all players and pulls them toward the boss |
| Mindjack | **AoE** | Spirit separates from body - reach your body within 30 seconds |

**Notes:**
- During Mindjack, you move slowly while being chased by a hand
- You cannot take damage during Mindjack but must return to your body in time
- Watch for increasing complexity of Order to Charge/Fire patterns

## Zenos yae Galvus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Art of the Storm | **AoE** | AoE around Zenos (blue aura) - stand at arena edge |
| Art of the Swell | **Knockback** | AoE at arena edge (green aura) with knockback - stand near boss |
| Art of the Sword | **Spread** | Line attack to all players - spread out |
| Veinsplitter | **AoE** | Circle around Zenos then circles around his clones |
| Lightless Spark | **Cleave** | Tethered player gets cone attack - position boss away from allies |
| Concentrativity | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |

**Notes:**
- Blue aura (Storm) = get out, Green aura (Swell) = get in
- During Lightless Spark, tethered player should position Zenos away from group
- After Veinsplitter's initial circle, maintain distance from the clones
