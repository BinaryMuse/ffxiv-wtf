A fortress in Coerthas Central Highlands overrun by dragons. The Ishgardians have lost control and need adventurers to reclaim it.

## Chudo-Yudo

A dragon that guards the entrance of the vigil.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lion's Breath | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE that inflicts Burns DoT - face away from party |
| Swinge | **Cleave** | Untelegraphed frontal AoE when boss moves to the side - stay behind |
| Rake | **Tankbuster** | Medium damage attack on the tank |

**Notes:** 
- Tank should always face the boss away from the party
- Healers can use Esuna to cleanse Burns
- Watch for Swinge when the boss disengages - can occur twice in a row

## Koshchei

A large dragon fought in an open courtyard with cannons.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spiked Tail | **Tankbuster** | Casted attack that also applies Poison debuff |
| Typhoon | **AoE** | Spawns 3-4 tornadoes in two waves - find the safe gaps |
| Sonic Storm | **AoE** | Telegraphed circular AoE on a random player - move away |
| Dragon Adds | **Adds** | Dragons spawn that can be shot down with cannons |

**Notes:** 
- One DPS should use the cannons to shoot down incoming dragons
- Dragons will bombard the arena if not shot down
- Navigate between tornadoes carefully during Typhoon

## Isgebind

The final boss, a powerful frost dragon.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Frost Breath | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack - tank face away from party |
| Cauterize | **AoE** | Telegraphed line AoE covering portions of the room - avoid blue markers |
| Touchdown | **Knockback** | Knockback attack that follows Cauterize |
| Rime Wreath | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable party-wide damage |
| Sheet of Ice | **AoE** | Ice-based ground AoE |

**Notes:** 
- Watch for bright blue markers during Cauterize and move out immediately
- Position yourself to avoid being knocked into hazards after Touchdown
- Healers should be ready for Rime Wreath damage
