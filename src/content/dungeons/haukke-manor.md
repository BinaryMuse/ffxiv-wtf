A haunted manor in the Central Shroud once belonging to a noblewoman who made dark pacts for eternal beauty. Now overrun with voidsent.

## Manor Claviger

A voidsent guardian wielding a large key-like weapon.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cleave | **Cleave** | Strong melee cleave attack - dangerous to non-tanks |
| Void Fire II | **AoE** | Ranged AoE spell - move out of the radius |

**Notes:** Tank should face the boss away from the party to protect squishier members from the cleave.

## Manor Jester & Manor Steward

A duo fight against two voidsent servants.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Jester Magic | **AoE** | Single-target magical attacks from the Jester |
| Steward AoE | **Raidwide** | AoE that drains HP from all party members in range |

**Notes:** 
- Focus down one target at a time to avoid being overwhelmed
- The Steward's AoE drain affects the whole group - burn it down or move out of range

## Lady Amandine

The final boss, the manor's former mistress who sold her soul to voidsent.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Void Fire | **AoE** | Interruptible fire spell |
| Void Thunder | **AoE** | Interruptible lightning spell |
| Lamp Spawns | **Adds** | Spawns four lamps twice during the fight - major threat |
| Lady's Candle | **Adds** | Priority targets that buff the boss if left alive |

**Notes:** 
- Interrupt Void Fire and Void Thunder when possible
- Destroy lamps quickly when they spawn - they are the primary danger in this fight
- Use stuns and interrupts liberally to reduce incoming damage
