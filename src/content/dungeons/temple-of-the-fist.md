The sacred training grounds of the Fist of Rhalgr, where monks tested their skills against the deadly coeurls of the mountains.

## Coeurl Sruti and Coeurl Smriti

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Radial Blaster | **AoE** | Large AoE around the boss - move away |
| Wide Blaster | **Cleave** | Cone AoE from one boss - run behind it |
| Electric Burst | **Raidwide** | Moderate raid-wide damage requiring heals |
| Basic Instinct | **Adds** | Surviving coeurl gains massive damage buff if one dies first |

**Notes:**
- Kill both bosses at roughly the same time
- If one dies first, the survivor gains Basic Instinct significantly increasing damage
- Balance DPS between both targets

## Arbuda

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Fourfold Shear | **Tankbuster** | Multi-hit tankbuster dealing 20k+ damage - use cooldowns |
| Port and Star | **AoE** | Boss attacks left and right sides - stand front or back |
| Fore and Aft | **AoE** | Boss attacks front and back - stand to the sides |
| Hellseal | **AoE** | Players marked with sun/moon must stand on matching floor tiles |
| Tapas | **AoE** | Multiple puddle AoEs follow players - bait to arena edges |
| Killer Instinct | **AoE** | Three sides of boss are marked - attack only from unmarked side |
| Cardinal Shift | **Raidwide** | Moderate raid-wide damage |

**Notes:**
- All positioning attacks inflict Vulnerability Up debuffs if hit
- Watch for visual cues to determine attack direction
- During Hellseal, match your symbol to the correct floor tile

## Ivon Coeurlfist

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spirit Wave | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage requiring heals |
| Touch of Slaughter | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster requiring cooldowns |
| Rhalgr's Piece | **AoE** | Large AoE attacks to dodge |
| Green Markers | **AoE** | Marked players take significant damage - healers prepare |

**Notes:**
- Be prepared for multiple attacks requiring positioning
- Healers should watch for green-marked players and be ready to heal
- This spectral Monk uses various martial arts attacks
