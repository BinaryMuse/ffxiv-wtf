The third dungeon of Dawntrail, located in the depths of Yak T'el. A cenotaph for children lost to the dream of blessed siblings, now the path to the golden city.

## Feather Ray

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Immersion | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Troublesome Tail | **AoE** | Inflicts debuff that makes players mimic the boss's next ability; turn away from allies |
| Worrisome Wave | **Cleave** | Frontal conal AoE; can be mimicked by players |
| Hydro Ring | **AoE** | Donut AoE from boss that transforms arena into donut shape; stay in center |
| Blowing Bubbles | **AoE** | Bubble streams move outward from center; dodge between them |
| Bubble Bomb | **AoE** | Spawns bubbles that explode in AoEs |
| Rolling Current | **Knockback** | Knocks back bubbles on the arena |
| Trouble Bubbles | **Spread** | Combines Troublesome Tail with Blowing Bubbles; each player fires bubble streams while boss fires four; spread out |

**Notes:** The Nuisance debuff from Troublesome Tail makes you mirror the boss's attack. During Worrisome Wave, face away from allies so your mirrored cone doesn't hit them.

## Firearms

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dynamic Dominance | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage that creates dangerous zone around arena edge |
| Pummel | **Tankbuster** | Physical tank buster |
| Mirror Maneuver | **AoE** | Spawns mirrors and orbs; mirrors reflect line AoEs, orbs explode in huge AoEs if hit by lasers |
| Thunderlight Burst | **AoE** | Boss jumps to edge and shoots line AoE toward a mirror; bounces to create large circular AoE |
| Ancient Artillery | **AoE** | Expanding AoEs that hit squares in a line |
| Emergent Artillery | **Spread** | Delayed marked AoEs on all players |

**Notes:** Watch the mirrors during Thunderlight Burst—the laser bounces off them to create additional AoE zones. Avoid the orbs as they cause massive explosions if hit.

## Maulskull

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ashlayer | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Wrought Fire | **Tankbuster** | Magical tank buster that hits in an AoE; other players stay away |
| Colossal Impact | **Knockback** | Knockback from back half of room with marked AoEs or stack marker, then the opposite |
| Stonecarver | **Cleave** | Boss's fists light up twice, then cleave the marked half of arena in that order; slight knockback if hit |
| Ringing Blows | **Knockback** | Knockback from front of arena followed by Stonecarver |
| Deep Thunder | **Stack** | Tower requiring party stack; deals multiple hits of damage |
| Maulwork | **AoE** | Large circle AoEs on arena, then large line AoEs toward indicators |
| Skullcrush | **Knockback** | Knockback from front with marked AoE; get knocked back diagonally, then spread |

**Notes:** For Stonecarver, watch which of Maulskull's fists glow first and second—this indicates the order of half-room cleaves. Use knockback immunity or position carefully during Colossal Impact.
