An optional level 100 Expert dungeon in Dawntrail, set in the haunted district of Dreamborough in Living Memory. A land marred by shadow and haunted by vestiges of the Endless.

## His Royal Headness Leonogg I

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Malicious Mist | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Falling Nightmare | **AoE** | Spawns head above each player that drops AoE after delay; move away. Getting hit inflicts Benoggined (10s stun) |
| Team Spirit | **Adds** | Summons puppets at arena edges; touching them causes Bind (5s) then Benoggined (10s) |
| Spirited Charge | **AoE** | Puppets march across arena in waves; one half goes first, then the other |
| Evil Scheme | **AoE** | Circle AoEs from center that fan outward in exaflare pattern |
| Looming Nightmare | **AoE** | Two players marked with chasing AoEs that follow them |
| Scream | **AoE** | Three sets of opposing conal AoEs; stand in third, dodge into first |

**Notes:** Puppets are slightly ahead of their visual hitbox due to netcode—give them extra berth. Combined mechanics require dodging AoEs while avoiding puppet paths.

## Jack-in-the-Pot

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Last Drop | **Tankbuster** | Magical tank buster |
| Troubling Teacups | **AoE** | Summons teacups that explode in large AoE when tethered |
| Tea Awhirl | **AoE** | Tethers to teacups and spins them around arena; follow tethered cup and move away when it stops |
| Toiling Teapots | **AoE** | Teacups spawn with teapots indicating explosion order (1st, 2nd, 3rd); start at third set, move to first set once those puddles disappear |

**Notes:** For Tea Awhirl, track which teacup has the ghost tether and maintain distance. Toiling Teapots creates expanding poison—start safe and move into cleared areas.

## Träumerei

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Malicious Mist | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage that inflicts bleed DoT |
| Ghostcrusher | **Stack** | Line stack dealing magic damage |
| Bitter Regret | **AoE** | Appendages glow with large circle (line AoE down middle) or two small circles (line AoEs at sides) |
| Poltergeist | **AoE** | Plus-shaped AoE that places wall blocking living beings |
| Memorial March | **Adds** | Summons ghosts that either tether to players (stretch tether to reduce damage) or do line AoE across arena |
| Ghostduster | **Raidwide** | Instantly kills spirits; living beings are safe |
| Fleshbuster | **Raidwide** | Instantly kills living beings; spirits are safe |

**Notes:** The signature mechanic uses spirit panels that toggle Ghostly Guise buff. As a spirit, you can pass through Poltergeist walls and survive Fleshbuster. As living, survive Ghostduster. Swap forms using panels based on which instant-kill is coming.
