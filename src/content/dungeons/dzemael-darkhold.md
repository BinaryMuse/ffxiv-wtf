A cave system in Coerthas Central Highlands that House Dzemael attempted to develop into a fortress before voidsent overran it.

## All-seeing Eye

A voidsent eye that can only be damaged under special conditions.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Crystal Veil | **AoE** | Boss is only vulnerable while standing in blue crystal lights |
| Eyes on Me | **Raidwide** | Powerful point-blank AoE - reduced by 90% under Crystal Veil |
| Cursed Gaze | **Gaze** | Frontal cone inflicting Amnesia or Paralysis |
| Add Waves | **Adds** | Boss summons monsters in waves with frontal cone attacks |

**Notes:** 
- Always fight the boss under blue Crystal Veil lights
- Blue crystal lights fade over time - move to the next crystal when they do
- Stay under Crystal Veil during Eyes on Me to reduce damage by 90%
- Later add waves have dangerous frontal cones - position carefully

## Taulurd

A voidsent that requires constant movement to avoid attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Frontal Cone | **Cleave** | Frontal attack - don't stand in front |
| Elbow Drop | **Cleave** | Rear cone attack - dangerous to melee behind the boss |
| Fireball Adds | **Adds** | Four helper adds cast Fireballs and Rocks |
| Fireball | **AoE** | Large red AoE circles - must be avoided |
| Rock | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable rock attacks alternate with Fireballs |

**Notes:** 
- This is a "dance" fight requiring constant movement
- Melee must watch for rear cone attacks (Elbow Drop)
- Avoid standing in Fireball AoE circles
- Stand at the boss's sides when possible

## Batraal

A large voidsent demon that uses powerful blast attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Desolation | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack |
| Grim Halo | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE around the boss |
| Grim Fate | **AoE** | Random target blasts dealing heavy damage |
| Sea of Pitch | **AoE** | Ground-targeted AoE puddles |

**Notes:** 
- Tank should keep the boss faced away from the party
- Melee need to watch for Grim Halo and move out
- Healers should be ready for heavy damage from Grim Fate
