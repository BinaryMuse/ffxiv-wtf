The climactic finale of A Realm Reborn's main scenario. Players storm the Praetorium to confront Gaius van Baelsar and put an end to the Ultima Weapon threat.

## Mark II Magitek Colossus

A massive Magitek warmachina guarding the entrance to the Praetorium.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Grand Sword | **Tankbuster** | Heavy frontal cleave dealing 900-1300 damage - tank should use cooldowns |
| Exhaust | **Tankbuster** | Deals 600-800 damage and knocks back the tank |
| Circle AoEs | **AoE** | Red circle AoE markers - move out to avoid |

**Notes:**
- Tank should face the boss away from the party
- Healers should keep the tank topped off for Grand Sword
- Avoid standing in red circle indicators

## Nero tol Scaeva

The ambitious Tribunus Laticlavius of the XIVth Legion.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lightning Field | **AoE** | Creates an electrified zone - stay out of the lightning |
| Add Spawns | **Adds** | Summons reinforcements throughout the fight - kill adds quickly |

**Notes:**
- Tank should face the boss away from the group
- Prioritize killing adds as they spawn
- Stay mobile to avoid lightning fields

## Gaius van Baelsar

The Black Wolf himself, Legatus of the XIVth Legion and governor of Ala Mhigo.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Terminus Est | **AoE** | Draws an X in the sky before dashing forward - move to the side |
| Blue Flames | **AoE** | Places blue flame zones on the ground that apply a DoT - avoid standing in them |
| Horrida Bella | **Raidwide** | Deals damage to all party members |

**Notes:**
- Watch for the X indicator and move perpendicular to avoid the dash
- Never stand in blue flame patches as they apply a lasting damage over time effect
- This is an endurance fight - manage resources and stay alive
- Tank should keep the boss positioned away from flame patches
