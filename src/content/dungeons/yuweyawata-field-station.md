A level 100 dungeon introduced in Patch 7.1 of Dawntrail. Players investigate a secret research facility in Heritage Found, searching for clues about Gulool Ja's mother and uncovering Zoraal Ja's past experiments.

## Lindblum Zaghnal

An electrically-charged beast that uses pillars to channel lightning attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Electrical Overload | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magical damage |
| Gore | **AoE** | Boss jumps to a pillar and charges it with electricity |
| Gore 2 | **Adds** | Summons Raw Electrope adds that cast Electrify; also pulses raidwide and spawns ground AoEs with Sparking Fissure |
| Caber Toss | **AoE** | Pillar shoots sets of line AoEs in succession - dodge into safe spots |
| Lightning Storm | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on all players - spread apart |

**Notes:**
- Kill Raw Electrope adds quickly during Gore 2
- Watch for the pillar's line AoE patterns and move into safe zones
- Spread out for Lightning Storm to avoid overlapping damage

## Overseer Kanilokka

A spectral overseer that manipulates souls and perception.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dark Souls | **Tankbuster** | Magical tankbuster - use defensive cooldowns |
| Free Spirits | **AoE** | Curved line AoEs that appear quickly in succession |
| Phantom Flood | **AoE** | Donut AoE - stand in the safe circle in the middle |
| Dark II | **AoE** | Two sets of conal AoEs in succession - dodge the first then reposition |
| Telltale Tears | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on sets of players - spread apart |
| Lost Hope | **Mechanic** | Inflicts Temporary Misdirection on all players; boss does proximity AoE from center with paths to arena edge - navigate to the edge |

**Notes:**
- For Phantom Flood, immediately move to the center of the arena
- Lost Hope reverses your controls - carefully navigate to the arena edge despite misdirection
- Watch for the curved paths of Free Spirits and weave between them

## Lunipyati

A fearsome Tural Vidraal, the final boss of the dungeon.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Slabber | **Tankbuster** | Magical tankbuster - use defensive cooldowns |
| Sonic Howl | **Raidwide** | Raidwide magical damage |
| Turali Stone IV | **Stack** | Magical stack marker - group up to share damage |
| Jagged Edge | **Spread** | Marked circle AoEs on players - spread apart |
| Beastly Roar | **Proximity** | Proximity AoE from the boss that creates rotating circle AoEs traveling across the ring |
| Crater Carve | **AoE** | Large circular AoE that destroys the middle of the arena, leaving only the outer ring |
| Leporine Loaf | **Raidwide** | Deals raidwide damage and creates arena walls; spawns moving AoEs indicated by arrows |
| Raging Claw | **AoE** | Boss turns and does a large frontal AoE - avoid standing in front |

**Notes:**
- After Crater Carve, the arena becomes a ring - avoid falling into the center
- For Beastly Roar, move far from the boss and watch for rotating AoEs
- During Leporine Loaf, follow the arrow indicators to avoid moving AoEs
- Stack for Turali Stone IV, spread for Jagged Edge
