A desolate wasteland drained of aether by the Allagan Empire, now home to dangerous creatures and ancient machines.

## Hedetet

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Crystal Needle | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Hailfire | **AoE** | Target marker on player - hide behind a rock to avoid |
| Shardfall | **Raidwide** | Arena-wide attack - hide behind rocks or instant KO |
| Dissonance | **AoE** | Large donut AoE - safe spot is directly under boss |
| Shardstrike | **Spread** | Circle markers on all players - spread out, avoid rocks |

**Notes:**
- Rocks are essential for survival - don't destroy them unnecessarily
- During Shardfall, hide behind a rock then move away from its circle attack
- If all rocks are destroyed, new ones will spawn in marked locations
- During Shardstrike, avoid overlapping rocks or they'll be destroyed

## Defective Drone

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Aetherochemical Flame | **Raidwide** | Damages all players |
| Aetherochemical Residue | **AoE** | Target marker - poison puddle appears, move to edge |
| Full Throttle | **AoE** | Stand in front of sparking drone WITHOUT glowing bars |
| Aetherochemical Coil | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Rockbiters | **AoE** | Untargetable enemies travel north-south - avoid their path |
| Dreadnaught | **Adds** | Tank grabs aggro, party burns it down quickly |

**Notes:**
- During Full Throttle, look for the drone giving off sparks without glowing bars
- Standing in front of the correct drone prevents damage
- Move poison puddles to arena edges to minimize affected area

## Mist Dragon

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rime Wreath | **Raidwide** | Damages all players |
| Line Attack | **AoE** | Target marker on player, dragon fires line leaving ice puddles |
| Fog Plume | **AoE** | Star marker drops, line attacks shoot from star points |
| Mist Trap | **Adds** | Players hit by Fog Plume are trapped - attack Mist to free |
| Vaporize | **Adds** | Dragon vanishes, Draconic Regards spawn - kill before circle grows |
| Deep Fog | **AoE** | Area covered in fog, find dragon's line attack and dodge |
| Touchdown | **AoE** | Proximity marker at edge - move away |

**Notes:**
- During Fog Plume, look for the star points to predict line attack directions
- During Vaporize, don't attack the dragon or you'll be knocked back and frozen
- Kill Draconic Regards quickly to stop the growing circle marker
- During Deep Fog, run in a circle to find the dragon's location
