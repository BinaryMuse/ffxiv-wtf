Return to the sacred Dravanian mountain to face new volcanic threats and creatures.

## The Leightonward

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wild Horn | **Cleave** | Cone-shaped AoE to the front - tank face away from party |
| Inflammable Fumes | **AoE** | Creates fire puddles to avoid |
| Spore Sac | **Adds** | Spawns adds that should be grouped and killed |

**Notes:** 
- Standard tank-and-spank with frontal cleave
- Keep boss faced away from the party

## Gowrow

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ripper Claw | **Tankbuster** | High damage attack on tank |
| Fireball | **AoE** | Targeted circle AoEs on players |
| Lava Flow | **AoE** | Ground-based fire attacks |

**Notes:** 
- This lizard-like boss has minimal movement requirements
- Watch animations rather than ground telegraphs - some attacks have no markers
- Keep moving to avoid fire attacks

## Lava Scorpion

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Deadly Thrust | **Tankbuster** | Requires tank cooldowns |
| Lava Pools | **AoE** | Boss periodically drops lava on the floor - avoid standing in it |
| Add Phase | **Adds** | Summons adds during the fight |
| Molten Silk | **AoE** | Web-like ground AoEs |

**Notes:** 
- The most challenging encounter in the dungeon
- Avoid lava pools that accumulate on the floor
- Between bosses, watch for lava worms that split into two when defeated
- Before this boss, prioritize Blood of the Mountain enemies - their Torch cast deals high raid-wide damage
