Return to the pirate-infested isle to face new threats from the Maelstrom's enemies.

## Sanguine Sirens

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wave Combat | **Adds** | Series of waves with different enemies, overseen by Captain Rhoswen |
| Siren Adds | **Adds** | Various enemy waves to defeat |

**Notes:** 
- Focus on clearing each wave efficiently
- This is more of a gauntlet than a traditional boss

## Ymir

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Blanket Thunder | **AoE** | Lightning AoE attack |
| Megavolt | **Raidwide** | Room-wide lightning damage |

**Notes:**
- Snail-type creature with lightning-based mechanics
- Avoid lightning AoE zones

## Grand Storm Marshal Slafyrsyn

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Circles of Light | **AoE** | Buff enemy attack and cast time - move enemies out of these circles |
| Storm Commander Rhiki | **Adds** | Mini-boss before final boss that inflicts slash vulnerability on tank |
| Heavy Swing | **Tankbuster** | High damage tank attack |
| Arena Hazards | **AoE** | Various AoE attacks to avoid |

**Notes:** 
- Before this boss, defeat Storm Commander Rhiki who debuffs the tank
- Move enemies out of light circles to reduce incoming damage
- Healers need to watch for increased tank damage from vulnerability debuffs
