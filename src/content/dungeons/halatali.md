A training facility in Eastern Thanalan used to breed and train battle creatures. The beasts have grown out of control and must be subdued.

## Firemane

A fiery horse creature near a central fire pit.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Fire | **AoE** | Interruptible cast - use Interject or Head Graze |
| Damantus/Noxius Adds | **Adds** | Adds drift toward the fire pit and explode for raidwide damage |

**Notes:** 
- Noxius adds deal approximately 3x more damage than Damantus when they explode
- Either kill adds before they reach the pit, or burn the boss quickly
- Fire cast can be interrupted with crowd control abilities

## Thunderclap Guivre

A drake that fights in an arena with a central electrified pond.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cone AoE | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack - keep boss facing away |
| Arena Electrocution | **Raidwide** | At 80% and 40%, boss becomes invulnerable and electrifies the floor |
| Lightning Sprites | **Adds** | Spawn during invulnerable phases - kill to end the phase |

**Notes:** 
- Tank the boss on the ramp at the arena edge to avoid electrocution damage entirely
- Kill Lightning Sprites quickly to end the invulnerable phases

## Tangata

The final boss, a large creature that uses fire-based attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Double Smash | **Cleave** | Heavy frontal cone attack |
| Firewater | **AoE** | Circular fire AoE puddles on the ground |
| Fire Shield Phase | **Adds** | Boss surrounds itself with fire and becomes immune, spawning adds |
| Knockback | **Knockback** | Pushes players away from the boss |

**Notes:** 
- Kill Damantus, Noxius, and Fire Sprite adds to break the boss's immunity
- Keep the boss centered to give DPS time to kill adds
- Tank should face boss away from party due to Double Smash
