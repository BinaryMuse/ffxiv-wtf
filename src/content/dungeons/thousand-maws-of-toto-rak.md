An ancient Gelmorran prison in the South Shroud, now overrun with dangerous creatures and toxic flora.

## Coeurl O'Nine Tails

This mini-boss is fought twice throughout the dungeon.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Poison Mist | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE that applies poison - move away from boss |
| Acid Mist | **AoE** | Same as Poison Mist - avoid standing near boss |
| Warden's Whip Adds | **Adds** | Second encounter spawns adds that cast poison and slow |

**Notes:** 
- First encounter is a simple tank-and-spank
- Second encounter spawns 2 Warden's Whip adds - burn them down quickly
- Keep adds away from healer to prevent slowed cast times

## Graffias

A large scorpion-like creature in a poison-filled arena.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Realm Shaker | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable party-wide damage |
| Silk Screen | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE with knockback |
| Sticky Web | **AoE** | Targets a player and spawns a Fleshy Pod that explodes |
| Deadly Thrust | **AoE** | Tail drops large poison puddles across the arena |
| Add Phases | **Adds** | Spawns adds at 70% and 30% health |

**Notes:** 
- Green sludge on the arena floor applies Leaden (slow) debuff
- At 50% health, the boss's tail becomes targetable - DPS must destroy it immediately
- Destroying the tail stuns the boss, applies vulnerability, and stops Deadly Thrust
- Move away from Sticky Web pods before they detonate
