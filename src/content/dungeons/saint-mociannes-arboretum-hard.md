A return to the overgrown Arboretum, now infested with slimes and corrupted plant life requiring a thorough cleaning.

## Nullchu

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Odious Air | **Cleave** | Wide frontal cone AoE - move behind boss |
| Vine Whip | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Rafflesia Add | **AoE** | Untargetable add spawns poison puddles under two players |
| Fault Warren | **Stack** | Stack marker on random player |
| Devour | **AoE** | Boss leaps to Rafflesia, then 180-degree AoE - stay near Rafflesia |
| Taproot | **Spread** | AoE circles on all players - spread out |

**Notes:**
- Stay close to the Rafflesia add when it spawns
- Boss leaps to Rafflesia then does massive frontal AoE
- Being near the Rafflesia gives you more time to escape the Devour AoE
- Poison puddles inflict Leaden debuff

## Lakhamu

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Stone II | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Tectonics | **Raidwide** | Damages all party members |
| Landslip | **AoE** | Sand shifts in arrow direction while Silt Golems charge line AoEs |
| Earthquake | **AoE** | Point-blank then ring AoE - stay at edge of first, move in after |
| Earth Shaker | **Spread** | Two players targeted with line AoEs - spread out |
| Empty Gaze | **Gaze** | Gaze attack inflicting Hysteria - turn away from boss |

**Notes:**
- Arena edge has thorns causing Bleed and Vulnerability Up - avoid it
- During Landslip, position so sand pushes you away from Golem AoEs but not into edge
- For Earthquake, stay at edge of point-blank AoE, then move inside after first hit
- Turn away during Empty Gaze to avoid uncontrollable running

## Tokkapchi

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mudsling | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Quickmire | **AoE** | Muddy water shoots up - move to circular platforms to avoid |
| Quagmire | **AoE** | Circle AoEs on players leave poison puddles - stay away from platforms |
| Mud Pie | **Adds** | Mud Slime spawns - knock it around to prevent it growing |
| Corrupture | **AoE** | Slime explodes - damage scales with slime size |
| Feculent Flood | **Cleave** | Large cone at slime - knock slime out of AoE |
| Bog Bequest | **AoE** | Ring-shaped AoE - avoid and knock slime out |
| Muddy Dorpokkur | **Adds** | Weak adds spawn as distractions |

**Notes:**
- Arena is grate over muddy water with four safe circular platforms
- Attacking the Mud Slime knocks it back - use this to move it
- If slime is hit by any AoE or Quickmire, it grows and Corrupture becomes deadly
- Coordinate with party to keep knocking slime out of danger zones
- Stand away from safe platforms during Quagmire to not poison them
