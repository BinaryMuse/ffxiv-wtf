A dungeon exploring an underwater cavern system near Thavnair. Players venture into the Aetherfont to confront sea creatures and electrical beasts that have made this place their home.

## Lyngbakr

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sonic Bloop | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Upsweep | **Raidwide** | AoE damage to all players |
| Tidal Breath | **Cleave** | Attack marker on entire frontal area; move behind boss |
| Body Slam | **AoE** | Creates ice spikes throughout arena; Upsweep causes spikes to emit circle AoEs |
| Floodstide | **Spread** | Circle attack markers follow all players; spread apart |
| Tidalspout | **Stack** | Stack marker on one player; unmarked players group up |

**Notes:** After Body Slam, ice spikes remain on the field. When Upsweep is cast, these spikes create additional circle AoEs—maintain distance from spikes during Upsweep.

## Arkas

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ripper Claw | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Battle Cry | **Raidwide** | AoE damage to all players |
| Lightning Leap | **AoE** | Large circle AoE marker; forking lightning lines emerge from impact zone |
| Spinning Claw | **AoE** | Large circle AoE centered on boss, followed by straight lightning lines (wider than visual) |
| Lightning Rampage | **AoE** | Multiple circle attacks with lightning lines; similar to Lightning Leap but more |
| Lightning Claw | **Stack** | Stack marker; group up, then move away from resulting lightning circle |
| Electric Eruption | **AoE** | Places three lightning circles in arena |

**Notes:** Lightning attacks are wider than their visual indicators suggest. Give extra space when dodging. After Lightning Claw stack, move away from the resulting electrical field.

## Octomammoth

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tidal Roar | **Raidwide** | AoE damage to all players |
| Octostroke | **Cleave** | Tentacles appear in random locations and perform cone attacks |
| Saline Spit | **AoE** | Targeted circle AoEs on players |
| Telekinesis | **AoE** | Moves objects that create AoE hazards |
| Wallop | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Clearout | **Knockback** | Knockback from boss; position to avoid hazards |
| Breathstroke | **Cleave** | Wide frontal cone breath attack |

**Notes:** Watch for tentacle positions during Octostroke—they can appear anywhere and have varied cone directions. Position to avoid being knocked into hazards during Clearout.
