An overgrown Sharlayan greenhouse in the Dravanian Hinterlands, now inhabited by dangerous plant life.

## Rose Garden

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spinning Breath | **AoE** | Rotating breath attacks that spin clockwise or counterclockwise - move with the safe zone |
| Untargetable Adds | **Adds** | Summons adds with small breath attacks |
| Rose Hip | **Adds** | Balloon adds that must be killed quickly before they explode for raid-wide damage |

**Notes:** 
- Watch the direction of the spinning breath and move accordingly
- Prioritize Rose Hip adds to prevent raid-wide explosion damage

## Queen Hawk

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Column AoEs | **AoE** | Bees randomly target players with column attacks from the room edges |
| Ally | **AoE** | Bees gather in two groups and launch huge double column AoEs with no ground markers |
| Knight Hawk | **Adds** | Bee adds that tether to the boss - DPS check where raid-wide damage increases each round |

**Notes:** 
- Kill Knight Hawk adds quickly to prevent stacking raid-wide damage
- When you see bees gathering, prepare to dodge the unmarked column attacks

## Belladonna

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Point-Blank AoE | **AoE** | Large AoE around the boss - stand close to avoid |
| Bulb Circles | **AoE** | Yellow circles from Bulbs apply stackable magic vulnerability debuff |
| Frond Fatale | **Gaze** | Look away to avoid being charmed into the Bulbs' AoEs |
| Add Spawns | **Adds** | Kill targetable adds when they spawn |

**Notes:** 
- The gaze attack charms you into walking into Bulb AoEs - always look away
- Avoid yellow circles to prevent stacking vulnerability
