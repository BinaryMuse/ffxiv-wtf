A pivotal story dungeon featuring battles against the Heavens' Ward knights in Ishgard's holiest sanctum.

## Ser Adelphel Brightblade

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Holiest of Holy | **Raidwide** | Room-wide damage requiring AoE heals |
| Holy Shield Bash | **Knockback** | Targets a random player with a stun and knockback combo |
| Brightspheres | **AoE** | Summons white orbs that explode in the order they were summoned |
| Shining Blade | **AoE** | Adelphel charges around summoning orbs - avoid the explosions |
| Execution | **AoE** | Circle AoE that follows a targeted player - move away from the party |

**Notes:** 
- At around 30% health, Adelphel transforms and becomes more dangerous
- Track the order of orb spawns to avoid explosions

## Ser Grinnaux the Bull

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hyperdimensional Slash | **AoE** | Creates red line AoEs in random directions, leaving Dimensional Torsion rifts |
| Dimensional Torsion | **AoE** | Tether to and damage nearby players - stay away from them |
| Faith Unmoving | **Knockback** | Room-wide attack with knockback - keep your back away from Dimensional Torsion rifts |
| Dimensional Rift | **AoE** | Circle AoE to dodge |

**Notes:** 
- Avoid being knocked into Dimensional Torsion rifts by positioning carefully
- At low health, Grinnaux transforms

## Ser Charibert

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Altar Pyre | **Raidwide** | Room-wide damage |
| Holy Chain | **Stack** | Chains two players together - run apart to break the chain |
| Heavensflame | **AoE** | Places flame rings on the ground - avoid standing in them |
| Summoned Knights | **Adds** | Slow-inflicting enemies that march across the room - avoid them |
| Dawn Knight & Dusk Knight | **Adds** | Spawns at half health - kill them while handling other mechanics |

**Notes:** 
- This dungeon has significant unavoidable damage - healer gear is important
- Break Holy Chain quickly by running away from your partner
- Watch for knights marching across the arena
