A dungeon set in the ancient Elpis facility known as Ktisis Hyperboreia, where the Ancients once created and studied life. Players explore this research complex in the distant past, encountering creatures born of creation magic.

## Lyssa

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Skull Dasher | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Frigid Stomp | **Raidwide** | Raidwide damage that triggers icicles to shoot line AoEs |
| Heavy Smash | **Stack** | Stack marker; group up to split damage |
| Frostbite and Seek | **AoE** | Boss disappears into one of six snow piles (indicated by footprints); move to footprint destination to avoid center AoE |
| Icicall | **AoE** | Summons three ice crystals that shoot line AoEs in triangle pattern; stay in arena center |

**Notes:** During Frostbite and Seek, watch for the footprints indicating where Lyssa will reappear. The center of the arena will be hit with a large AoE, so move toward where the footprints lead.

## Ladon Lord

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Scratch | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Intimidation | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Pyric Blast | **Stack** | Stack marker; group up to split damage |
| Inhale | **AoE** | One of three heads lights with fire; indicates direction of following Pyric Breath |
| Pyric Breath | **Cleave** | 120-degree cone in direction of lit head (left/center/right) |
| Pyric Spheres | **AoE** | Summons fireballs that shoot line AoEs in cross pattern |

**Notes:** Watch which head lights up during Inhale. If the left head glows, the left side will be cleaved. Center head means frontal cleave. Right head means right side cleave.

## Hermes

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Trismegistos | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| True Tornado | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Hermetica | **AoE** | Boss tethers to brands on arena edges; they shoot line AoEs across arena. Getting hit knocks into wall of gales |
| True Bravery | **AoE** | Summons adds that must be defeated |
| Double | **AoE** | Creates clone that mirrors boss abilities |
| True Aero | **AoE** | Wind-based AoEs around the arena |

**Notes:** A ring of gales surrounds the arena—getting knocked into it deals heavy damage. Watch the tethered brands during Hermetica to identify where the line AoEs will fire.
