A goblin cheese-making facility in Eastern La Noscea that has been invaded by dragons. Help Brayflox defend their home.

## Great Yellow Pelican

A large bird accompanied by poisonous adds.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Numbing Breath | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE that applies paralysis |
| Violet Back Adds | **Adds** | Spawn with the boss, cast poison and paralysis |

**Notes:** 
- Tank should face the boss away from the party
- Kill Violet Back adds quickly to reduce incoming debuffs
- More adds spawn periodically throughout the fight

## Inferno Drake

A fire-breathing drake that attacks with cleaves.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Burning Cyclone | **Cleave** | Frontal cleave attack - face boss away from party |
| Tempest Biast | **Adds** | Add spawns and chases Brayflox around the arena |
| Blaze Spikes | **Raidwide** | After killing the add, boss gains a fire damage reflect buff |

**Notes:** 
- Keep the boss facing away from the party at all times
- Kill the Tempest Biast add when it spawns
- Blaze Spikes damage is negligible and can be healed through

## Hellbender

A large toad-like creature that traps players in bubbles.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Stagnant Spray | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE |
| Queer Bubble | **Adds** | Traps a player inside a bubble add - destroy it immediately |
| Eerie Light | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE - move out of melee range |

**Notes:** 
- Free trapped players from Queer Bubble immediately
- When Hellbender reaches low health, Aiatar drops in and kills it, starting the next fight

## Aiatar

The final boss, a large dragon that uses poison attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dragon Breath | **Cleave** | Frontal column AoE with short cast time |
| Toxic Vomit | **AoE** | Creates poison puddles that heal the boss if it stands in them |

**Notes:** 
- Tank must keep the boss out of poison puddles at all costs
- Puddles heal Aiatar significantly if the boss stands in them
- Short cast time on Dragon Breath requires quick reactions
