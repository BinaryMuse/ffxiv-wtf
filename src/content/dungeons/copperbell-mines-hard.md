The mines have been further infested with hecatoncheires and other dangers. A harder version of the original dungeon with completely new bosses and mechanics.

## Hecatoncheir Mastermind

A commander of the hecatoncheires fought on a narrow bridge.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mining Dross | **Adds** | Tank should pick up and AoE these down immediately |
| Bridge Ignition | **AoE** | Circle AoEs ignite bridge sections, leaving persistent fire |
| Bomb Spawns | **Adds** | Bombs spawn after ignition - kill immediately before Self-Destruct |
| Hecatoncheir Adds | **Adds** | Additional hecatoncheires spawn at certain HP thresholds |

**Notes:** 
- Kill bombs immediately - their Self-Destruct is devastating
- Tank picks up Mining Dross adds while DPS handles bombs
- After second ignition, two additional hecatoncheires spawn
- Bombs are the priority mechanic - focus them above all else

## Gogmagolem

A large golem that cannot be damaged directly - must use bombs.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Waymaker Bombs | **Adds** | Use lever to spawn bombs, carry them to the boss to deal damage |
| Rockslide | **AoE** | Targets tank only - tank should stand on cart tracks |
| Kindling Sprites | **Adds** | Random spawns that DPS must kill |
| Blasting Caps | **Adds** | Spawn in groups of three - avoid their Self-Destruct |
| Plaincracker | **AoE** | At 30% HP, leaves blue persistent damage circle |
| Biggy | **Adds** | Large Spriggan spawns after Gogmagolem dies |

**Notes:** 
- A geared melee DPS should carry bombs to the golem while it wanders
- Tank stays on cart tracks to keep Rockslide away from party
- After killing Gogmagolem, fight Biggy and avoid Shattering Caps

## Ouranos

The final boss with a unique crystal-feeding mechanic.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Abyss Worm | **Adds** | Summoned at start - must be fed crystals when it searches for fodder |
| Crystal Feeding | **AoE** | DPS picks up crystal and drops it in sand when notified |
| Power Attack | **AoE** | Inflicts movement speed debuff - healer must cleanse crystal feeder |
| Hecatoncheir Adds | **Adds** | Spawn during fight - other DPS kills them |

**Notes:** 
- Only feed crystals to the worm when notified - overfeeding causes a wipe
- Crystals don't respawn - burn Ouranos before running out
- Healer must immediately remove slow debuff from the crystal feeder
- Tank should position Ouranos away from next crystal to avoid knockback issues
