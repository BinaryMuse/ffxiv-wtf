The final dungeon of Shadowbringers, set in the Amalj'aa homeland under siege by the Telophoroi. Players fight through Garlean forces and tempered beasts to confront a manifestation of Bahamut himself.

## Amhuluk

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Critical Rip | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Electric Burst | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage; hits harder with Lightning Resistance Down |
| Lightning Bolt | **AoE** | All players get lightning circles; move to unelectrified rods at arena edge to transfer debuff |
| Thundercall | **Adds** | Pulsing lightning orbs spawn; find safe spots between pulses |
| Wide Blaster | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack |
| Spike Flail | **Cleave** | Rear cone attack; move to boss's sides to avoid front/back combo |

**Notes:** Transfer lightning to rods during Lightning Bolt or take increased damage from Electric Burst. Wide Blaster and Spike Flail often combo—stay at sides.

## Magitek Core

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Defensive Reaction | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage throughout the fight |
| Wave Cannon | **AoE** | Two of three wall cannons glow; stand in non-glowing column |
| Analysis | **AoE** | Various Magitek attacks from surrounding machinery |
| Core Exposure | **AoE** | Glowing platform appears; all players stand on it to launch up and attack core |

**Notes:** This is a multi-phase encounter with adds and cannon attacks. When the glowing platform appears, everyone must stand on it to damage the core.

## Lunar Bahamut

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Twisted Scream | **Raidwide** | Party-wide AoE damage |
| Lunar Nails | **AoE** | Purple spikes drop in sequence and explode in that order; move to safe zones after explosions |
| Akh Morn | **Stack** | Multi-hit stack marker; stay grouped so all hits land on tank |
| Megaflare Dive | **AoE** | Boss dives through arena; move to recent explosion zones or newer spike locations |
| Gigaflare | **Raidwide** | Heavy party-wide damage; healers prepare |
| Flatten | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |

**Notes:** Track Lunar Nail drop order—they explode in sequence. After explosions, those spots become safe for Megaflare Dive. Akh Morn requires tight stacking on tank.
