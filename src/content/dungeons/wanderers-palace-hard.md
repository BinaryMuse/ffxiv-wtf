The mamool ja have taken over the palace. Face their champions in this challenging hard mode dungeon.

## Frumious Koheel Ja

A mamool ja champion that uses burning spears.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sacred Spears | **Adds** | Destroy quickly - deal repeated damage and apply Burns |
| Fire Angon | **Tankbuster** | Fire attack on the tank |
| Clobber | **Knockback** | Charges and knocks back players |

**Notes:** 
- Sacred Spears are the priority - they deal constant damage to the arena
- Destroy spears quickly to reduce incoming damage
- Healers must monitor party health throughout the fight

## Slithy Zolool Ja

A mamool ja that uses colored totems with different effects.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Red Standard | **AoE** | Inflicts Fire Resistance Down - avoid |
| Blue Standard | **AoE** | Provides Damage Up buff - DPS should grab this |
| Green Standard | **AoE** | Transforms to Toad or cleanses the debuff |
| Purple Standard | **AoE** | Inflicts Heavy debuff - avoid |
| Standard Retrieval | **Raidwide** | Clears all standards - take Blue before this |
| Toad | **AoE** | Random transformation into a toad |

**Notes:** 
- DPS should always grab Blue Standard for damage buff
- If Blue Standard remains during Standard Retrieval, boss gains the buff instead
- Avoid Red and Purple standards
- Green Standard can cleanse Toad transformation

## Manxome Molaa Ja Ja

The final champion with add and doom mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Red Circles | **AoE** | Don't let boss stand in these |
| Sacred Idols | **Adds** | Kill when they tether to the boss |
| Doom | **Gaze** | Look away or heal to full HP to cleanse |
| Cone AoEs | **Cleave** | Stay close to boss to dodge more easily |

**Notes:** 
- Keep the boss out of red circles at all costs
- Destroy Sacred Idols when they appear and tether to the boss
- For Doom, look away from the boss OR healers can cleanse by healing to full HP
- Staying close to the boss makes dodging cone AoEs easier
