An icy cavern in Coerthas Central Highlands where Lady Iceheart has taken refuge. Features mechanics involving ice and freezing.

## Wandil

An ice elemental that applies stacking freeze debuffs.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ice Guillotine | **Cleave** | No-cast cleave on tank - keep boss facing away |
| Snowdrift | **Raidwide** | Moderate damage + 1 stack of Frozen debuff |
| Cold Wave | **AoE** | Multiple puddle AoEs around the arena - avoid them |
| Frost Bomb | **Adds** | Kill quickly before explosion deals damage + Frozen stacks |
| Tundra | **AoE** | Blue ring at arena edge applies Frozen stacks |

**Notes:** 
- At 4 Frozen stacks, your character freezes in place
- Half way through, boss places permanent ice ring shrinking the arena
- Kill Frost Bomb adds immediately
- Healers should monitor party Frozen stacks

## Yeti

A beast with unusually high defense - must use snowballs to damage.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Buffet | **Cleave** | Small cone AoE in front of boss |
| Spriggan Ice Cutters | **Adds** | Don't kill - turn into snowballs with Frozen Mist |
| Frozen Mist | **Cleave** | Tank faces boss toward Spriggans to create snowballs |
| Northerlies | **Raidwide** | High raid damage - interrupt with snowball hits |
| Frostbound | **Adds** | Kill immediately - they try to destroy snowballs |

**Notes:** 
- Tank must face boss toward Spriggan adds so Frozen Mist turns them into snowballs
- Attack snowballs while facing the boss to knock them into Yeti
- Snowballs deal high damage and can interrupt Northerlies
- Do NOT kill Spriggan Ice Cutters - they become snowballs
- Kill Frostbound adds that try to destroy snowballs

## Fenrir

The final boss, a legendary ice wolf serving Lady Iceheart.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ecliptic Bite | **Cleave** | No-cast cleave on tank - face away from party |
| Heavensward Howl | **Cleave** | No-cast cleave on tank - face away from party |
| Thousand-year Storm | **Raidwide** | Moderate raid-wide damage |
| Icicles | **AoE** | 3 icicles drop - 2 target players with columns, 1 has point-blank AoE |
| Lunar Cry | **Raidwide** | Heavy damage - hide behind 3rd icicle to avoid |

**Notes:** 
- Tank should keep boss faced away at all times (multiple no-cast cleaves)
- Lunar Cry only occurs every OTHER time icicles drop
- When icicles drop with Lunar Cry, hide behind the 3rd icicle
- Move away from icicle after Lunar Cry to avoid point-blank AoE
- Healers should be ready for both Lunar Cry and Thousand-year Storm
