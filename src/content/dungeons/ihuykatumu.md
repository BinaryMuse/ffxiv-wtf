The first dungeon of Dawntrail, set along the mighty Ihuykatumu river in Kozama'uka. Players traverse the waterway on a barge pulled by a giant punutiy while fighting fierce wildlife.

## Prime Punutiy

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Punutiy Press | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage; healers prepare |
| Shore Shaker | **AoE** | Ring AoEs that expand outward from the boss |
| Song of the Punutiy | **Adds** | Summons untargetable adds that tether to non-tanks with circle AoEs; tank gets a conal tether from boss |
| Resurface | **AoE** | Boss moves to edge and sucks in a cone, then spits objects creating line/circle/cone AoEs; ends with donut AoE |
| Hydrowave | **AoE** | Conal AoE targeting a random player |

**Notes:** During Resurface, watch the boss's position and the objects being spat out to find safe zones. The donut AoE at the end requires staying close to the boss.

## Drowsie

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Uppercut | **Tankbuster** | Physical damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Spit | **Adds** | Summons Mimiclot adds that must be tanked and killed; random players marked with circle AoEs |
| Sneeze | **Cleave** | Wide conal AoE from boss |
| Sow | **AoE** | Plants seeds on the arena |
| Drowsy Dance | **AoE** | Seeds erupt into vines with directional line AoEs (Wallop); continued dancing increases AoE size and damage |

**Notes:** Pay attention to the vine directional indicators during Drowsy Dance. As the boss continues dancing, the line AoEs grow larger, so resolve them quickly.

## Apex Predator Apollyon

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| High Wind | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage; boss consumes adds to enhance abilities |
| Blade | **Tankbuster** | Physical tank buster; after consuming sandworms, becomes AoE with wind DoT and leaves donut AoE |
| Windwhistle | **AoE** | Summons tornado that travels across arena shooting line AoEs; standing inside causes knockback and vulnerability |
| Razor Storm | **Cleave** | Boss jumps to edge and does large frontal AoE |
| Thunder III | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on all players; spread to avoid overlap |
| Levinsickle | **AoE** | Ground AoEs create lightning pools that do two sets of conal AoEs |
| Blades of Famine | **AoE** | Large line AoEs from arena edge where boss jumped |
| Swarming Locust | **AoE** | Boss jumps to arena edge three times in succession |
| Razor Zephyr | **AoE** | Line AoE toward a random player |

**Notes:** The tornado from Windwhistle is the main threat—avoid standing in its path. After High Wind, the boss's abilities become enhanced, so be prepared for more damage.
