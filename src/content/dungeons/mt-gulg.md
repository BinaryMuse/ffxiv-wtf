The final leveling dungeon of Shadowbringers, ascending the mountain fortress of Vauthry. Players climb through waves of sin eaters to reach the Lightwarden at the summit.

## Forgiven Cruelty

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rake | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Cyclone Wing | **Raidwide** | Damages all players; healers prepare |
| Lumen Infinitum | **AoE** | Unmarked line AoE at random player; don't stand in front of boss |
| Typhoon Wing | **AoE** | Triangle pattern with cone attacks from each point; stand along triangle edges |
| Typhoon Wing + Orbs | **AoE** | Combines cone attacks with circle AoEs from orbs at arena edge plus boss point-blank |

**Notes:** Later Typhoon Wing casts add orbs and a point-blank—position between cone attacks while avoiding both orbs and the boss.

## Forgiven Whimsy

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Catechism | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Sacrament of Penance | **Raidwide** | Damages all players; healers prepare |
| Exagenesis | **AoE** | Floor segments matching boss's face segments explode; stand on faceless segments |
| Judgement Day | **AoE** | Circle markers appear; at least one player must stand in each or party takes damage + vuln |
| Exagenesis + Orbs | **AoE** | Combines face explosions with donut AoEs from orbs |

**Notes:** Boss is stationary—positional attacks have no bonus. Watch the boss's rotating faces to predict which floor segments are safe.

## Forgiven Obscenity

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sacrament Sforzando | **Tankbuster** | Line attack on tank; face boss away from party |
| Orison Fortissimo | **Raidwide** | Damages all players; healers prepare |
| Divine Diminuendo | **AoE** | Point-blank circle, later gains Ring of Radiance donuts; stand at edge of inner circle |
| Conviction Marcato | **Cleave** | Line attack with four unmarked lines from circles; stand where circles meet |
| Penance Pianissimo | **AoE** | Damaging ring around arena edges; stay in center safe circle |
| Feather Marionette | **AoE** | Four copies spawn in corners; find one with glowing rings for safe zone |
| Solitaire Ring | **AoE** | Line of orbs with rings at different distances; rings fire lines based on distance from orbs |

**Notes:** During Solitaire Ring, stand in front of far rings first, then move to areas where line attacks already occurred.
