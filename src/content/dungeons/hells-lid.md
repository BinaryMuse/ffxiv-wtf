A volcanic island in the Ruby Sea where the auspice Genbu resides, guarding the sacred Reisen Temple from intruders.

## Otake-maru

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 100-tonze Swing | **Knockback** | Point-blank AoE when boss raises arm - run out of melee range |
| 10-tonze Slash | **Cleave** | Frontal cone targeting random player - dodge the telegraph |
| Liquid Carapace | **AoE** | Boss chases a player while vomiting lava - keep moving away |
| Otake-maru's Weapons | **AoE** | Boss leaps to reclaim weapons, causing AoE on each landing |
| Volcanic Debris | **AoE** | Random circular AoEs from falling boulders |

**Notes:**
- All attacks inflict stacking Vulnerability Up debuffs
- When boss raises its arm, run out of melee range immediately
- The targeted player during Liquid Carapace must constantly move away
- After reclaiming both weapons, boss does a point-blank AoE

## Kamaitachi

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Whipping Whittret | **Cleave** | No cast bar high damage frontal - keep boss facing away |
| 'The wind begins to howl!' | **AoE** | Donut AoE - safe at max or melee range |
| The Patient Blade | **AoE** | 180-degree area AoE - stay close to move out quickly |
| 'The wind shifts!' | **AoE** | Column AoE bisecting arena - stand to either side |
| Tsumuji-kaze | **Adds** | Adds spawn with circular AoEs - tank picks up, DPS burns |
| Tornado Dash | **AoE** | Tornadoes tether and boss dashes along lines - stay far away |
| Reaper's Gale | **AoE** | Proximity AoE when boss lands on final tornado |

**Notes:**
- Pay attention to text prompts on screen for donut vs column AoE
- The tornado dash is wider than it looks - stay very far away from orange tethers
- Run away from the remaining tornado for proximity damage reduction

## Genbu

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Caduceus | **Tankbuster** | No cast bar, heavy tank damage - healers watch closely |
| Hell of Water | **AoE** | Cross-shaped AoE centered on boss |
| Hell of Waste | **Spread** | Orange marker on player, then circular AoE - spread out |
| Sinister Tide | **AoE** | Blue ball follows arrow tiles - observe path and move |
| Hell of Waves | **Knockback** | Raid-wide knockback |
| Chelonian Gate | **Adds** | Boss summons shield - destroy before Duty Gauge fills or wipe |

**Notes:**
- During Sinister Tide, watch the arrows on the floor to predict ball path
- The blue ball follows arrows until it hits a wall
- During Chelonian Gate, destroy the Gate add before the Duty Gauge reaches 100%
- Failing to break the shield results in a party wipe
