A castle in Kugane where the Confederacy's enemies have taken refuge, culminating in a battle against the legendary swordsman.

## Zuiko-maru

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clearout | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE - move out of the telegraph |
| Harakiri Kosho | **Adds** | Bomb adds spawn and tether to players - run away from your tethered add |
| Kenki Release | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage |
| Helm Crack | **Stack** | Stack marker - all players stack on marked player |

**Notes:**
- Run away from your tethered bomb add
- DPS should focus on the add tethered to another player
- Keep adds together and destroy them to trigger their safe AoE

## Dojun-maru

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clockwork Medium | **Adds** | Summons Joi Onmitsu adds that explode for heavy raid-wide if not killed |
| Clockwork Raiton | **Spread** | Lightning circular AoE around individual players - spread out |
| Tatami-gaeshi | **AoE** | Column AoEs from adds that knock players into the air |

**Notes:**
- Kill adds quickly before they explode
- Spread out during Clockwork Raiton to avoid overlapping
- Watch for column AoEs from the adds

## Yojimbo

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Payday | **AoE** | Gold piles spawn - collect them before the dog does |
| Zanmato | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE - damage based on how much gold the dog collected |
| Metta-giri | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage |
| Dragon's Lair | **Adds** | Spawns low-health Dragon Head adds to kill |
| Dog Knockback | **Knockback** | Unavoidable knockback from the dog |

**Notes:**
- The dog fills a Duty Gauge as it collects gold
- Higher gauge means more damage from Zanmato
- Collect gold piles to minimize Zanmato damage
- Dragon Head adds during Dragon's Lair have low health
