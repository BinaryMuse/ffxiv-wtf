An ancient burial site in the Black Shroud that has been corrupted by the Lambs of Dalamud cult. Players must stop their dark rituals in the depths.

## Void Soulcounter

Mini-bosses that spawn after destroying cultist orbs throughout the dungeon.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Enthunder | **AoE** | Self-buff adding lightning damage - can be interrupted |

**Notes:** These are simple tank-and-spank encounters. Let the tank establish aggro before DPSing.

## Galvanth the Dominator

The final boss is a demon lord summoned by the cultists.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mind Blast | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE - move away from boss |
| Add Phases | **Adds** | Spawns adds at 75%, 50%, and 25% health - boss becomes immune |
| Inconspicuous Imp | **Adds** | Priority kill target - destroying it removes boss immunity |

**Notes:** 
- Focus Inconspicuous Imps first to break the boss's immunity
- At 75%: 1 Imp spawns
- At 50%: 1 Imp, 2 Skeleton Soldiers, 1 Miteling spawn
- At 25%: 2 Imps, 2 Skeleton Soldiers, 2 Mitelings spawn
