A whimsical fae realm dungeon in Il Mheg, filled with mischievous pixies and dangerous illusions. Players must navigate through the dreamlike landscape to confront the fuath king.

## Aenc Thon, Lord of the Lingering Gaze

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Candy Cane | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on tank; use defensive cooldowns |
| Hydrofall | **AoE** | Random players targeted with circle AoEs; spread out |
| Laughing Leap | **Stack** | Either targets center or marks player with stack marker; group if marked |
| Landsblood | **AoE** | Water geysers spawn; ring-shaped geysers are safe, solid circles explode |

**Notes:** During Landsblood, stand on ring-shaped geyser patterns or in spaces where geysers have already exploded.

## Griaule

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Swinge | **Cleave** | Large frontal cone AoE; move behind or to sides |
| Tiiimmberrr | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Fodder | **Adds** | Five saplings tethered to boss spawn; intercept tethers by standing between saplings and boss |
| Coiling Ivy | **Adds** | All players trapped in roots; destroy roots quickly |

**Notes:** Intercepting tethers during Fodder is crucial—each tether that reaches the boss increases its power significantly.

## Aenc Thon, Lord of the Lengthsome Gait

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Crippling Blow | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Virtuosic Capriccio | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE with Bleeding debuff |
| Imp Choir | **Gaze** | Look away from boss to avoid transformation |
| Toad Choir | **Cleave** | Unmarked frontal cone turns players into toads; get behind boss |
| Funambulist's Fantasia | **AoE** | Creates a chasm; players must navigate a narrow tightrope to reach boss before wipe cast completes |
| Changeling's Fantasia | **AoE** | Various transformation-based attacks |

**Notes:** Imp Choir requires looking away (gaze attack), while Toad Choir requires positioning behind the boss—don't confuse these two similar-sounding mechanics.
