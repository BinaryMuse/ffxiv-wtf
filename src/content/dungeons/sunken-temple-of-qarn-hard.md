Return to the depths of the temple where more dangerous guardians await. Features unique multi-part boss mechanics.

## Damaged Adjudicator

A stone guardian with four targetable body parts.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Glowing Part | **Adds** | Attack the part with the red glowing orb - main body resists damage |
| Ruins Crawler | **Adds** | Spawn when a part is destroyed - burn them down |
| Sandblast | **Cleave** | Untelegraphed conal AoE - face boss away from party |
| Desert Storm | **AoE** | Line AoE in front of boss - move to avoid |
| Dust to Dust | **AoE** | Orange marker spawns sand orb that explodes in puddles |
| Shifting Sands | **AoE** | Large sand pit that kills players who sink in - escape immediately |

**Notes:** 
- Target the glowing body part (Head, Arm, or Tail) - main body takes minimal damage
- When part is destroyed, Ruins Crawler adds spawn - kill before boss reforms
- Repeat until all parts destroyed, then finish the main body
- Shifting Sands is deadly - escape immediately or die

## Sabotender Emperatriz

A cactuar queen with a must-interrupt attack.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 100,000 Needles | **Raidwide** | Long cast - deal enough damage to boss to interrupt or wipe |
| Sabotender Guardia | **Adds** | Prevents boss damage - kill immediately |
| 3,000 Needles | **Raidwide** | Moderate raid-wide damage |
| Sabotendash | **Tankbuster** | Moderate damage to tank |
| Sabotender Soldado | **Adds** | Starting adds - defeat them |

**Notes:** 
- 100,000 Needles is a DPS check - you must deal enough damage to interrupt it
- If Sabotender Guardia spawns during 100,000 Needles, kill it immediately
- Guardia prevents damage to the boss and can cause wipe if ignored
- Boss starts with two Sabotender Soldado adds

## Vicegerent to the Warden

The final guardian of the temple depths.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mummy Adds | **Adds** | Spawn throughout the fight - kill them |
| Incinerate | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack |
| Creeping Darkness | **AoE** | Ground-targeted AoE attacks |
| Curse of the Mummy | **Raidwide** | Debuff applied during the fight |

**Notes:** 
- Handle mummy adds as they spawn
- Tank faces boss away from party for frontal attacks
- Standard positioning - melee behind, ranged spread
