An ancient Ala Mhigan city drowned beneath Loch Seld, now home to voidsent and the cursed spirit of a former advisor to the king.

## Kelpie

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Torpedo | **Tankbuster** | Moderate tank damage |
| Rising Seas | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE dealing moderate damage |
| Hydro Pull | **Knockback** | Boss leaps to one side then pulls players toward it - run opposite |
| Hydro Push | **Knockback** | Boss leaps to one side then knocks players back - stack in front |
| Bloody Puddle | **Spread** | Two players get orange markers and drop circular AoE puddles |

**Notes:**
- Arena edges apply Water Resist Down debuffs - avoid them during attacks
- For Hydro Pull, run to the opposite side of the arena
- For Hydro Push, stack in front of the boss to be knocked across safely
- Players marked with Bloody Puddle are tethered to water orbs that follow them

## The Old One

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mystic Light | **Cleave** | Large frontal cone AoE targeting one player |
| Mystic Flame | **Spread** | Two players targeted with large circular AoEs - spread out |
| Subservient | **Adds** | Spawns multiple adds that must be killed before Self-destruct |
| Order to Self-destruct | **Raidwide** | If adds aren't killed in time, heavy raid-wide damage |
| Shifting Light | **AoE** | Players transform into spriggans with one move to instantly kill adds |

**Notes:**
- Kill Subservient adds quickly before Order to Self-destruct completes
- When transformed by Shifting Light, use your single ability to kill adds
- Prioritize add management over boss damage during these phases

## Hrodric Poisontongue

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rusting Claw | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster requiring cooldowns |
| Right Arm Raise | **Cleave** | Unmarked high-damage cone in front - avoid boss's front |
| Crouch + Look Behind | **Cleave** | Unmarked high-damage cone behind - avoid boss's rear |
| The Spin | **AoE** | Proximity attack - move away from boss to reduce damage |
| Ring of Chaos | **AoE** | Marked player gets large donut AoE - move close to boss |
| Circle of Chaos | **Spread** | Circle markers follow players - spread apart |
| Eye of the Fire | **Gaze** | Gaze attack - turn away from boss |

**Notes:**
- Watch the boss's animations for unmarked cone attacks
- Right arm raised = front cone, crouching + looking behind = rear cone
- For Ring of Chaos, marked player should move close to boss so others can attack safely
