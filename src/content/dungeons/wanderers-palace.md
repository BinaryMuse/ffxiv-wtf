An ancient Nymian palace in Upper La Noscea now overrun by Tonberries. Features unique mechanics with Tonberry Stalkers that pursue the party.

## Keeper of Halidom

A Goobbue guardian with poison attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Beatdown | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack that inflicts poison |
| Moldy Sneeze | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack that inflicts poison |
| Inhale | **AoE** | Pulls players toward the boss - followed by Goobbue's Grief |
| Goobbue's Grief | **AoE** | Circle AoE around boss - run away when Inhale starts |
| Moldy Phlegm | **AoE** | Random circular AoE on one player with poison |

**Notes:** 
- Tank should face the boss away from the party
- When Inhale begins, run away immediately to avoid both it and Goobbue's Grief
- Have Esuna ready to cleanse poison debuffs
- Move out of Moldy Phlegm telegraphs

## Giant Bavarois

A flan that spawns colored minions.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Bavarois Army | **Adds** | Spawns colored minions - kill blue first (it heals the boss) |
| Amorphic Flail | **AoE** | AoE attack around the boss |
| Enmity Break | **AoE** | Boss ignores aggro and chases random player |

**Notes:** 
- Priority kill order: Blue minion first (heals the boss), then others
- When the boss breaks enmity, the targeted player should kite until it returns to the tank
- Gather and AoE the add spawns quickly

## Tonberry King

The final boss with complex mechanics involving Tonberry adds.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lateral Slash | **Tankbuster** | Low damage slash attack |
| Sharpened Knife | **Tankbuster** | High damage slash with buff - watch the cast bar |
| Scourge of Nym | **AoE** | Hits random player and applies Heavy debuff |
| Tonberry Adds | **Adds** | Small Tonberries spawn throughout the fight |
| Everybody's Grudge | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage based on Rancor stacks |
| Tonberry Slasher | **Adds** | Dangerous adds that instantly kill nearby players |

**Notes:** 
- Watch the cast bar to distinguish between Lateral Slash and Sharpened Knife
- Kill 3-4 Tonberry adds at a time to build Rancor stacks on the boss
- Limit Rancor stacks to 4 so healers can manage Everybody's Grudge damage
- Stay far away from Tonberry Slasher adds - they instantly kill players
- Kite remaining adds until the boss uses Everybody's Grudge
