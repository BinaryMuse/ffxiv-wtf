The final Endwalker dungeon, set beneath the moon's surface. Players explore underground caverns that hold connections to the Void, culminating in confrontations with classic Final Fantasy enemies.

## Dark Elf

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Abyssal Outburst | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage |
| Void Dark II | **Spread** | Individual AoEs on each player; spread out |
| Hexing Staves | **AoE** | Summons two or three staves that trigger plus-shaped AoEs during Ruinous Confluence |
| Ruinous Confluence | **AoE** | Detonates staves in plus patterns; with three staves, only one tile is safe |
| Sorcerous Shroud | **AoE** | Makes staves invisible; remember positions before they explode |
| Shadowy Sigil | **AoE** | Each tile shows square or triangle; stand on opposite symbol from what boss displays |

**Notes:** The arena is a 4x4 grid. During Sorcerous Shroud, memorize staff positions before they vanish. Watch the boss's symbol during Shadowy Sigil and stand on tiles with the OTHER symbol. Getting hit can inflict Doom (Esuna to cleanse).

## Damcyan Antlion

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sandblast | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage |
| Landslip | **AoE** | Divides arena into lanes; pushes players north or south based on arrow indicators |
| Antlion March | **AoE** | Boss dashes around striking pillars that collapse as line AoEs |
| Earthen Geyser | **Stack** | Stack AoE on healer creating quicksand; move out immediately after |
| Pound Sand | **AoE** | Follows Earthen Geyser; leaves quicksand that applies Six Fulms Under debuff |

**Notes:** Watch the arrow indicators during Landslip to know which direction you'll be pushed. After stacking for Earthen Geyser, immediately move out of the quicksand or you'll be pulled in.

## Durante

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Arcane Edge | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Old Magic | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage |
| Antipodal Assault | **Stack** | Group stack AoE marker |
| Forsaken Fount | **AoE** | Summons orbs that explode in circular AoEs |
| Contrapasso | **AoE** | Split orbs move away from each other; follow arrow telegraphs |
| Duplicitous Battery | **AoE** | Spiral pattern circles; stand in second set, then move to first |
| Twilight Phase | **Cleave** | Half-room cleave based on boss position |
| Dark Impact | **AoE** | Large targeted impact zone |

**Notes:** Pay close attention to arrow and line telegraphs throughout this fight—they indicate safe positioning. During Duplicitous Battery, the timing requires standing in the second pattern initially, then moving into the first after it explodes.
