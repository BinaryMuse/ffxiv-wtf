A level 50 MSQ dungeon that serves as the penultimate story instance of A Realm Reborn. Players assault the Garlean stronghold Castrum Meridianum to confront the XIVth Legion.

## The Black Eft

A Magitek-enhanced warmachina accompanied by 8th Cohort soldiers.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| High-powered Magitek Ray | **AoE** | Untelegraphed frontal line AoE - move to the side or behind the boss |
| Incendiary Support | **Raidwide** | Roomwide AoE that hits four times - healers should restore party health |
| Request Assistance | **Adds** | Summons soldiers and Magitek Colossi - use AoE attacks to clear them |
| Magitek Cannon | **AoE** | Circle AoE near a random player - move away to avoid |

**Notes:**
- Tank should face boss away from party to avoid Magitek Ray
- Off-tank or DPS should pick up adds to protect healers
- Prioritize killing adds with AoE damage

## Magitek Vanguard F-1

A powerful Magitek warmachina with heavy-hitting attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cermet Drill | **Tankbuster** | Heavy single-target damage on the tank - use defensive cooldowns |
| Thermobaric Strike | **Proximity** | Proximity AoE in a corner - move to the opposite end of the arena |
| Hypercharge | **AoE** | Creates circle and ring AoEs with a safe zone between them |
| Overcharge | **AoE** | Frontal conal AoE - avoid standing in front of the boss |

**Notes:**
- Watch for the corner indicator for Thermobaric Strike and run to the far side
- For Hypercharge, stand in the safe zone between the inner circle and outer ring
- Tank should keep boss faced away from party

## Livia sas Junius

The Tribunus of the XIVth Legion who wields gunbaghnakhs in combat.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Artificial Plasma | **Raidwide** | AoE attack that damages all players |
| Roundhouse | **AoE** | Large circle attack centered on boss, followed by edge AoEs - move out then back in |

**Notes:**
- For Roundhouse, first move away from the central circle, then return to the center to avoid the secondary edge AoEs
- Keep the boss relatively centered in the arena
- Healers should be ready for Artificial Plasma damage
