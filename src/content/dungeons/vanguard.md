The fourth dungeon of Dawntrail, set in enemy territory within Zoraal Ja's fortress in Shaaloani. Players storm the vanguard after the Dawnblazer train breaches the dome.

## Vanguard Commander R8

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Electrowave | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage that covers arena edges with dangerous AoEs |
| Enhanced Mobility | **Cleave** | Boss dashes across arena with wingblade cleaving half the arena; stand opposite the dash direction, then move into cleared area |
| Dispatch | **AoE** | Summons untargetable adds based on command type |
| Dispatch (Commence firing) | **AoE** | Adds at front do line AoEs with minimal telegraph |
| Dispatch (Aerial bombardment) | **AoE** | Floating adds create expanding circle AoEs |
| Dispatch (Electrope testing) | **Spread** | Marks all players with AoEs plus line AoEs from all arena sides; find safe spaces between lines |

**Notes:** Enhanced Mobility has only two solutions based on wingblade placement—either center or edges are safe. Watch the boss's dash path and blade position carefully.

## Protector

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Electrowave | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Rapid Thunder | **Tankbuster** | Magical tank buster |
| Search and Destroy | **AoE** | Spawns turrets creating numerous line and circle AoEs; keep moving |
| Fulminous Fence | **AoE** | Summons walls in the arena that restrict movement |
| Battery Circuit | **AoE** | Boss spins with conal AoE, spawning circles underneath and in front/back; rotate with boss while navigating walls |
| Motion Sensor | **AoE** | Inflicts Acceleration Bomb on all players; stop moving when debuff expires. Line AoEs spawn in sequence |
| Tracking Bolt | **Spread** | Large marked AoE on all players |

**Notes:** Motion Sensor requires you to stop moving when the debuff timer expires or you'll take heavy damage. Time your movement between the sequential line AoEs while watching your debuff.

## Zander the Snakeskinner

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Electrothermia | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Saber Rush | **Tankbuster** | Physical tank buster |
| Soulbane Saber | **Cleave** | Line AoE in faced direction that explodes half the arena after delay |

**Phase 2** (after regenerating to full HP):

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Screech | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Shade Shot | **Tankbuster** | Magical tank buster |
| Syntheslean | **AoE** | Conal AoE on a random player |
| Syntheslither | **Cleave** | Boss moves east/west, marks path with swords, then cleaves where swords are; stand opposite first cleave |
| Slitherbane Foreguard | **Cleave** | Soulbane Saber plus frontal cleave during initial line AoE |
| Slitherbane Rearguard | **Cleave** | Soulbane Saber plus rear cleave during initial line AoE |

**Notes:** After defeating Zander the first time, he regenerates with a new form and enhanced abilities. Watch for Foreguard/Rearguard variants which add additional cleaves to Soulbane Saber.
