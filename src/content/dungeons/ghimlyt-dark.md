The climactic final battle of the Stormblood era, as the Alliance confronts the Garlean Empire on the frozen battlefield.

## Mark III-B Magitek Colossus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Jarring Blow | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Wildfire Beam | **Spread** | Circle markers follow all players - spread apart |
| Magitek Slash | **AoE** | Rotating cone attack leaving fire puddles - move into cleared areas |
| Exhaust | **AoE** | Line attack on random player - move out of marker |
| Ceruleum Vent | **Raidwide** | Damages all players |
| Magitek Ray | **Stack** | Stack marker on random player |

**Notes:**
- Watch the rotation indicator during Magitek Slash
- Move into areas where fire puddles have disappeared
- Spread for Wildfire Beam, stack for Magitek Ray

## Prometheus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Nitrospin | **Raidwide** | Damages all and creates ring of fire around arena |
| Oil Shower | **AoE** | Large arc attack - safe cone in front of boss |
| Needle Gun | **Cleave** | Cone attack in front of boss |
| Burrow | **AoE** | Boss burrows, fireball appears - avoid line attack path |
| Unbreakable Cermet Drill | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Freezing Missiles | **AoE** | Proximity markers at edges - move to center, then dodge fireball |

**Notes:**
- Stay near boss to easily reach safe zone for Oil Shower
- Tank should keep boss centered for positioning
- Don't step in the fire ring or fiery lines from Burrow

## Julia quo Soranus and Annia quo Soranus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Heirsbane (Annia) | **AoE** | Target markers take extra damage |
| Delta Trance (Annia) | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Order to Bombard (Annia) | **AoE** | Large circles + Crossbones line knockbacks |
| Angry Salamander (Annia) | **AoE** | Line attack at random player |
| Stunning Sweep (Annia) | **AoE** | Annia jumps to player with large circle AoE |
| Innocence (Julia) | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Ceruleum Tanks (Julia) | **AoE** | Chain explosion of tanks - move to first tank's location after safe |
| Air Strike (Julia) | **Spread** | Circle markers under players + glowing X line attacks |
| Tag Team Transition | **AoE** | Both sisters - line attacks across arena, dodge thin lines |
| Imperial Authority | **Raidwide** | Enrage - kill both sisters before cast completes |

**Notes:**
- Sisters take turns fighting - watch for ability changes
- During Tag Team, find spots without thin line markers
- At low health, sisters use Artificial Boost then Imperial Authority as enrage
- Focus one sister at a time, use Limit Break when both gauges are full
