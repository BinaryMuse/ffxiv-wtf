A dark continuation of the Tam-Tara story. Edda has returned to the depths with sinister intentions involving her deceased beloved.

## Liavinne

An undead archer, one of Edda's former companions risen from death.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Quick Nock | **AoE** | Frontal cone attack |
| Inhumanity | **AoE** | AoE damage attack |
| Rotting Arrow | **AoE** | Targeted ranged attacks |
| Tantrum | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |

**Notes:**
- Focus the boss while handling adds
- Straightforward fight compared to later encounters

## Spare Body

A stitched-together corpse animated by dark magic.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Terror | **AoE** | Large circle AoE that inflicts Terror debuff |
| Swing Attacks | **Cleave** | Heavy melee attacks |

**Notes:** 
- Avoid the Terror AoE circles
- Terror prevents movement and actions for several seconds

## Avere Bravearm

The animated head of Edda's beloved, the final and most tragic boss.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Void Thunder | **AoE** | Lightning-based attacks |
| Terror | **AoE** | Circle AoE inflicting Terror debuff |
| Adds | **Adds** | Undead spawns throughout the fight |
| Edda's Wrath | **Raidwide** | High damage attack during the fight |

**Notes:** 
- This is the culmination of Edda's tragic story from the MSQ
- Watch for Terror AoE indicators and move out
- Handle adds while focusing the boss
- Healers should be ready for party-wide damage

**Dungeon Mechanic:** Usher enemies are tethered to other enemies, making them invulnerable. Kill the tethered enemy first to make the Usher vulnerable.
