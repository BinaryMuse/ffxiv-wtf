The first dungeon of Heavensward, set in an abandoned fortress in Coerthas Western Highlands.

## Towering Oliphant

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rear | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE around the boss - move away |
| Prehistoric Trumpet | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage requiring healing |
| Wooly Inspiration | **Cleave** | Large frontal cone with pull effect |
| Rock of Ages | **AoE** | Stuns a random player and follows with unavoidable AoEs |
| Tusk Butt | **Knockback** | Launches a player into the air |

**Notes:** The boss will stun players and follow up with AoEs - healers need to heal through these attacks.

## Ser Yuhelmeric

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Brain Drain | **Adds** | Party-wide damage that revives Frozen Knights around the arena |

**Notes:** 
- When adds spawn, group them together for efficient AoE damage
- Later phases feature multiple Brain Drain casts in succession
- Focus on burning down the adds quickly to reduce incoming damage

## Opinicus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rubble Mechanic | **AoE** | Uses rubble around the arena for mechanics - stay aware of rubble positions |
| Whirling Gaol | **AoE** | Creates wind zones that damage players |
| Alpine Draft | **Knockback** | Knocks players back - position carefully near rubble for cover |

**Notes:** 
- Similar to Garuda's pillar mechanic from earlier content
- Track the closest pile of rubble to your position throughout the fight
- Use rubble for cover when indicated
