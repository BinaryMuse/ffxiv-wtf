The voidsent have grown stronger in Amdapor Keep. Return to face more powerful enemies in this challenging hard mode.

## Anchag

A voidsent that uses statues around the arena as shields.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Yggr | **Tankbuster** | Tethers to player, deals damage 3x with Vulnerability + Infirmity |
| Valfodr | **AoE** | Line AoE charge at one player - move away from party |
| Hall of Sorrow | **AoE** | Circle AoE around the boss - move out |
| Foreboding Statues | **Adds** | Four statues around arena - can be damaged by boss attacks |

**Notes:** 
- When tethered by Yggr, run behind a Foreboding Statue to redirect it
- Tank boss in the middle of the room
- Don't stand too close to statues - boss attacks can destroy them
- Keep statues alive as they're needed for Yggr mechanic

## Boogeyman

A voidsent that becomes invisible and must be revealed.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Smite of Gloom | **Tankbuster** | Single target damage on tank |
| Invisible | **Adds** | Boss becomes untargetable with stacking damage buff |
| Luminescence Orb | **Adds** | Destroy to gain Irradiated buff |
| Irradiated | **AoE** | When buff expires, illuminates area - reveals boss if nearby |
| Ripples of Gloom | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE requiring party healing |
| Batter | **AoE** | Circle AoE around the boss |

**Notes:** 
- When boss goes invisible, destroy the Luminescence Orb
- Stand near the orb to gain Irradiated buff
- When buff expires, be near where you think the boss is to reveal it
- Boss gains stacking damage buff while invisible - reveal quickly
- If boss splits, both forms need to be "lit up"

## Ferdiad

A high-ranking voidsent with add phases and trap mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Scythe Attacks | **AoE** | Scythe projectiles - avoid them |
| Wild Card | **Adds** | Three Wicked Jester adds spawn tethered to boss |
| Expanding AoE | **Raidwide** | Arena-wide AoE expands - kill adds before it covers arena |
| Player Traps | **AoE** | Players get trapped - free them quickly |
| Jongleur's X | **AoE** | Cross-shaped AoE attack |

**Notes:** 
- Tank near the edge of the arena
- Kill tethered adds immediately during Wild Card phase
- If adds aren't killed before the AoE covers the arena, party wipes
- Free trapped players quickly
- Avoid scythe attacks throughout the fight
