A dungeon set in the mountains of Garlemald, where an abandoned village once practiced the reaper arts. Players seek a voidgate that may lead to the missing dragon Azdaja, confronting voidsent and the Archfiend Cagnazzo.

## Albion

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Albion's Embrace | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Roar of Albion | **Raidwide** | Large AoE requiring line-of-sight behind ice block |
| Call of the Mountain | **AoE** | Boss calls stampede of animals from thicket side; avoid the stampede path |
| Right Slam | **Cleave** | Cleaves right side relative to boss facing; dodge left |
| Left Slam | **Cleave** | Cleaves left side relative to boss facing; dodge right |
| Knock on Ice | **AoE** | Drops ice blocks onto the ground |
| Icebreaker | **AoE** | One ice block explodes with large AoE; all players get spread markers |

**Notes:** During Roar of Albion, hide behind an ice block to avoid the massive AoE. Watch the stampede direction during Call of the Mountain and move to safety.

## Galatea Magna

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Soul Nebula | **Raidwide** | Raidwide magical AoE |
| Waxing Cycle | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE immediately followed by donut AoE; start far, move in |
| Waning Cycle | **AoE** | Donut AoE immediately followed by point-blank AoE; start close, move out |
| Soul Scythe | **AoE** | Boss faces cardinal edge, jumps there with large AoE when cast finishes |
| Scarecrow Chase | **AoE** | Four waves emit with numbered portal teleport sequence; boss shoots line AoEs from each portal. Inflicts Doom on hit |
| Tenebrism | **Gaze** | All players get Glassy-eyed; towers spawn to soak. Players petrify from their location—look away from each other |

**Notes:** For Waxing/Waning Cycle, remember: Waxing = out then in, Waning = in then out. Scarecrow Chase's Doom can be cleansed with Esuna. During Tenebrism, soak towers while facing away from party.

## Cagnazzo

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Void Torrent | **Tankbuster** | Magical tankbuster on highest enmity |
| Stygian Deluge | **Raidwide** | Moderate raidwide; floods arena edge on first cast |
| Antediluvian | **AoE** | Bubbles appear from edge and slowly expand; avoid where they land |
| Body Slam | **Knockback** | Boss jumps to center and knocks back; avoid outer flood and bubbles |
| Hydraulic Ram | **AoE** | Boss dashes across arena with small AoEs after jumping to north wall |
| Hydrofall | **Stack** | Stack marker; group to split magical damage |
| Cursed Tide | **AoE** | Inflicts Neap Tide (spread AoE) or Spring Tide (stack) debuffs |
| Cagnazzo's Power | **Adds** | Summons four Fearsome Flotsam adds; must kill before Tsunami |
| Tsunami | **Raidwide** | Ultimate ability; wipes party if adds weren't killed |
| Voidcleaver | **AoE** | Tethers players to orbs that shoot conal AoEs at their position |
| Lifescleaver | **Cleave** | Conal AoEs from Cagnazzo |

**Notes:** After Stygian Deluge, the outer arena floods and becomes dangerous. Kill all Flotsam adds before the Duty Gauge fills or Tsunami wipes the party. Watch for Neap vs Spring Tide debuffs.
