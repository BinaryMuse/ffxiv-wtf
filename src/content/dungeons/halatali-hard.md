The training grounds have been taken over by dangerous new creatures and gladiators. A harder version with completely different bosses.

## Pyracmon

A giant cyclops with devastating swing attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 1000-tonze Swing | **Raidwide** | Room-wide attack - activate Mammet lever for Manawall immunity |
| 100-tonze Swipe | **Cleave** | Wide frontal cone with knockback - face boss away from party |
| 100-tonze Swing | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE - move away when cast begins |
| Eye of the Beholder | **Cleave** | Large conal AoE with Electrocution DoT - move behind boss |
| Piercing Glower | **AoE** | Line AoE attack |

**Notes:** 
- For 1000-tonze Swing, use the Mammet Activator lever during the cast
- The Admirer Mammet creates a Manawall aura granting immunity
- Tank should face the boss away from the party at all times
- Move close to or behind the boss for Eye of the Beholder

## Catoblepas

A beast with dangerous eye attacks that require coordination.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Demon Eye | **Raidwide** | Ultimate attack reducing accuracy - activate Shadowy Orbs for immunity |
| Tainted Eye | **Adds** | Spawns at boss's head, applies Vulnerability stacks - kill immediately |
| Bestial Roar | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage |

**Notes:** 
- Each player must activate one of four Shadowy Orbs to survive Demon Eye
- Kill Tainted Eye immediately when it spawns at the boss's head
- Tainted Eye stacks map-wide Vulnerability if left alive

## Mumuepo the Beholden

A multi-phase finale with NPC gladiators and a beast-tamer duo.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| NPC Group | **Adds** | Four gladiators - kill order: Silent Moss → Langloisiert → U'libho → Franz |
| Absolute Bind | **AoE** | Chains two players in place - click spawning staff to break |
| Tailswipe | **Cleave** | Large AoE from Narashima - stay at its sides |
| Narashima | **Adds** | Beast that uses Tailswipe - avoid flanks |

**Notes:** 
- First phase: Kill NPCs in order - healer (Silent Moss) first, tank (Franz) last
- Second phase: Fight Mumuepo with Narashima
- Kill Mumuepo first in the final phase
- When chained, find and click the randomly spawning staff to break free
