An optional capstone dungeon set within a virtual reality simulation. Players enter the Stigma Dreamscape to confront Omega-inspired constructs in a digital battlefield.

## Proto-Omega

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mustard Bomb | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Atomic Ray | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Side Cannons | **Cleave** | Cleaves indicated sides of arena; move to safe zone |
| Rear Interceptors | **Cleave** | Cleaves behind the boss; stay in front or to sides |
| Chemical Missile | **AoE** | Drops fire puddles; avoid getting knocked into them |
| Electric Slide | **Stack** | Stack marker that knocks players back; stay still for predictable knockback |
| Guided Missile | **AoE** | Marked players must kite missiles until they explode |

**Notes:** Watch the visual indicators for Side Cannons and Rear Interceptors to find safe zones. During Guided Missile, keep moving to prevent the missiles from catching you.

## Arch-Lambda

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wheel | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Atomic Flame | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Auto-mobile Assault Cannon | **Cleave** | Series of dashes followed by Wave Cannon; position behind boss after final dash |
| Wave Cannon | **Cleave** | Cleaves entire room except directly behind boss |
| Auto-mobile Sniper Cannon | **Spread** | Players marked with numbered dots matching arena positions; spread to avoid hitting each other |

**Notes:** The key to Wave Cannon is tracking the boss's dashes and ending up behind it. For Sniper Cannon, note your number and position away from that side of the arena.

## Stigma-4

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Atomic Ray | **Raidwide** | Inflicts uncleansable bleed; use HoTs to sustain party |
| AI Takeover | **Adds** | Spawns Omega Frame and Hybrid Dragon adds; kill before self-destruct |
| Transformation (Circle) | **AoE** | Boss transforms into circle shape; indicates donut AoE |
| Transformation (Spiral) | **AoE** | Boss transforms into spiral shape; indicates point-blank AoE |
| Mindjack | **AoE** | Inflicts Forced March pushing players inside or outside; position based on transformation shape |

**Notes:** Watch the boss's transformation to determine if it's a donut (get close) or point-blank (get away). Mindjack's Forced March direction must be combined with transformation knowledge.
