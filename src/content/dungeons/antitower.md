An inverted tower beneath the surface of the Sea of Clouds, home to bizarre creatures and the doll Calcabrina.

## Zuro Roggo

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Water Bomb | **AoE** | Circle attack to avoid |
| Odious Croak | **Cleave** | Continuous cone attack - move out of the way |
| Toy Hammer | **AoE** | Stuns the player with Concussion status |
| Musical Bubbles | **AoE** | Avoid bubbles around the arena to prevent Damage Down status |
| Toad Transformation | **Adds** | Two players transform into Poroggo Choirtoads that must be defeated to dispel |

**Notes:** 
- Stay away from musical bubbles floating around the arena
- Kill the transformed player-toads quickly to free your allies

## Ziggy

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Gyrating Glare | **Raidwide** | Damages all players |
| Mystic Light | **AoE** | Circle attacks from untargetable Stardusts - move far away |
| Jittering Glare | **Cleave** | Cone attack on a random player |
| Jittering Jounce | **AoE** | Tethered players can hide behind rocks to transfer the tether |
| Jittering Jab | **Tankbuster** | High-damage attack on the player with highest enmity |

**Notes:** 
- Use rocks strategically to transfer tethers
- Move far from Stardust adds when they appear

## Calcabrina

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Doll Phase | **Adds** | Six dolls appear - red Brina dolls cast donut attacks, blue Calca dolls cast cone attacks |
| Terrifying Glance | **Gaze** | Cone attack on a marked player that inflicts Confusion - look away |
| Brace | **AoE** | Grants Directional Parry - attack only from the safe direction indicated |
| Dollhouse | **AoE** | Transforms two random players into dolls that drift uncontrollably while casting attacks |
| Slapstick | **Raidwide** | Hits all players during Dollhouse |

**Notes:** 
- Ranged DPS can use Limit Break to quickly kill dolls during Doll Phase
- Look away during Terrifying Glance to avoid Confusion
- Check which direction is safe before attacking during Brace
