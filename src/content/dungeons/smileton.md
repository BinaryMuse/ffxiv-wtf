A capstone dungeon set in Mare Lamentorum on the moon. The Loporrits created the Big Cheese to construct a paradise, but contradictory orders have sent it burrowing toward the moon's center, leaving chaos in its wake.

## Face

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Heart on Fire IV | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Temper's Flare | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Lines of Fire | **AoE** | Tethers to stone tablets that shoot AoEs down four rows. Blue faces inflict "Smiley Face," red faces inflict "Frowny Face" |
| Off My Lawn | **Knockback** | Knockback from north or south, sometimes with marked AoEs on all players |
| Upside Down | **AoE** | Reverses face colors; reposition accordingly |

**Notes:** The key mechanic is alternating which face debuff you receive. Getting hit by the same color twice causes heavy damage and knockdown. Position yourself to be knocked into the correct row during Off My Lawn.

## Frameworker

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Steel Beam | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Circular Saw | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Leap Forward | **AoE** | Boss jumps in indicated direction creating huge circle AoE at landing spot |
| Print Workers | **AoE** | Tethers to workers that replicate Leap Forward; dodge away from their landing zones |
| Omnidirectional Onslaught | **AoE** | Sequential conal AoEs; dodge into first one after it resolves |

**Notes:** Watch the direction indicator before Leap Forward to know where the boss will land. Print Workers adds complexity by having multiple copies jump simultaneously.

## The Big Cheese

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Piercing Missile | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Violent Discharge | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE; healers prepare |
| Dispense Explosives | **AoE** | Creates mines on arena; do not step on them |
| Explosive Power | **AoE** | Detonates and removes mines |
| Explosives Distribution | **AoE** | Places two explosives; one arrow = moves close, three arrows = moves far |
| Right/Left Disassembler | **Cleave** | Line AoE from boss's right or left arm; move to indicated side |
| Electric Arc | **Stack** | Stack marker; group to split damage |
| Leveling Missiles | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on all players; spread out |

**Notes:** Conveyor belts affect movement speed. Stay close to boss on the fast-moving side. The arrow indicators on explosives show how far they'll travel before detonating.
