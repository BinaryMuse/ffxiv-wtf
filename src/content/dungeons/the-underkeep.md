A level 100 dungeon introduced in Patch 7.2 "Crossroads," set beneath the city of Alexandria in the ruins of the old kingdom's castle.

## Gargant

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Chilling Chirp | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage; later casts add ground AoEs and marked AoEs |
| Almighty Racket | **Cleave** | Frontal AoE in faced direction |
| Aerial Ambush | **AoE** | Boss climbs pole then moves through sand; charges with line AoE across arena starting from its destination pole |
| Earthsong | **AoE** | Summons two sets of earth orbs that explode in AoEs |
| Trap Jaws | **Tankbuster** | Physical tank buster |

**Notes:** For Aerial Ambush, watch which pole the boss moves to—the line AoE will start from that pole. Avoid the orb explosions from Earthsong while handling other mechanics.

## Soldier S0

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Field of Scorn | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Thunderous Slash | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster |
| Sector Bisector | **Cleave** | Summons clones that disappear in order; final clone does half-room cleave on the side its sword is glowing |
| Ordered Fire | **AoE** | Spawns adds that do line AoEs across the arena |
| Static Force | **AoE** | Conal AoEs that follow players |
| Electric Excess | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on all players |

**Notes:** For Sector Bisector, watch the order clones disappear and note which side the final clone's sword is glowing—that side gets cleaved. Spread for Electric Excess while dodging other mechanics.

## Valia Pira

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Entropic Sphere | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Coordinate March | **AoE** | Purple spheres travel indicated paths; if they hit pink orbs, cross-shaped AoEs explode |
| Turrets | **AoE** | Spawn and shoot line AoEs |
| Electric Field | **Spread** | Conal AoEs on ground and players that expand after delay; spread to large safe spots |
| Neutralize Front Lines | **Cleave** | Frontal AoE accompanied by marked AoEs on all players |
| Bloodmarch | **AoE** | Spawns pink orb on indicated tile |
| Deterrent Pulse | **Stack** | Line stack marker on healer |

**Notes:** For Coordinate March, track purple sphere paths and avoid where they'll hit pink orbs (causes cross AoEs). Electric Field AoEs expand after a delay—find large safe spots and spread. Stack on healer for Deterrent Pulse.
