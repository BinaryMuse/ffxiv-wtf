A dungeon set in the Void, where players search for the dragon Azdaja. The Fell Court of Troia is a twisted realm ruled by dark powers, culminating in a confrontation with one of the Four Archfiends.

## Evil Dreamer

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Void Gravity | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on all players; spread out |
| Dark Vision | **AoE** | Overlapping line AoEs; move out of indicators |
| Unite Mare | **AoE** | Circular AoEs cover entire arena; destroy one Evil Dreamer head to create safe spot |
| Endless Nightmare | **AoE** | Large head casts enrage; tethered adds spawn and explode—stay away from tethered AoE |

**Notes:** This is a gauntlet-style encounter with waves of Evil Dreamer adds. During Unite Mare, quickly destroy one of the heads to create a safe zone before the arena-wide AoEs resolve.

## Beatrice

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hush | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Eye of Troia | **Gaze** | Shrinking gaze ring; look away when it resolves or get Doom debuff |
| Beatific Scorn | **AoE** | Void lines on arena explode in sequence; start at later line, move into first explosion |
| Voidshaker | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE with Void Nail spread markers |
| Void Nail | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on players during Voidshaker |
| Toric Void | **AoE** | Donut AoE around Beatrice with Eye of Troia gazes; stay inside while looking away |
| Antipressure | **Stack** | Stack marker during line AoE effects; group up |

**Notes:** Eye of Troia inflicts Doom if you're looking at it—Esuna can cleanse this. During Toric Void, stay in the safe zone while looking away from all gaze markers.

## Scarmiglione

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cursed Echo | **Raidwide** | Moderate raidwide that inflicts bleed DoT |
| Rotten Rampage | **AoE** | Four sets of sequential AoEs with player markers; dodge while avoiding Brain Rot stacks |
| Brain Rot | **AoE** | Stacking debuff from AoE hits; three stacks causes Zombification (wipe) |
| Blighted Bedevilment | **Knockback** | Knocks all players back; avoid poison pit around arena |
| Blighted Bladework | **AoE** | Large AoE on marked portion of arena; stand far away |
| Blighted Sweep | **Cleave** | Large frontal AoE following Bladework; get behind boss |
| Void Vortex | **Stack** | Stack marker without castbar |
| Corruptor's Pitch | **Raidwide** | Ultimate after add phase; wipes if adds not killed |

**Notes:** Avoid accumulating three Brain Rot stacks at all costs. During the add phase, kill all Necroserf adds before the Duty Gauge fills or Corruptor's Pitch will wipe the party.
