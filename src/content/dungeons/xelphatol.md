The Ixali stronghold in the mountains, where the beastmen make their final stand to summon Garuda.

## Nuzal Hueloc

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Short Burst | **Tankbuster** | Moderate damage on the main tank |
| Windblast | **AoE** | Column AoE to dodge |
| Lift | **AoE** | Boss flies into the air and spawns adds |
| Hotblast | **AoE** | Position under boss after destroying Floating Turret to knock boss down |
| Airstones | **Adds** | Kill immediately - they make the boss invulnerable |

**Notes:** 
- When the boss flies up, kill the Floating Turret last
- After destroying the turret, stand directly under the boss to knock it down
- Prioritize Airstone adds whenever they appear

## Dotoli Ciloc

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| On Low | **Cleave** | Cone AoE to dodge |
| On High | **Knockback** | Raid-wide AoE with knockback - stand in front of barriers to avoid spikes |
| Swiftfeather | **AoE** | Red-marked players drop AoE tornados as they move - place along arena edge |

**Notes:** 
- Position barriers carefully for knockback protection
- Place tornado drops at the edge away from barriers
- After this boss, watch for Warballoons - destroy them immediately when they dock

## Tozol Huatotl

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hawk (Green) | **Spread** | Green markers mean spread out from other players |
| Hawk (Orange) | **Stack** | Orange stack markers mean all players should stack together |
| Summon Garuda | **AoE** | Summons Garuda at arena center - dodge her ranged or donut AoEs (not targetable) |
| Raid-wide AoEs | **Raidwide** | General party-wide damage to heal through |

**Notes:** 
- Pay attention to marker colors: green = spread, orange = stack
- Garuda is not targetable - just dodge her attacks
- Manage general raid-wide damage with healing
