A Garlean military fortress in Gyr Abania, one of the final strongholds before the assault on Ala Mhigo.

## Magna Roader

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wheel | **Tankbuster** | Extremely heavy tankbuster requiring cooldowns and healing |
| Magitek Fire II | **AoE** | Circular AoE puddles to avoid |
| Magitek Fire III | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage requiring healer attention |
| Wild Speed | **AoE** | Boss becomes immune and divebombs across arena - use turrets to stop |
| Legion Adds | **Adds** | Adds spawn and take control of turrets during Wild Speed |

**Notes:**
- Wheel hits very hard - tanks must use cooldowns and healers be ready
- During Wild Speed, kill adds, take control of a turret, and fire at the boss
- Use the turrets to stop the boss's diving phase

## Number XXIV

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Stab | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster (15-20k damage) requiring cooldowns |
| Gale Cut | **AoE** | Multiple circular AoEs to dodge |
| Elemental Pillars | **AoE** | Three pillars spawn with Fire, Ice, or Lightning AoEs |
| Barrier Shift | **AoE** | Must match boss's elemental buff by standing in correct pillar |

**Notes:**
- This boss revolves around matching elemental buffs
- Stand in the pillar matching the boss's element to continue damaging him
- Without the matching buff, your attacks deal knockback damage instead

## Inferno

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ketu Slash | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster requiring cooldowns and healing |
| Rahu Blaster | **AoE** | Large column AoE to avoid |
| Ketu & Rahu | **AoE** | Combination attacks with circular and column AoEs |

**Notes:**
- Watch for the combination of Ketu and Rahu attacks
- Stay mobile to avoid overlapping AoE patterns
- Tank should use cooldowns for Ketu Slash
