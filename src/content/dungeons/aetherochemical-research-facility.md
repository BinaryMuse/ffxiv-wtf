The climactic final dungeon of the Heavensward main scenario, set in an Allagan facility above Azys Lla.

## Regula van Hydrus

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Magitek Turrets | **Adds** | Random players are tethered to turrets - tanks stand between turret and player to soak |
| Stun + Tankbuster | **Tankbuster** | Boss stuns then heavily hits the tank - requires cooldowns and healing |
| Magitek Slug | **AoE** | Boss jumps around targeting random players with column AoEs |
| Magitek Spread | **AoE** | Massive arena-wide AoE - stand directly behind the boss to avoid |

**Notes:** 
- When tethered to a turret, have the tank stand between you and it
- Destroy turrets while managing tether damage

## Harmachis

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Weighing of the Heart | **AoE** | Transforms the boss between different forms |
| Sphinx Form | **AoE** | Uses Riddle of the Sphinx (circular AoE) and Ka (cone AoE) |
| Anti-Cobra Form | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster requiring healer attention |
| Anti-Naga Form | **Gaze** | Petrifaction gaze - turn away to avoid being petrified |
| Anti-Machina Form | **Stack** | Ballistic Missile targets 1 player to stack; Meteor requires all to stack |

**Notes:** 
- Watch for the boss's transformation and adapt to each form
- Turn away during Anti-Naga Form to avoid petrification
- Stack for Meteor in Anti-Machina Form

## Ascian Prime

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lahabrea Phase | **AoE** | Fire-based attacks while Igeyorhm throws column AoEs |
| Igeyorhm Phase | **AoE** | Ice-based attacks while Lahabrea throws column AoEs |
| Thin Ice | **AoE** | Movement causes you to slide uncontrollably in one direction |
| Dark Orb | **AoE** | Dodge dark orb attacks |
| Sea of Pitch | **AoE** | Avoid dark puddles on the ground |
| Fire/Blizzard Spheres | **AoE** | Combined elemental sphere attacks |

**Notes:** 
- Two-phase fight featuring Igeyorhm and Lahabrea sequentially
- The active boss attacks while the inactive one teleports and throws column AoEs
- Be careful moving during Thin Ice - you'll slide
