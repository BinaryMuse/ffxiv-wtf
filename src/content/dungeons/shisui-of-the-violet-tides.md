An underwater palace in the Ruby Sea, once home to the Ruby Princess and now overrun by violent spirits and voidsent.

## Amikiri

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sharpstrike | **Tankbuster** | Moderate tankbuster requiring cooldowns |
| Prey | **AoE** | Targets a player with a puddle AoE that stuns |
| Shuck | **AoE** | Attempts to kill the stunned Prey target - destroy boss's leg to interrupt |
| Kamikiri | **Adds** | Adds spawn and must be picked up by tank and killed quickly |
| Digest | **AoE** | Blue puddle AoEs drop where targeted players stand - spread out |

**Notes:**
- During Prey/Shuck combo, kill the boss's leg to interrupt Shuck and flip the boss
- Flipping the boss applies a Vulnerability debuff for increased damage
- Tank should use cooldowns during add phases

## Ruby Princess

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Seduce | **AoE** | Boss draws players toward center - use chests to gain "Old" buff for immunity |
| Coriolis Kick | **AoE** | Large circular AoE at arena center - move out |
| Abyssal Volcano | **AoE** | Center AoE plus two players get chased by explosions - keep moving |
| Tornadogenesis | **Tankbuster** | Moderate tankbuster requiring cooldowns |

**Notes:**
- Assign each player a chest before the fight starts
- When Seduce is cast, run to your chest to become "Old" and avoid being drawn in
- Players targeted during Abyssal Volcano should keep moving for ~15 seconds

## Shisui Yohi

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Thick Fog | **Adds** | Blankets arena with fog and spawns orbs - kill orbs quickly |
| Shark Adds | **Adds** | Sharks spawn periodically and must be killed quickly |
| Bite and Run | **AoE** | Attack following shark spawns |
| Mad Stare | **Gaze** | Gaze attack that fears anyone looking at the boss |
| Foul Nail | **Tankbuster** | Standard tankbuster attack |

**Notes:**
- Kill fog orbs immediately when they spawn
- Stay away from the boss's ripples when the final add dies
- Look away from the boss during Mad Stare to avoid fear
