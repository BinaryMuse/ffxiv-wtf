An ancient temple in Southern Thanalan dedicated to the sun goddess Azeyma. Its deadly traps and guardians still function after centuries.

## Teratotaur

A minotaur-like creature with a deadly gaze attack.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Triclip | **Tankbuster** | High damage to the tank |
| Frightful Roar | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE - move away from boss |
| Mow | **Cleave** | Conal AoE - avoid standing in front |
| Mortal Ray | **Gaze** | Applies Doom to all players - step on glowing orange tile to cleanse |
| Doom | **Raidwide** | Kills player when timer expires - must be cleansed |
| Dung Wespe | **Adds** | Kill before they cast Final Sting for massive damage |

**Notes:** 
- When Doom is applied, immediately step on the glowing orange floor tile to cleanse it
- Prioritize killing Dung Wespe adds before they use Final Sting
- Tank should face boss away from party due to Mow

## Temple Guardian

A stone golem that periodically becomes vulnerable.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Soulstone | **Adds** | Destroy the Soulstone when it appears to stun the golem |
| Boulder Clap | **AoE** | Frontal cone attack |

**Notes:** 
- Focus the Soulstone immediately when it spawns
- Destroying the Soulstone stuns the golem, allowing a damage window
- Repeat the cycle until the boss is defeated

## Adjudicator

The final boss, a large stone construct that summons various hazards.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sun Juror | **Adds** | Stone heads that must be dragged to glowing squares and killed there |
| Mythical Verge | **AoE** | Spawns objects that shoot blue lasers - avoid at all costs |
| Dark Zone | **Adds** | Black zones appear with staffs inside - DPS must enter and destroy staffs |
| Dark Pulse | **Raidwide** | Staffs cast this if not destroyed in time |

**Notes:** 
- Tank must drag Sun Jurors to the glowing floor squares before killing them
- All players must avoid the blue lasers from Mythical Verges
- DPS must quickly enter dark zones and destroy staffs before Dark Pulse
- At 30% HP, boss spawns 2 dark zones with 4 staffs total - coordinate destruction
