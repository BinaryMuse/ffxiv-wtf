The final leveling dungeon of Endwalker, set on a journey through worlds that have met their end. Players witness the fates of civilizations that failed to overcome the Final Days, culminating in a confrontation with beings who embody despair itself.

## Caustic Grebuloff

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Pox Flail | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Blighted Water | **Stack** | Stack marker on random player; group up to split damage |
| Miasmata | **AoE** | Wind blows orbs in one direction creating AoEs; move to gaps. Getting hit inflicts Necrosis |
| Cough Up | **AoE** | Dodge individual puddle AoEs, then stack on red arrow marker or lose character control |
| Wave of Nausea | **AoE** | Large arena AoE with safe spot directly under boss; move into hitbox |
| Craven Companionship | **Stack** | All players marked; group together to avoid damage |

**Notes:** Watch the wind direction during Miasmata to predict where orbs will travel. Wave of Nausea requires standing directly on top of the boss—the safe zone is in its hitbox.

## Peacekeeper

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Elimination | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Decimation | **Raidwide** | AoE at arena edges; move toward center |
| Electromagnetic Repellant | **AoE** | Creates off-limits zone in arena middle; stay away |
| Order to Fire | **AoE** | Cannons fire line AoEs while players get stack marker; spread to avoid overlap |
| No Future | **Spread** | Ground AoE pattern with player markers; spread to avoid overlap |
| Peacefire | **AoE** | Rotating AoE around arena; keep moving |
| Eclipsing Exhaust | **Knockback** | Knockback from center; position in safe spots |

**Notes:** This is a mechanically dense fight. Balance avoiding the central danger zone while dealing with multiple overlapping AoE patterns. Keep moving during Peacefire.

## Ra-la

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Pity | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank; use cooldowns |
| Warm Glow | **Raidwide** | Heavy room-wide AoE; happens twice in succession |
| Prance | **AoE** | Boss jumps creating sequential AoEs; move into first explosion after it resolves |
| Lifesbreath | **AoE** | Summons butterflies firing line AoEs that inflict Doom; healers must top off affected players |
| Loving Embrace | **Cleave** | Half-room cleave indicated by glowing wing; move to non-glowing side |
| Still Embrace | **AoE** | Large targeted circle AoE; move away from marker |

**Notes:** Lifesbreath's Doom debuff kills players if not healed to full before debuff expires. Healers need to prioritize affected party members. Watch which wing glows during Loving Embrace.
