The traditional Xaela trial grounds on the Azim Steppe, where warriors must prove their worth through combat and survival challenges.

## Garula

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Heave | **Cleave** | Wide frontal cone AoE - move to the side or behind |
| Crumbling Crust | **AoE** | Multiple circular AoEs around the arena - dodge them |
| Rush | **AoE** | Boss tethers to a player and charges - run far away until tether turns purple |
| Warcry | **Adds** | Activates sleeping animals around arena edges with various attacks |
| Earthquake | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage following Warcry |

**Notes:**
- When tethered for Rush, run as far as possible until the tether turns purple
- Warcry awakens animals: Rams create line AoEs, Sheep create circles, Coeurls create massive cone AoEs
- Coeurl cone AoEs are the most dangerous - prioritize avoiding those

## Bardam

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Phase 1 - Golem Gaze | **Gaze** | Keep your back to the center golem while dodging AoEs |
| Phase 1 - AoEs | **AoE** | Circular and ring AoEs to dodge while avoiding gaze |
| Phase 2 - Pillars | **AoE** | Stand in one of three pillars, then avoid donut AoEs |
| Phase 2 - Chase | **AoE** | Run in small circles in designated quadrants while AoEs chase you |
| Phase 3 - Meteor | **AoE** | Hide behind remaining rock when meteor drops |

**Notes:**
- This is a unique mechanics-only encounter with no combat
- The boss has no health bar and doesn't attack directly
- Phase 3 combines previous phases - dodge golem AoEs and blue lines, then hide behind rock

## Yol

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Feather Cut | **Cleave** | Tank cleave with moderate-to-high damage |
| Wind Unbound | **AoE** | AoE attack pattern to dodge |
| Flutterfall | **Spread** | Players with orange markers should spread out |
| Eye of the Fierce | **Gaze** | Gaze attack - look away from the boss |

**Notes:**
- Spread out when you have an orange marker from Flutterfall
- Look away during Eye of the Fierce to avoid the gaze effect
- Tank should use cooldowns for Feather Cut
