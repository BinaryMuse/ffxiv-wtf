Storm the Garlean fortification at the border between Eorzea and Ala Mhigo in this climactic pre-Stormblood dungeon.

## Magitek Predator

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Magitek Claw | **Tankbuster** | Moderate damage - use cooldowns |
| Sky Armor Reinforcements | **Adds** | Adds tether to DPS players and cannot be tanked - prioritize killing them |
| Magitek Ray | **AoE** | Column AoE attack to dodge |
| Magitek Missiles | **AoE** | Random player marked with red indicator - move away from others |

**Notes:** 
- Kill Sky Armor adds quickly - they target DPS and can't be tanked
- Spread out when targeted by missiles

## Armored Weapon

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Launcher | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage to heal through |
| Dynamic Sensory Jammer | **AoE** | Applies "Extreme Caution" debuff - **stop moving and attacking** or take high damage |
| Diffractive Laser | **AoE** | Circle AoE puddle on random player |
| Magitek Bit | **AoE** | Grid formation line attacks - stand between telegraphs |
| Distress Beacon | **Adds** | Summons multiple adds to burn down quickly |

**Notes:** 
- When you get Extreme Caution, **freeze** - don't move or attack until it expires
- Stand between the grid lines during Magitek Bit

## The Griffin

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Claw of the Griffin | **Tankbuster** | High damage tank attack |
| Flash Powder | **Gaze** | Face away from boss to avoid Accuracy Down debuff |
| Sanguine Blade | **AoE** | Attack covering most arena except behind boss - move behind quickly |
| Lionshead | **AoE** | Long cast - DPS the middle sword to create a safe zone |
| Restraint Collar | **Adds** | Binds the healer - destroy immediately |

**Notes:** 
- Always look away during Flash Powder
- Get behind the boss for Sanguine Blade
- Free the healer immediately when Restraint Collar appears
- DPS the center sword during Lionshead to create safety
