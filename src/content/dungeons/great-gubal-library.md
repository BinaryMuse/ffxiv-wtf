A vast library in the Dravanian Hinterlands containing the accumulated knowledge of the Sharlayan scholars.

## Demon Tome

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Liquefy | **AoE** | Series of line AoEs down the middle, then along both edges |
| Dark Blizzard III | **AoE** | Targets random players with AoE damage and Heavy/Slow debuffs |
| Repel | **Knockback** | Pushes all players away from the boss |
| Disclosure | **AoE** | **Lethal attack** - the boss closes and all players must move behind it to survive |
| Words of Winter | **AoE** | Covers the floor with ice, causing players to slide when moving |

**Notes:** 
- When Disclosure is cast, get behind the boss immediately or die
- Dark purple line attacks inflict Slow if you're hit - dodge them

## Byblos

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Head Down | **AoE** | Line attack targeting a random player - getting closer to the boss reduces its size |
| Page 64 Adds | **Adds** | Two adds appear during invincibility phase - they use Death Ray line attacks |
| Roar | **Raidwide** | Inflicts all players with a status effect during invincibility phase |
| Tail Swipe | **Cleave** | Avoid standing behind the boss |

**Notes:** 
- Byblos jumps onto its book and becomes invincible while adds spawn
- Focus on dodging Death Ray attacks from the Page 64 adds

## The Everliving Bibliotaph

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Void Call | **Adds** | Summons voidsent adds from glowing circles |
| Deep Darkness | **Raidwide** | Party-wide darkness damage |
| Void Spark | **AoE** | Targeted circle AoEs on players |
| Magic Burst | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE around the boss |

**Notes:** 
- A hulking mammet created to guard the library's forbidden section
- Contains an arcane tome with voidsent sealed within
- Kill adds quickly when they spawn
