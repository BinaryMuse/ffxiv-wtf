The fourth and final floor of Omega: Alphascape, the climax of the Omega raid series. Face Omega's male and female forms separately, then together.

## Omega-M

The male form of Omega, using blade-based attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Solar Ray | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster with nearby AoE - other players move away |
| Efficient Bladework | **Cleave** | Blade attack near tank - move away from tank |
| Subject Simulation M | **AoE** | Form change - get close to boss (Discharger follows) |

## Omega-F

The female form of Omega, using energy-based attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Laser Shower | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage - heal through |
| Discharger | **Knockback** | Knockback from boss - stay close to avoid flying off |
| Subject Simulation F | **AoE** | Form change - move away (Efficient Bladework follows) |

## Combined Phase (Omega M & F)

Both forms appear together for the final phase.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Firewall | **AoE** | Debuff restricting which form you can damage - focus assigned target |
| Cosmo Memory | **Raidwide** | Heavy group-wide AoE - use mitigation |
| Bladedance | **Tankbuster** | Major tank buster - heavy cooldowns required |
| Shield & Shower | **Adds** | Kill Omega-M first, then Omega-F |

**Strategy:**
- During Omega-M phases, stay close after Subject Simulation
- During Omega-F phases, move away after Subject Simulation
- Tanks pull M and F apart during combined phase
- Players with Firewall debuff focus their assigned form
- Kill Omega-M before Omega-F during Shield & Shower
- Be ready for repeated Laser Shower after consecutive boss kills

**Notes:** The key is remembering the form-change patterns: M = get close, F = move away. During the combined phase, coordination between tanks keeping the bosses separated is essential.
