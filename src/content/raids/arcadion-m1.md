The first fight of the Arcadion raid series in Dawntrail. Black Cat is a fierce feline fighter in the AAC Light-heavyweight division.

## Black Cat

The arena is a 4×4 grid where tiles can crack and break when hit twice by certain mechanics.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Bloody Scratch | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magical damage |
| Biscuit Maker | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster requiring cooldowns |
| Clawful | **Stack** | Stack marker requiring grouped positioning |
| One-two Paw | **Cleave** | Black Cat cleaves left and right sides in order her claws glow; watch which glows first |
| Black Cat Crossing | **AoE** | Conical AoEs from boss; stand in second safe spot, then move to first |
| Mouser | **AoE** | Marks arena squares that will be hit; squares hit twice break. Position on tiles hit once or not targeted |

### Advanced Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Elevate and Eviscerate | **Knockback** | Throws targeted player back; position to land on uncracked tiles |
| Predaceous Pounce | **AoE** | Boss dashes with line and circle AoEs; safe spots exist on arena edges |
| Grimalkin Gale | **Spread** | Spread to avoid clipping each other |
| Shockwave | **Knockback** | Knockback from center; knockback immunities work |
| Copycat | **AoE** | Summons a clone to use Predaceous Pounce |

### Tile Mechanic

The central mechanic involves the grid floor:
1. **Tiles hit once** = Cracked but safe to stand on
2. **Tiles hit twice** = Break and become dangerous
3. **Position carefully** to preserve tiles for later mechanics
4. **During Mouser** = Watch which tiles are being targeted and plan positioning

### Tips

- Watch both claws during One-two Paw—the glow order determines cleave order
- During Black Cat Crossing, stand in the second conal safe spot and move into the first after it resolves
- Preserve uncracked tiles for knockback mechanics
- Coordinate stack and spread positions to avoid breaking critical tiles
