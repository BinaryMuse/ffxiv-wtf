The third floor of Alexander: Midas, featuring a single boss encounter against Quickthinx Allthoughts, the leader of the Illuminati. A unique encounter with jail mechanics and electrical hazards.

## Quickthinx Allthoughts

The goblin mastermind behind the Illuminati's plans for Alexander.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Frontal Cleave | **Cleave** | Tank faces boss away from raid |
| Sizzlespark | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE damage - heal through |
| Sizzlebeam | **AoE** | Line AoE - dodge to the side |
| Bomb Deployment | **Adds** | Bombs spawn around arena - destroy or avoid |
| Jail Mechanic | **Adds** | Player trapped in jail cell - others must free them |
| Electric Floor | **AoE** | Floor panels electrify - avoid standing on lit panels |
| Add Waves | **Adds** | Goblin adds spawn periodically - pick up and AoE down |
| Glupgloop | **AoE** | Puddle AoEs spawn - don't stand in them |

**Strategy:**
- Tank boss in center, facing away from raid
- Watch for floor panels lighting up - move off them before they electrify
- When a player is jailed, others must interact with the jail to free them
- Kill bombs before they explode
- Handle add waves while maintaining boss damage
- Spread for individual AoEs, stack for heal checks

**Notes:** The jail mechanic is the signature feature of this fight. If jailed players aren't freed quickly, they take significant damage. Pay attention to your surroundings and free teammates promptly.
