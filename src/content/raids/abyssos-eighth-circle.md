The fourth and final encounter of the Abyssos raid tier. Hephaistos, known as "Perfect Imperfection," is a hemitheos who can transform into different beast forms during battle.

## Hephaistos

A transformation-based fight where the boss shifts between forms, each with unique mechanics. Features knockback sequences, gaze attacks, and fire-based hazards.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Genesis of Flame | **Raidwide** | Moderate party-wide magic damage - shield and heal |
| Flameviper | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster attack requiring cooldowns |
| Sunforge (Serpent) | **Cleave** | Summons serpent - line AoE down the middle of arena |
| Sunforge (Phoenix) | **Cleave** | Summons phoenix - cleaves sides of arena, safe in middle |
| Reforged Reflection | **AoE** | Boss transforms into different forms with unique attacks |
| Blazing Footfalls | **Knockback** | Boss dashes across arena with knockbacks, then jumps to marked spots in sequence |
| Rearing Rampage | **Raidwide** | Moderate raidwide magic damage |
| Fourfold Fires | **AoE** | Proximity meteors at intercardinal edges that leave fiery puddles |
| Chthonic Vent | **AoE** | Boss tethers to fire puddles and summons serpents |

### Sunforge (Serpent vs Phoenix)

Watch what creature appears:

- **Serpent**: Line AoE down the center - move to sides
- **Phoenix**: Cleaves both sides - stay in the center

The visual telegraph shows which creature before the attack resolves.

### Reforged Reflection (Transformation)

Hephaistos transforms into different forms:

**Quadrupedal Form:**
- Performs dashing attacks across the arena
- Watch for dash indicators and move out of path

**Serpentine Form:**
- Summons adds requiring target switching
- Uses gaze mechanics - look away when indicated

### Blazing Footfalls

A sequence mechanic:

1. **Boss dashes** across the arena with knockback effects
2. **Position to avoid** being knocked into edges
3. **Marked spots appear** showing where boss will jump next
4. Watch the **numbered sequence** and avoid landing zones in order

### Arena Hazard

After the first raidwide:
- **Arena edges ignite** with fire
- Standing in edges inflicts a **burn DoT**
- Stay toward center when possible

### Tips

- Learn to quickly identify serpent vs phoenix for Sunforge
- During Blazing Footfalls, track the numbered jump sequence
- For gaze mechanics in serpentine form, look away from the boss
- Stay away from arena edges after they ignite
- Position away from intercardinals during Fourfold Fires (proximity damage)
- Healers watch for Genesis of Flame into Rearing Rampage combinations
