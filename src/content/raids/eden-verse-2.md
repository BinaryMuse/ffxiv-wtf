The second floor of Eden's Verse features a multi-phase encounter against Garuda, Ifrit, and their fusion Raktapaksa. Each primal brings distinct mechanics that combine in later phases.

## Garuda Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Superstorm | **Raidwide** | Party-wide AoE damage |
| Ferostorm | **AoE** | Blue pillars with cone AoEs away from Garuda - position safely |
| Air Bump | **Stack** | Two-player stack mechanic - pair up or get stunned |
| Thorns | **AoE** | Ground AoE following Air Bump - move away if you have vulnerability |
| Storm of Fury | **Cleave** | Blue lines target two players with conal AoEs - spread apart |
| Vacuum Slice | **AoE** | Red AoE across arena center creates void zones |
| Occluded Front | **AoE** | Six bombs pushed toward void zone - stand between, then move away |

## Ifrit Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Inferno Howl | **Raidwide** | Party-wide fire damage |
| Eruption | **AoE** | Ground-targeted fire explosions - keep moving |
| Radiant Plume | **AoE** | Large fire AoEs appear - dodge to safe zones |
| Incinerate | **Tankbuster** | Fire tankbuster - mitigate and heal |
| Hands of Flame | **Spread** | Fire markers on players - spread to avoid overlap |
| Hands of Hell | **AoE** | Chase AoEs that follow players - kite away |
| Strike Spark | **AoE** | Ifrit dashes and leaves fire trails |

## Combined Phase (Garuda & Ifrit)

Both primals attack simultaneously with overlapping mechanics from their individual phases.

## Raktapaksa

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Conflag Strike | **Tankbuster** | Combined primal tankbuster |
| Air Bump + Eruption | **Stack** | Stack mechanic with fire AoEs - stack then move |
| Ferostorm + Radiant | **AoE** | Combined wind and fire patterns - find safe spots |

**Strategy:**
- During Garuda phase, watch for pillar positions and Vacuum Slice safe zones
- For Air Bump, always have at least two players stacking to avoid stun
- In Ifrit phase, keep moving for Eruption and spread for Hands of Flame
- The combined phase requires handling both sets of mechanics simultaneously
- Raktapaksa combines elements from both primals - adapt quickly

**Notes:** This fight is arena-positioning heavy. Watch mechanics rather than cast bars for many abilities.
