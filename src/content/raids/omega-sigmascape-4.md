The fourth and final floor of Omega: Sigmascape, featuring Kefka from Final Fantasy VI. The mad clown god uses a "Trickery" mechanic that reverses normal mechanics.

## Kefka

The iconic villain uses deceptive attacks where "?" indicators reverse normal behavior.

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hyper Drive | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster - use cooldowns |
| Blizzard Blitz | **AoE** | Donut AoE - safe zone is melee range center |
| Blizzard Blitz (?) | **AoE** | Trickery version - stand IN the AoE instead |
| Flagrant Fire | **Spread** | Marked players spread apart |
| Flagrant Fire (?) | **Stack** | Trickery version - stack together instead |
| Thrumming Thunder | **AoE** | Two column AoEs across arena - avoid |
| Thrumming Thunder (?) | **AoE** | Trickery version - stand IN them |

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ultima Upsurge | **Raidwide** | Moderate raid-wide damage - heal through |
| Shockwave | **Knockback** | Blue orb appears - move toward it to avoid being knocked off |
| Graven Image (Column) | **AoE** | Tower shows multiple column AoEs - dodge between them |
| Graven Image (Area) | **AoE** | Large orb on one side of tower - move to opposite side |
| Graven Image (Gaze) | **Gaze** | Eye on tower - look away from tower |
| Graven Image (? Gaze) | **Gaze** | Trickery eye - look AT the tower instead |
| Timely Teleport | **AoE** | Two black circles spawn - Kefka teleports to outer circle and attacks; safe zones are beside outer circle |

**The Trickery System:**
- Any telegraph with "?" icons reverses the normal mechanic
- Normal AoE = avoid it; "?" AoE = stand in it
- Normal spread = spread out; "?" spread = stack up
- Normal gaze = look away; "?" gaze = look at it

**Strategy:**
- Always check for "?" indicators before reacting to mechanics
- Watch the Graven Image tower carefully for its attack type
- For Shockwave, position toward the blue orb before knockback
- During Timely Teleport, find safe zones beside the outer circle

**Notes:** The entire fight tests your ability to recognize and respond to the Trickery mechanic. Train yourself to look for "?" icons and reverse your normal instincts when they appear.
