The third floor of Alexander: The Creator, featuring Cruise Chaser - an expertly crafted forgekin defending Alexander. A high-mobility fight requiring constant movement and positioning.

## Cruise Chaser

A transforming machine capable of switching between a winged vessel and bipedal gunner form.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spin Crusher | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack - tank faces away from party |
| Whirlwind | **Raidwide** | Moderate raid-wide magic damage |
| Blassty Charge | **AoE** | Boss charges across arena - avoid the path |
| Photon | **Raidwide** | Shield check - healers shield and heal through |
| Optical Sight | **AoE** | Multiple targeted AoEs - spread and avoid overlapping |
| Limit Cut | **AoE** | Numbered markers - boss dashes to players in order |
| GA-100 | **Tankbuster** | Heavy magic tankbuster - use cooldowns |
| Propeller Wind | **Gaze** | Look away or be confused |
| Transform | **AoE** | Boss changes form - prepare for different attack patterns |
| Super Hawk Blaster | **AoE** | Line AoEs across the arena - find safe zones |

**Strategy:**
- Tank faces Cruise Chaser away from the party
- Spread for Optical Sight to avoid overlapping AoEs
- During Limit Cut, watch for numbered markers and predict dash patterns
- For Photon, ensure shields and healing are ready
- Look away during Propeller Wind to avoid confusion
- Stay mobile - this fight requires constant repositioning
- Find gaps during Super Hawk Blaster

**Notes:** Cruise Chaser is one of the more movement-intensive fights in Alexander. The boss's ability to transform means attack patterns shift throughout the encounter. Stay alert and keep moving.
