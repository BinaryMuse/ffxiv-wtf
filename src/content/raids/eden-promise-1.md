The first floor of Eden's Promise features the Cloud of Darkness in her Dissidia form. The fight cycles through three distinct phases with increasing complexity on each loop.

## Cloud of Darkness

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Art of Darkness | **Cleave** | Glowing ball indicates attack side - stand on opposite side of boss |
| Ground-razing Particle Beam | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage |
| Wide-angle Phaser | **AoE** | Boss leaps to one side - move toward where boss is heading |
| Flood of Darkness | **Raidwide** | Darkness damage pulse |
| Zero-form Particle Beam | **AoE** | Linear beam attack - dodge to sides |
| Anti-air Particle Beam | **AoE** | Center squares become hazardous - move to edges |
| Summon | **AoE** | Two arena sides become dangerous - stay center |
| Rejuvenating Balm | **AoE** | Stand between boss and thorn bush, not on sides |
| Devouring Dark | **AoE** | Expanding dark zones - avoid |
| Hypercharged Cloud | **Adds** | Kill quickly or boss gains damage buff |

### Phase Breakdown

**Opening Phase:**
- Dodge various AoEs throughout the arena
- Watch for Art of Darkness tell - glowing ball shows attack direction

**Grid Phase:**
- Arena becomes a grid of squares
- Purple-bordered squares are dangerous - move to safe squares
- Watch for Summon and Anti-air Particle Beam combinations
- Kill Hypercharged Clouds immediately

**Thorns Phase:**
- Avoid large thorn bushes throughout arena
- Break tethers by running away
- For Wide-angle Phaser, move toward the boss's leap direction
- Rejuvenating Balm requires positioning between boss and thornbush

**Strategy:**
- Track Art of Darkness carefully - the ball position is your tell
- In Grid phase, prioritize safe squares while handling AoEs
- During Thorns phase, use the safe space between overlapping attacks
- The fight loops with added complexity - patterns repeat with more overlays

**Notes:** Phases repeat with additional mechanics layered on each loop. Meteor towers appear in later iterations requiring at least one player to soak each.
