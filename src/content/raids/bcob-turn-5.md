The climactic finale of the Binding Coil, featuring Twintania - one of the most iconic and challenging fights of A Realm Reborn. This fight tests coordination, positioning, and individual skill.

## Twintania

A multi-phase fight with several deadly mechanics that require precise execution.

### Phase 1 (100% - 85%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Death Sentence | **Tankbuster** | Heavy physical damage with Physical Vulnerability stacking - requires cooldowns |
| Plummet | **Cleave** | Frontal cleave dealing significant damage to the tank |

### Phase 2 - Divebombs (85% - 55%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Divebombs | **AoE** | Twintania disappears and divebombs marked players in sequence |
| Neurolink | **AoE** | Drops puddles that apply heavy debuff - used later for mechanics |
| Adds | **Adds** | Scourge of Meracydia adds spawn and must be killed |

**Notes:** 
- Watch for green markers indicating divebomb targets
- Spread out and bait divebombs away from the raid
- Kill adds quickly before they overwhelm healers

### Phase 3 - Twisters (55% - 29%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Twisters | **AoE** | Small tornadoes spawn under players at end of cast - instant death if touched |
| Fireballs | **Stack** | Red triangle marks a player - party stacks to share damage |

**Twister Strategy:**
- At the end of Twintania's Twister cast, tornadoes appear under players
- You must move away immediately - running into a twister is instant death
- Twisters also knock back nearby players into walls
- Players are typically assigned specific spots to move to

**Fireball Strategy:**
- Collapse to middle to share damage after spreading for Twisters
- The marked player should not run away

### Phase 4 - Hatch (29% - 0%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hatch | **AoE** | Orbs spawn that deal massive damage - stand in Neurolink to absorb safely |
| Twisters | **AoE** | Continue from Phase 3 |
| Fireballs | **Stack** | Continue from Phase 3 |

**Notes:**
- Use Neurolinks dropped in Phase 2 to safely absorb Hatch orbs
- Maintain Twister and Fireball strategies while handling Hatch
- This is the final push - save cooldowns and Limit Break

**General Notes:**
- Recommended to use Party Finder rather than Duty Finder due to long queue times
- Practice Twister movement extensively - it's the most common wipe cause
- Tank swaps may be needed for Death Sentence stacks
- This fight does not appear in roulettes
