The third floor of Eden's Gate features a reimagined Leviathan encounter, summoned through the party's memories to restore the water element. The fight takes place on a dynamic platform with water mechanics.

## Leviathan

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rip Current | **Tankbuster** | Cleaving tankbuster with vulnerability - face away from party |
| Tidal Roar | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide water damage - heal and shield |
| Tidal Wave | **Knockback** | Waterfall destroys east/west arena sections - move north |
| Undersea Quake | **AoE** | Ground AoE following Tidal Wave - stay in safe zones |
| Temporary Current | **AoE** | Diagonal AoE covering most of arena - position opposite Leviathan's head |
| Drenching Pulse | **AoE** | Baited AoE on every player - stack center at max melee range |
| Freak Wave | **Spread** | Circle AoE on each player - spread to pre-assigned positions |
| Maelstrom | **AoE** | Puddles that expand and deal heavy damage - avoid pools |
| Spinning Dive | **AoE** | Leviathan charges across arena - dodge the path |
| Surging Tsunami | **Knockback** | Knockback from arena edge - position accordingly |

**Strategy:**
- Pre-assign tank positions for Rip Current (east or west)
- Split into light parties: one healer, one tank, two DPS per side
- For Temporary Current, watch where Leviathan's head appears and stand opposite
- Stack center for Drenching Pulse, then spread for Freak Wave
- During Maelstrom, avoid expanding puddles while handling other mechanics

**Notes:** This fight introduces split-party mechanics that become more common in later Eden tiers. Coordination between healers for cross-party healing can help during intense phases.
