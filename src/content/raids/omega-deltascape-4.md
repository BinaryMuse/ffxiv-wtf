The fourth and final floor of Omega: Deltascape featuring Exdeath, the warlock from Final Fantasy V who wields the power of the Void.

## Exdeath

A powerful mage with two distinct phases, using Void-enhanced versions of elemental spells.

### Phase 1

The boss can absorb energy from the Void behind him, changing spell effects.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Doom | **Raidwide** | Debuff on marked players - healers must dispel or players die |
| Thunder III | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster with circular AoE - tank swap at 2 stacks of Lightning Resist Down |
| Void Thunder III | **AoE** | Point blank AoE applying Paralysis - move away from boss |
| Blizzard III | **Spread** | Spreads puddle AoEs on marked players - spread apart |
| Void Blizzard III | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE that freezes stationary players - keep moving |
| Fire III | **AoE** | Marks players to drop puddles - place away from group |
| Void Fire III | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage applying Pyretic - stand completely still |
| Holy | **Stack** | All players stack together to share damage |
| Vacuum Wave | **Knockback** | Raid-wide damage with knockback (no edge danger in Phase 1) |

### Phase 2 (Transition)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Big Bang | **AoE** | Boss disappears, bomb spawns center - all players spread to edges |
| Neo Exdeath | **Cleave** | Giant head erupts with large conal AoE - position behind it |

### Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Flare | **Spread** | Marks 3 players with large AoEs - spread far apart |
| Meteor | **Raidwide** | Dropped at arena center - use cooldowns and heavy healing |
| Black Hole | **AoE** | Orbs spawn around arena - avoid contact |
| Vacuum Wave | **Knockback** | Now can knock players off the edge - stay toward center |

**Strategy:**
- Watch for Void absorption animation to predict spell variants
- Tank swap at 2 stacks of Lightning Resist Down during Thunder III
- Healers must cleanse Doom quickly
- For Void Fire III (Pyretic), stop all actions including movement
- In Phase 3, stay near center to avoid Vacuum Wave edge deaths

**Notes:** The key is recognizing normal vs Void spell variants. Void Blizzard requires movement while Void Fire requires stillness - getting these confused is deadly.
