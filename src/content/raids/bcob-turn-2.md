Turn 2 is a unique timed encounter with a 12-minute limit featuring 6 mini-boss nodes leading to the final ADS boss. Strategic route planning is essential.

## Node System

When you kill a mini-boss node, two things happen:
1. That node's unique ability is removed from the final ADS boss
2. ADS gains a passive buff from that node

This creates a trade-off: eliminating dangerous abilities makes the boss stronger overall.

## All Nodes - Shared Abilities

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Frontal Cleave | **Cleave** | Applies stacking debuff increasing damage taken, refreshes with each hit |
| High Voltage | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE causing damage and paralysis - must be silenced every time |
| Repelling Cannons | **AoE** | 360-degree close-range AoE around the node |
| Piercing Laser | **AoE** | Long-range rectangular attack that must be avoided |

### Tank Swap Mechanic

The stacking debuff from cleaves requires constant tank swaps:
- Position tanks so cleaves don't hit both
- Off-tank Provokes when main tank signals (often by jumping)
- Swap at dangerous debuff levels (~3+ stacks)

## ADS (Final Boss)

After defeating nodes (minimum 1 required), face the final ADS boss. It will have:
- All abilities from nodes you didn't kill
- Passive buffs from nodes you did kill

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| High Voltage | **Raidwide** | Must be silenced - causes paralysis and heavy damage |
| Vacuum Wave | **Knockback** | Radial knockback from boss (removed by killing specific node) |
| Chain Lightning | **Spread** | Spreads between nearby players (removed by killing specific node) |
| Firestream | **AoE** | Linear fire attack (removed by killing specific node) |
| Ballast | **AoE** | Large directional attack (removed by killing specific node) |

**Notes:**
- Bring at least two silencers (Bards work well) for High Voltage
- Two primary viable routes exist - choose based on which abilities you want removed
- Recommended composition: 2 tanks, 2 healers, 4 DPS
- You cannot kill all nodes within the time limit - plan your route carefully
