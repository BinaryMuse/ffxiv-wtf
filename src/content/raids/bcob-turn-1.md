The first turn of the Binding Coil delves into the depths beneath Castrum Occidens, where adventurers encounter Caduceus, a massive snake guarding the way forward.

## Caduceus

A unique boss fight centered around managing Steel Scales stacks and coordinating the split phase at 66% HP.

### Phase 1 (100% - 66%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hood Swing | **Cleave** | Massive frontal cone (160-180 degrees) - only tanks should be in front |
| Whip Back | **Cleave** | Devastating rear-facing AoE - do not stand behind the boss |
| Steel Scales | **Enrage** | Boss gains a stack every ~45 seconds, increasing damage dealt |
| Glowing Tiles | **Adds** | Floor tiles glow and spawn Dark Matter Slimes on players standing on them |
| Acid Spit | **AoE** | Single-target attack that leaves a damaging DoT pool on the ground |

### Dark Matter Slimes

When tiles glow, slimes spawn on any player standing on them:
- Slimes fixate on whoever spawned them
- If moved too far away, they explode dealing massive AoE damage
- Lower slimes to under 20% HP before feeding them to Caduceus
- Feeding slimes removes Steel Scales stacks from the boss

### Phase 2 (66% - 0%)

At 66% HP, Caduceus splits into two separate snakes.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Split | **Adds** | Boss divides into two snakes, each retaining Steel Scales stacks |
| Recombination | **Enrage** | If the two halves get too close, they merge with combined stacks |
| Synchronized Kill | **Enrage** | Both halves must die within ~20 seconds or the surviving copy enrages (rapid Steel Scales stacks) |

**Notes:**
- Organize into two teams (1 Tank, 1 Healer, 2 DPS each) for the split phase
- Approach from the center/sides to avoid frontal and rear cleaves
- Clear all Steel Scales stacks before the 66% split if possible
- Keep the two snake halves separated but kill them within 20 seconds of each other
