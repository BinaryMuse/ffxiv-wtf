The second floor of Eden's Gate introduces the unique Spell-in-Waiting mechanic, where the boss delays attacks by up to 30 seconds. Players must track delayed abilities and react accordingly.

## Voidwalker

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spell-in-Waiting | **AoE** | Delays abilities by up to 30 seconds - watch for visual indicators |
| Doomvoid Guillotine | **Cleave** | Column AoE with adds - dodge to the sides |
| Doomvoid Slicer | **AoE** | Arena-wide AoE with adds - avoid the danger zones |
| Dark Fire III | **Spread** | Purple markers on four players - spread out |
| Unholy Darkness | **Stack** | White/gold markers - stack together with party |
| Shadoweye | **Gaze** | Face away from the targeted player |
| Hand of Erebos | **Knockback** | Stand next to the hand to avoid being flung across arena |
| Entropy | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage - heal through |
| Shadowflame | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster - use cooldowns |

**Strategy:**
- Pay close attention to Spell-in-Waiting - it affects whether you stack or spread
- When Dark Fire III appears, spread to avoid overlapping damage
- For Unholy Darkness, stack with the party
- Shadoweye requires looking away from the affected player, not the boss
- Stand near Hand of Erebos to avoid the knockback

**Notes:** The fight is split into two phases. The key to success is tracking which abilities are delayed by Spell-in-Waiting. Normal mode is forgiving once you understand the core mechanic.
