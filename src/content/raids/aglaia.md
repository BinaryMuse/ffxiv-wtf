The first 24-player alliance raid of the Myths of the Realm series. Face four of the Twelve, the patron deities of Eorzea, in this journey to uncover the truth behind the gods.

## Byregot

The Builder, patron deity of craftsmen. This encounter features a transforming arena and hammer-based mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ordeal of Thunder | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide lightning damage |
| Byregot's Strike | **Tankbuster + Knockback** | Blue AoE circle on tank with knockback - tank away from party |
| Builder's Build | **Cleave** | Buff that causes Byregot's Strike to spawn four lightning cone AoEs - position adjacent to cones |
| Byregot's Ward | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage requiring defensive cooldowns |
| Hammer Divine | **AoE** | Four hammers spawn at arena edges and slam down, shifting floor tiles by one space |
| Levinforge | **AoE** | Lightning-based attacks during platform phase |

**Notes:** In Phase 2, the boss leaps to center, destroying parts of the platform and removing arena walls. Watch the hammer positions to predict which tiles will shift.

---

## Rhalgr

The Destroyer, patron deity of monks. A straightforward combat encounter with physical attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Destructive Bolt | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage |
| Bronze Work | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage requiring cooldowns |
| Rhalgr's Beacon | **AoE** | Large circular AoEs spawn across arena |
| Lightning Storm | **Spread** | Players marked with spread AoEs - spread apart |
| Hand of the Destroyer | **Cleave** | Massive frontal cleave - avoid the front of the boss |

**Notes:** Rhalgr's Emissary appears as a mini-boss before the main encounter. Focus on positioning and avoiding the frontal cleaves.

---

## Azeyma

The Warden, goddess of the sun. Features gaze mechanics and solar-themed attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Warden's Warmth | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide fire damage |
| Solar Flair | **Gaze** | Look away from the boss or be afflicted with debuffs |
| Haute Air | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster requiring cooldowns |
| Sun's Shine | **AoE** | Solar beams create line AoEs across the arena |
| Radiant Rhythm | **AoE** | Rotating AoE patterns that sweep the arena |
| Fleeting Spark | **Spread** | Players marked with spread markers |

**Notes:** Track the sun imagery for attack telegraphs. Gaze mechanics require looking away during the cast.

---

## Nald'thal

The Traders, twin deities of commerce and death. This dual-aspected boss features unique balance mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| As Above, So Below | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raid-wide that changes arena appearance - watch lantern colors |
| Golden Tenet | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |
| Heaven's Trial | **Stack + Spread** | Three cone attacks plus one stack marker - spread then stack |
| Far Above, Deep Below | **Stack** | Blue chasers plus stack marker mechanics |
| Once Above, Ever Below | **AoE** | Colored AoE circles matching lantern colors - match colors correctly |
| Hell of Fire | **Cleave** | Half-room blast - move to safe half |
| Wayward Soul | **AoE** | Falling spheres that create impact zones |
| Soul's Measure | **Adds** | Adds tethered to player groups - coordinate kills |
| Tipped Scale | **Enrage** | Players must balance arena halves to 24-24 points (players = 1 point, adds = 8 points) |

**Notes:** 
- When lanterns are **blue**, stand in the **center**
- When lanterns are **orange**, stand in the **blue circle**
- For Tipped Scale, coordinate player positioning to balance both sides of the scale
