The second floor of Omega: Deltascape featuring Catastrophe, a gravity-manipulating boss. Players receive a special Levitate action that is central to avoiding mechanics throughout the fight.

## Catastrophe

A large floating eye-creature that uses gravity-based attacks requiring mastery of the Levitate duty action.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Evilsphere | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on main tank - use cooldowns |
| Tremblor | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage - heal through |
| Gravitational Wave | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage - heal through |
| Demon Eye | **Gaze** | Damages players looking at boss - turn away when cast begins |
| Epicenter | **Raidwide** | Platform-wide AoE requiring heavy healing |
| Antilight (Purple) | **AoE** | Purple orbs deal damage on ground - use Levitate to avoid |
| Antilight (Gold) | **AoE** | Gold orbs deal damage in air - stay grounded |
| Antilight (Mixed) | **AoE** | Purple and gold orbs - move to center or safe zones |
| Earthquake | **AoE** | Ground-based attack - use Levitate to avoid |
| Aetherial Rift | **AoE** | Causes sinking - use Levitate to prevent falling |
| Gravitational Explosion | **Stack** | Stack at same elevation as marker - use Levitate if marker is in air |
| Maniacal Probe | **AoE** | Four tentacles spawn in corners - stack on the one without an eye |

**Strategy:**
- Position boss halfway toward edge so DPS/healers can stand center
- Master the timing of Levitate for purple vs gold orbs
- For Demon Eye, simply turn your character around
- During Maniacal Probe, quickly identify the eyeless tentacle and stack there

**Notes:** The key to this fight is understanding when to use Levitate. Purple orbs = float, gold orbs = stay grounded. Getting this backwards will result in heavy damage or death.
