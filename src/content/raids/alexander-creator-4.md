The fourth and final floor of Alexander: The Creator, featuring Alexander Prime - the culmination of the entire Alexander raid series. A mechanically intensive fight with heavy positioning requirements.

## Alexander Prime

The ultimate form of Alexander, requiring the full coordination of the raid to defeat.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Punishing Heat | **Tankbuster** | Heavy magic tankbuster with vulnerability debuff |
| Mega Holy | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage - shields and mitigation required |
| Blazing Scourge | **Spread** | Red markers on players - spread away from group |
| Incinerating Heat | **Stack** | Large arrow on healer - minimum 4 players stack to share |
| Gravitational Anomaly | **AoE** | Puddles spawn and grow - position to leave safe zones |
| Sacrament | **AoE** | Cross-shaped laser in cardinal directions - stand in diagonals |
| Radiant Sacrament | **AoE** | Donut AoE - only safe inside boss hitbox |
| Divine Spear | **Tankbuster** | Frontal cleave with high damage |
| Temporal Stasis | **AoE** | Time stop mechanic - resolve debuffs correctly |
| Divine Judgment | **Raidwide** | Ultimate attack - use Tank LB3 to survive |

**Key Mechanics:**

**Sacrament vs Radiant Sacrament:**
- Sacrament: Beams fire front, back, and both sides - stand in diagonal corners
- Radiant Sacrament: Donut AoE - stand inside boss hitbox

**Incinerating Heat:**
- Stack with at least 4 players on the marked healer
- Players with Blazing Scourge (red markers) move away first

**Gravitational Anomaly:**
- Puddles spawn and grow over time
- Position carefully to maintain safe zones for movement

**Divine Judgment:**
- Use Tank LB3 at 3-5 seconds on the cast timer
- This is required to survive the ultimate attack

**Strategy:**
- Tanks swap after Punishing Heat due to vulnerability debuff
- Quickly identify Sacrament type and move to correct safe zone
- Stack for Incinerating Heat, spread for Blazing Scourge
- Manage Gravitational Anomaly puddles to preserve space
- Save Tank LB3 for Divine Judgment

**Notes:** This is the finale of the Alexander story and the most complex normal mode encounter in the series. Success requires recognizing mechanics quickly and positioning correctly. The fight has been described as "a glorified game of ping pong" due to the constant movement required.
