The fourth and final floor of Eden's Gate features a reimagined Titan encounter with a unique tile-based arena. The boss alternates between Fist and Wheel forms, each with distinct mechanics.

## Titan

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Voice of the Land | **Raidwide** | Opens the fight - heavy raid-wide damage |
| Stonecrusher | **Tankbuster** | Hard-hitting tankbuster with physical vulnerability - swap after |
| Weight of the Land | **AoE** | Tiles glow and explode - move to normal tiles |
| Evil Earth | **AoE** | Tile near Titan glows - stand on nearest safe tile |
| Crumbling Down | **AoE** | Two proximity markers - run to north or south edges |
| Massive Landslide | **AoE** | Linear AoEs from Titan - dodge to safe zones |
| Geocrush | **Knockback** | Titan leaps with proximity damage - max range from landing |
| Earthen Fury | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage - requires mitigation |
| Fault Line | **AoE** | Titan charges in wheel form - avoid the path |
| Bomb Boulders | **AoE** | Boulders spawn and explode - avoid marked tiles |

### Titan's Forms

**Fist Form:** Standard attacks with predictable patterns.

**Wheel Form:** Titan rolls across the arena with charge attacks and additional mobility.

**Strategy:**
- For Weight of the Land, watch for glowing tiles and move immediately
- Stonecrusher applies vulnerability - tanks must swap to survive follow-ups
- During Crumbling Down, move to the far edges to minimize proximity damage
- In Wheel form, Titan is more mobile - be ready to dodge charge attacks
- Keep Titan centered when possible to maximize safe space

**Notes:** This encounter concludes the Eden's Gate tier. The tile mechanics are forgiving in normal mode but teach patterns needed for Savage.
