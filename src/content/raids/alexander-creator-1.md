The first floor of Alexander: The Creator, featuring an upgraded Faust miniboss and the junkyard machine Refurbisher 0. A straightforward encounter focusing on add management and positioning.

## Faust Z

An advanced war machine with double the weapons systems and defensive plating of previous Faust models.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tankbuster | **Tankbuster** | Heavy single-target damage - use cooldowns |
| Cleave | **Cleave** | Frontal cleave - face away from party |

**Notes:** Simple DPS check - burn down quickly before moving to main boss.

## Refurbisher 0

A machine designed to collect and repair damaged machina, now turned hostile.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Scrapline | **AoE** | Line AoE across the arena - avoid the marked zone |
| Full Metal Faust | **Adds** | Faust-type add spawns - pick up and burn down |
| Scrap | **AoE** | Ground AoEs spawn - avoid the marked areas |
| Stockpile | **AoE** | Boss gathers junk - increases damage if not interrupted |
| Acid Rain | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE damage - heal through |
| Scrap Burst | **AoE** | Targeted AoE on players - spread out |
| Double Scrapline | **AoE** | Two line AoEs - find safe zone between them |

**Strategy:**
- Tank boss in center, facing away from party
- Avoid Scrapline markers - watch for single or double patterns
- Kill Full Metal Faust add quickly when it spawns
- Spread for targeted AoEs
- Healers prepare for Acid Rain damage
- Find safe zones between double Scrapline patterns

**Notes:** This fight introduces the Creator tier's emphasis on positional awareness. The double Scrapline requires quick recognition of safe zones.
