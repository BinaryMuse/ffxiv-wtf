The third encounter of the Pandaemonium raid series. The Phoinix, a blazing mythic creation resembling a phoenix, tests players with fire-based attacks and add management.

## Phoinix

A fire-themed fight featuring positional cleaves, rotating AoE patterns, and add phases requiring coordinated target switching.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Scorched Exaltation | **Raidwide** | Heavy party-wide fire damage - requires shields and healing |
| Flames of Undeath | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage throughout the fight |
| Heat of Condemnation | **Tankbuster** | Double tankbuster hitting both tanks - spread apart and use defensive cooldowns |
| Experimental Fireplume (Large) | **AoE** | Large fireball drops in arena center - move to the edge to avoid |
| Experimental Fireplume (Small) | **AoE** | Ring of small fireballs spawn at cardinals and rotate - move to where previous AoEs already exploded |
| Cinderwing | **Cleave** | Half-room cleave on left or right side of boss - move to the opposite side |
| Trail of Condemnation | **AoE** | Line AoE from boss position - move to arena sides |
| Darkened Fire | **Adds** | Untargetable adds spawn that must be destroyed via Brightened Fire |
| Brightened Fire | **AoE** | Players marked 1-8 with numbered AoEs - move to Darkened Fire adds to destroy them |

### Experimental Fireplume Patterns

Two variations to watch for:

**Large Fireball (Center):**
1. Single massive fireball targets arena center
2. **Move to arena edge** to avoid the explosion

**Small Fireballs (Rotating):**
1. Multiple small fireballs spawn at opposite cardinals
2. They rotate and explode in sequence
3. **Move into where AoEs already exploded** - the safe spots follow the rotation

### Darkened Fire + Brightened Fire

This add phase requires coordination:

1. **Darkened Fire adds** spawn - they cannot be damaged normally
2. **Players get numbered markers** (1-8) with Brightened Fire
3. **Move to the adds** when your number is called
4. Each add requires **four hits** from Brightened Fire to destroy
5. Coordinate which players go to which add

### Tips

- For Cinderwing, watch which wing glows - move to the safe side
- During Heat of Condemnation, tanks must separate to avoid sharing damage
- Track the fireball rotation pattern - safe spots move predictably
- Assign groups for Darkened Fire adds before pull
- Healers prepare for Scorched Exaltation with raid cooldowns
