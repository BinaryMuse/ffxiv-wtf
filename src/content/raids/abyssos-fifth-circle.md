The first encounter of the Abyssos raid tier. Proto-Carbuncle, a twisted mythic creation resembling a massive carbuncle, introduces unique mirror reflection mechanics.

## Proto-Carbuncle

A gem-themed fight featuring a reflective wall mechanic that changes how attacks resolve. Players must understand which attacks reflect and position accordingly.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sonic Howl | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage - shield and heal |
| Crunch | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster requiring mitigation and healing |
| Ruby Glow | **Raidwide** | Party damage that creates a reflective wall splitting the arena |
| Searing Ray | **Cleave** | Boss jumps to corner and cleaves entire arena in front - get behind boss. When Ruby Glow wall is up, attack reflects off wall |
| Topaz Stones | **AoE** | Four shards drop and explode. Reflects off Ruby Glow wall for additional AoEs |
| Acidic Slaver | **Raidwide** | Knocks down all players with moderate damage - transitions to Phase 2 |
| Venom Pool | **AoE** | Growing poison puddles that do not reflect off Ruby Glow |
| Starving Stampede | **AoE** | Boss charges across arena - avoid the path |
| Devour | **AoE** | Puddles spawn at cardinals requiring assigned players to soak |

### Ruby Glow (Reflection Mechanic)

The core mechanic of this fight:

1. **Wall appears** splitting the arena in half
2. **Certain attacks reflect** off this wall
3. **Searing Ray reflects** - if boss cleaves toward wall, the attack bounces back
4. **Topaz Stones reflect** - gems create AoEs on both sides of wall
5. **Position based on reflection** - sometimes the reflected attack is the danger

### Phase Transition

After Acidic Slaver:

1. **Topaz Stones become Toxic** - they create growing poison pools
2. **Poison pools do NOT reflect** - only original placement matters
3. **Boss uses more aggressive patterns** combining multiple mechanics

### Tips

- Assign clock positions (tanks N/S, healers E/W, DPS intercardinals)
- Split into light parties for certain mechanics
- For Searing Ray, check if wall is up and position accordingly
- Watch gem landing spots and their reflection points
- During Devour, assigned players must soak cardinal puddles
- Keep mobile to avoid growing poison pools in Phase 2
