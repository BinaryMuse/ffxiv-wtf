The second encounter of the Pandaemonium raid series. Hippokampos, a mythic creation taking the form of a water serpent, must be defeated in an arena where the walls mean instant death.

## Hippokampos

A water-themed fight that introduces arena flooding mechanics and teaches players to manage knockbacks and positioning.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Murky Depths | **Raidwide** | Moderate party-wide damage - shield and heal through |
| Doubled Impact | **Tankbuster** | Shared tankbuster on MT - both tanks stack together with cooldowns |
| Sewage Deluge | **Raidwide** | Massive damage that floods the arena. Only raised platforms and drains are safe - standing in water applies Dropsy DoT |
| Shockwave | **Knockback** | Marks one platform with knockback marker. Boss leaps there and knocks players back - position to avoid being pushed into walls |
| Spoken Cataract | **Cleave** | Boss's detached head does a half-room cleave while body does a line AoE. Watch both the head and body positions |
| Dissociation | **AoE** | Boss removes its head, which appears at arena edge and dashes across. Move out of the path |
| Tainted Flood | **Spread** | Places marked AoEs on players - spread apart to avoid overlapping |
| Predatory Sight | **Stack** | All players marked with triangle stack markers - must stack with at least one other player or take heavy damage |

### Arena Flooding (Sewage Deluge)

The signature mechanic of this fight:

1. **Massive raidwide damage** hits the party
2. **Arena floods** - standing in water applies Dropsy DoT
3. **Only safe spots**: Raised platforms and drain grates
4. **Position early** to avoid scrambling during the flood phase

### Dissociation + Cataract

This combination requires watching multiple elements:

1. **Head detaches** and appears at arena edge
2. **Watch head facing** - it will dash across that direction
3. **Body performs cataract** - avoid the line AoE
4. Find the safe zone that avoids both the head's dash path and the body's attack

### Tips

- The arena walls instantly kill - never get knocked into them
- For Doubled Impact, both tanks must stack and use cooldowns
- During Predatory Sight, pair up quickly - being alone means heavy damage
- Watch the boss's head position during Dissociation for the dash direction
- Healers prepare heavy healing for Sewage Deluge as it hits hard
