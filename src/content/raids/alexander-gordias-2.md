The second floor of Alexander: Gordias, a unique gauntlet-style encounter featuring 8 waves of trash enemies rather than a traditional boss fight. Requires careful add management and use of Magitek Gobwalker suits.

## Gordian Adds

A multi-wave add encounter testing coordination and kill priority.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Magitek Gobwalker Suits | **Adds** | Two DPS pilot suits to drag bombs and debuff enemies |
| Boomtype Magitek Gobwalker | **Adds** | Top priority - applies Vulnerability Up |
| Jagd Doll | **Adds** | Second priority - hits extremely hard |
| Gordian Hardhelm | **Adds** | Third priority - reduces healing, buffs physical vulnerability |
| Gordian Hardmind | **Adds** | Third priority - reduces healing, buffs magic vulnerability |
| Gordian Soldiers | **Adds** | Lower priority trash |
| Gordian Snipers | **Adds** | Lower priority ranged adds |
| Magitek Gobwidow | **Adds** | Large add, lower priority |
| Bomb Mechanic | **AoE** | Split party to manage fire resistance down debuffs |

**Kill Priority:**
1. Boomtype Magitek Gobwalker (vulnerability debuff)
2. Jagd Doll (high damage)
3. Gordian Hardhelm/Hardmind (healing reduction)
4. Gordian Soldiers
5. Gordian Snipers
6. Magitek Gobwidow

**Strategy:**
- Two DPS enter the Magitek Gobwalker G-VII suits at the start
- Suit pilots drag bombs away from the party and use debuff abilities on enemies
- Do NOT exit the suit or it's lost for the rest of the fight
- Use suit abilities sparingly to conserve energy
- Recharge the suit using its Recharge ability on low-health enemies
- When bombs appear, split the party so one side absorbs while the other stays safe
- Tanks must use cooldowns carefully as trash hits hard
- Kill enemies quickly to control wave spawns rather than waiting for timers

**Notes:** This is the most unique encounter in Alexander, functioning as an add gauntlet rather than a boss fight. Success depends on proper use of the Gobwalker suits and following the kill priority.
