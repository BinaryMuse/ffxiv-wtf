The third floor of Omega: Deltascape featuring Halicarnassus, a queen from Final Fantasy V who uses spellblade attacks and transformation magic.

## Halicarnassus

A spell-casting boss with transformation mechanics and tile-based puzzles.

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spellblade Holy | **Stack** | Stack on marked player to share damage |
| Holy Edge | **Spread** | Targeted players spread away from Holy stack |
| Ribbit | **Cleave** | Green frontal cone - turns players into frogs |
| Spellblade Thunder III | **AoE** | Long column AoE - dodge sideways; applies Paralysis |
| Spellblade Blizzard III | **AoE** | Point blank AoE around boss - melee move out |
| Spellblade Fire III | **AoE** | Large donut AoE - stay in melee range or max range |
| The Playing Field (Roles) | **AoE** | Stand on tiles matching your role (Tank/Healer/DPS) |
| The Playing Field (Frogs) | **AoE** | Players turned into frogs must stand on frog tiles |
| Place Dark Token | **Adds** | Spawns Soul Reapers or Great Dragon adds |
| The Queen's Waltz (Swords) | **AoE** | Thin cone AoEs on everyone - stack to bait, then move out |

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Panel Swap | **AoE** | Floor changes to blue/black tiles - stand on blue tiles (safe zones) |
| Mindjack | **AoE** | Stack together and face the boss to avoid following stack mechanic |
| Aetherial Tear | **AoE** | Navigate through sands to find the exit portal |

**Strategy:**
- Pay attention to tile colors and stand on correct role/frog tiles
- For Spellblade attacks: Thunder = dodge column, Blizzard = move out, Fire = stay close or far
- During Queen's Waltz, group together to aim cones in one direction, then move
- Kill adds quickly when they spawn

**Notes:** The tile mechanics can be confusing initially. Watch for visual indicators and remember your role tiles. If you get turned into a frog, quickly find frog tiles.
