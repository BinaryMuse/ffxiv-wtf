The third encounter of the Anabaseios raid tier. Themis, known as "Ephemeral Justice," wields both light and darkness, which determine how his attacks function.

## Themis

A duality-themed fight where the boss gathers light or darkness in his staff, changing how mechanics resolve. Features debuff-based positioning and expanding/contracting AoE patterns.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Eunomia | **Raidwide** | Moderate raidwide with bleed DoT - requires mitigation and sustained healing |
| Dike | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster requiring cooldowns |
| Divisive Ruling | **AoE** | Line AoE through boss - behavior changes based on light or dark |
| Dark and Light | **AoE** | Two circles appear (east/west). Players get light or dark debuffs - stand in opposite-colored circle |
| Sigils of Discord | **AoE** | Applies debuffs matching the puddle colors |
| Dineis | **Stack** | Targeted ground AoEs on players requiring stacking and dodging together |
| Emissary's Will | **Enrage** | Instantly kills players not standing in correct circles |
| Upheld Ruling | **Cleave** | Conal attacks based on light/dark charge |

### Light vs Dark (Core Mechanic)

Watch the boss's staff to determine attack patterns:

**Darkness Charge:**
- Divisive Ruling line AoE **pulses outward**
- Move **inside immediately** after the initial explosion

**Light Charge:**
- Divisive Ruling line AoE **expands outward**
- Move **left or right** to dodge the expanding line

### Dark and Light (Circle Mechanic)

Critical positioning mechanic:

1. **Two circles appear** on east and west sides
2. **Players receive debuffs** - either light or dark
3. **Stand in opposite color** - light debuff players go to dark circle, vice versa
4. **Wrong circle = damage + vulnerability**
5. **Emissary's Will**: Anyone outside correct circle dies instantly
6. Circles are removed after Emissary's Will resolves

### Fight Flow

The encounter follows a pattern:

1. Opens with Eunomia (raidwide + bleed)
2. Three sets of Divisive Ruling (alternating light and dark)
3. Another Eunomia
4. Dark and Light circle mechanics begin
5. Pattern repeats with increasing complexity

### Tips

- Watch the staff glow to identify light or dark charge
- For dark Divisive Ruling: move in after explosion
- For light Divisive Ruling: move sideways to avoid expansion
- Track your debuff color and go to opposite circle
- Healers prepare for Eunomia's bleed DoT with sustained healing
- Emissary's Will is fatal if positioned wrong - prioritize correct placement
