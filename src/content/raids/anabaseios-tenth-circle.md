The second encounter of the Anabaseios raid tier. You face Pandaemonium itself, a twisted being formed from massive stones and gates, in the Daemon's Nest.

## Pandaemonium

A mechanically dense fight featuring web mechanics, wing attacks, and coordinated stack/spread patterns. Requires careful positioning and partner coordination.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ultima | **Raidwide** | Heavy party-wide damage - requires shields and healing |
| Soul Grasp | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster attack requiring cooldowns |
| Dividing Wings | **Cleave** | Two winged rods tether to one DPS and one support, firing wide conal AoEs |
| Steel Web | **Stack** | Stack markers on players that web them together - tethered players must break webs |
| Silkspit | **Spread** | Web AoEs that tether to players and posts - spread to prevent tethers forming |
| Pandaemoniac Pillars | **AoE** | Pillars spawn requiring tower soaks |
| Pandaemoniac Turrets | **AoE** | Turrets fire in patterns - avoid line of fire |
| Binding Soul Snare | **AoE** | Debuff from webs - reaching 5 stacks causes death |

### Dividing Wings + Steel Web

A complex combination mechanic:

1. **Dividing Wings**: Two random players (1 DPS, 1 support) get tethered for wide conal AoEs
2. **Steel Web**: Two other random players (1 DPS, 1 support) get stack markers
3. **Stack groups form**: Players without Dividing Wings tethers stack with marked players (4 per stack)
4. **Webs form**: Stacked players get webbed together with Binding Soul Snare debuff
5. **Break webs**: The Dividing Wings players must run through the web tethers to free trapped allies
6. **Avoid 5 stacks**: Reaching 5 stacks of Binding Soul Snare causes death

### Silkspit (Spread Mechanic)

Requires pre-assigned positions:

1. **Web AoEs spawn** that can tether to players and arena posts
2. **Spread apart** to prevent tethers from forming
3. If tethered when ability resolves, gain **Binding Soul Snare** (immobilizes, kills at 5 stacks)
4. Assign spread positions before fight begins

### Tips

- Pre-assign Silkspit spread positions before pull
- Assign tower positions for Pandaemoniac Pillars
- For Steel Web, non-tethered players stack with marked players
- Dividing Wings players break webs by running through them
- Never reach 5 stacks of Binding Soul Snare
- Healers prepare heavy healing for Ultima casts
- Communication helps for web-breaking coordination
