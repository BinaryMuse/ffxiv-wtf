### Mirror Mechanics

**Mirror Colors determine reflection timing:**
- **Blue mirrors:** Reflect immediately
- **Green mirrors:** Reflect with medium delay
- **Red mirrors:** Reflect with long delay

Watch which attacks are cast and which mirrors are active to predict where reflections will hit.

**Strategy:**
- For Diamond Frost, marked players spread to assigned waymarks; others stack center
- Watch Shiva's weapon for Driving/Biting Frost tells
- Mirror mechanics require tracking both the original cast and reflection timing
- During Shining Armor, look away immediately
- For Axe Kick stay in, for Scythe Kick stay out

**Notes:** This encounter concludes the Eden's Verse tier. The mirror mechanic adds layers of complexity that distinguish this fight from other primal encounters.
