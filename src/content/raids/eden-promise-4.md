The fourth and final floor of Eden's Promise serves as the climax of the Shadowbringers raid series. The boss is a fusion of Mitron and Gaia, incorporating mechanics from previous primal encounters.

## Eden's Promise

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Maleficium | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage with primal variations |
| Formless Judgment | **Tankbuster** | Targets both tanks simultaneously |
| Junction | **AoE** | Tethers to a primal - modifies next Maleficium |
| Stock | **AoE** | Stores primal energy for later use |
| Release | **AoE** | Releases stored primal energy |
| Cast | **AoE** | Immediate primal attack |
| Diamond Dust | **AoE** | Ice phase mechanics |
| Rapturous Reach | **Cleave** | Wide cleaving attack |
| Paradise Lost | **Raidwide** | Heavy damage requiring mitigation |
| Classical Sculpture | **Adds** | Sculpture adds spawn with various patterns |

### Primal Junction Mechanics

**Maleficium changes based on tethered primal:**

| Primal | Effect | Safe Position |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| **Leviathan** | Side AoEs | Stay in middle lane |
| **Garuda** | Four narrow cones | Avoid cone paths |
| **Ramuh** | Circle AoE around boss | Move away from boss |
| **Ifrit** | Large front/back cones | Move to boss's sides |

### Add Phases

**Lissom Sculptures (Set 1):**
- Four sculptures spawn
- Conal AoEs and straight-line attacks
- Dodge based on sculpture facing

**Chiseled Sculptures (Set 2):**
- Two sculptures with knockback AoE
- Shareable AoE on both healers
- Split into two parties for healer stacks

**Beastly Sculptures (Set 3):**
- Six sculptures spawn
- Various AoE patterns
- Focus damage and dodge accordingly

**Strategy:**
- Track which primal is tethered to predict Maleficium variation
- For Leviathan: center lane safe
- For Garuda: avoid the four cone directions
- For Ramuh: max melee range or further
- For Ifrit: stand at boss's left or right
- During add phases, prioritize dodging while burning adds
- For Chiseled Sculpture healer stacks, split into light parties

**Notes:** This encounter brings together mechanics from the entire Eden storyline, testing your mastery of primal patterns from previous tiers.
