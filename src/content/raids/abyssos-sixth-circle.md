The second encounter of the Abyssos raid tier. Hegemone, a hemitheos creature, fights in an arena divided into sixteen squares that serve as positioning guides for her complex mechanics.

## Hegemone

A technical fight featuring grid-based positioning, infection mechanics, and conal attacks that require careful observation of the boss's orientation.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hemitheos's Dark IV | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage - shield and heal |
| Synergy | **Tankbuster** | Individual tankbusters on both tanks - both need cooldowns |
| Aetheric Polyominoid | **AoE** | Spikes at intercardinals shoot AoEs toward certain squares - avoid marked squares |
| Choros Ixou | **Cleave** | Boss's hands light up in cardinal or intercardinal directions - conal AoEs in those directions |
| Transmission | **Spread** | Infects four players with Glossomorph debuff. When it expires, players freeze and fire a cone in their facing direction |
| Dark Ashes | **Spread** | Places AoE markers on all party members - spread apart |
| Strophe Ixou | **Cleave** | Conal AoEs from front and back of boss that rotate in indicated directions |
| Pathogenic Cells | **AoE** | Grid squares become dangerous - watch for telegraph patterns |

### Aetheric Polyominoid (Grid Mechanic)

The core positioning mechanic:

1. **Spikes appear** at intercardinal positions
2. **Lines indicate** which squares will be hit
3. **Later variant (Aetheric Sigma)**: Staves swap impact zones with tethered staves right before resolution
4. **Watch the telegraphs** and stand on safe squares

### Transmission (Infection Mechanic)

A two-part mechanic requiring coordination:

1. **First group** (4 players) gets Glossomorph debuff
2. When debuff expires, players **freeze in place** and fire a conal AoE in their facing direction
3. **Turn away from party** to avoid cleaving allies
4. **Second group** gets infected as first resolves
5. Both groups must point their cones safely

### Choros Ixou (Hand Mechanic)

Watch the boss's hands:

1. **Hands glow** toward cardinal or intercardinal directions
2. **Conal AoEs fire** in those directions
3. Check the boss's **target circle orientation** to determine safe spots
4. Move to positions not covered by the hand indicators

### Tips

- Use the 16-square grid for precise positioning
- For Transmission, face outward or toward walls when frozen
- Track hand positions for Choros Ixou safe spots
- Tanks should spread for individual tankbusters
- Watch for staff tethers that swap AoE positions
- The grid layout makes positioning predictable once learned
