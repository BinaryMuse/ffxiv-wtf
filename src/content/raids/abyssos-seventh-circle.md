The third encounter of the Abyssos raid tier. Agdistis, a hemitheos taking the form of a massive tree creature, transforms the arena during the fight and summons various adds.

## Agdistis

A nature-themed fight featuring arena transformation, add management, and exaflare-style dodging. The circular arena becomes three raised platforms connected by bridges.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spark of Life | **Raidwide** | Heavy raidwide damage that inflicts an 8-second bleed DoT - requires heavy healing |
| Hemitheos's Aero III | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster attacks on both tanks - use defensive cooldowns |
| Hemitheos's Holy | **Spread** | Places AoE markers on all players - spread to avoid overlapping |
| Hemitheos's Glare III | **AoE** | Donut AoE in the center of the arena - move out of center |
| Bough of Attis | **Cleave** | Boss slams hands. If pulled back, slams front with line AoEs. If brought forward, slams back |
| Immortal's Obol | **AoE** | Proximity marker that transforms arena into three platforms with bridges |
| Forbidden Fruit | **Adds** | Summons untargetable adds on each platform with different attack patterns |
| Blades of Attis | **AoE** | Exaflare-style AoEs moving across arena - dodge and reposition into origin points |

### Bough of Attis (Hand Slam)

Watch the boss's arm position:

1. **Arms pulled back**: Boss will slam the front half, creating line AoEs in indicated directions - move behind
2. **Arms forward**: Boss will slam the back half - move to the front
3. Watch for **directional indicators** showing where line AoEs will travel

### Immortal's Obol (Arena Transformation)

The arena changes dramatically:

1. **Proximity marker appears** - move away from center
2. **Arena transforms** into three raised platforms with connecting bridges
3. **Falling off means death** - stay on platforms and bridges
4. Fight continues on this new layout until arena restores

### Forbidden Fruit (Add Patterns)

Adds spawn on platforms with specific attacks:

- **Immature Ios (Rams)**: Perform circle AoEs centered on their platform - move away
- **Immature Stymphalides (Birds)**: Perform line AoEs in their facing direction - avoid their front

### Blades of Attis (Exaflares)

Moving AoE pattern:

1. **AoEs spawn** and begin moving across the arena
2. **Dodge the initial hits** then move into where they started
3. Continue repositioning as the exaflares travel across platforms

### Tips

- Position for Bough of Attis based on arm animation
- During platform phase, stay aware of edges - falling is death
- Track add types and their attack patterns
- For exaflares, move into the origin points after first explosions
- Healers prepare for Spark of Life's heavy damage plus bleed
- Use bridges carefully during platform phase mechanics
