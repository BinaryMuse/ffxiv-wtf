The fourth and final encounter of the Asphodelos raid tier. Hesperos, the keyward who has become a hemitheos (an amalgamation of mythic beast and man), guards the secrets of Pandaemonium.

## Hesperos

A theatrical fight featuring elemental stage mechanics where the boss sets different "scenes" that determine upcoming attacks. The arena is surrounded by deadly spikes.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Elegant Evisceration | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster with red arrow indicator - tank must use cooldowns |
| Decollation | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage - shield and heal |
| Setting the Stage | **AoE** | Curtains drop on arena quadrants, then reveal elemental zones for the next attack |
| Pinax | **AoE** | Triggers the revealed elemental quadrants with various effects |
| Bloodrake | **Raidwide** | Drains HP from all players |
| Shifting Strike | **Knockback** | Boss dashes and knocks back - position to avoid spike walls |
| Director's Belone | **Spread** | Places AoE markers on players - spread to avoid overlapping |
| Inversive Chlamys | **AoE** | Cape attack that hits areas around the boss |

### Setting the Stage (Elemental Quadrants)

The signature mechanic of this fight:

1. **Curtains drop** on one, three, or four arena quadrants
2. Curtains glow red then disappear, revealing the element:
   - **Fire** - Large explosion in that quadrant
   - **Water** - Knockback from that quadrant
   - **Lightning** - AoEs spread across that quadrant  
   - **Poison** - Leaves damaging puddles

3. **Watch the reveals** and position in safe quadrants before Pinax resolves

### Pinax Combinations

Hesperos often combines multiple quadrant effects:

- **Single quadrant**: Simply avoid that area
- **Multiple quadrants**: Find the one safe zone
- **With knockback**: Position so knockback pushes you to safety, not into spikes

### Tips

- The arena border spikes instantly kill - never get knocked into them
- For Elegant Evisceration, watch for the red arrow indicator on tank
- Track which quadrants are revealed during Setting the Stage
- Position for knockbacks to push you toward center, not edges
- Stay mobile and ready to adjust based on elemental reveals
- Healers watch for Bloodrake into Decollation combinations
