The second fight of the Arcadion raid series. Honey B. Lovely is a bee-themed fighter with charm mechanics.

## Honey B. Lovely

The signature mechanic is the heart gauge—players receive three empty hearts after Honey B. Live, and getting hit fills one heart. Three filled hearts triggers Fatal Attraction (stun + heavy DoT).

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Call Me Honey | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Honey B. Live | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage that activates the heart gauge system |
| Honeyed Breeze | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster; both tanks must spread |
| Tempting Twist | **AoE** | Donut AoE; stand close to boss |
| Honey Beeline | **Cleave** | Front/back line AoE |
| Love Me Tender | **Spread** | Spread markers plus tower soaks; getting hit fills hearts |
| Loveseeker | **AoE** | Slow heart projectiles from boss; dodge carefully |
| Blow Kiss | **Cleave** | Untelegraphed frontal cone; stay at sides or behind |
| Splash of Venom | **AoE** | Poison blobs that explode into circle AoEs |
| Drop of Venom | **AoE** | Additional poison blob AoEs |
| Alarm Pheromones | **Adds** | Summons Groupbees on arena edge |
| Honey B. Finale | **AoE** | Rapid-fire mechanics requiring quick reactions |

### Heart Gauge System

1. **Three empty hearts** appear after Honey B. Live
2. **Getting hit** by most attacks fills one heart
3. **Three filled hearts** = Fatal Attraction (stun + lethal DoT)
4. **Strategy** = Avoid unnecessary hits, manage heart stacks carefully

### Tips

- Only one player needs to soak each tower during Love Me Tender to minimize heart fills
- Prioritize avoiding mechanics over DPS if hearts are stacking
- Stay out of frontal cone range for Blow Kiss as it has no telegraph
- Watch for slow-moving heart projectiles from Loveseeker
- Coordinate with party to ensure towers are covered without overlap
