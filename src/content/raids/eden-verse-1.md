The first floor of Eden's Verse features the primal Ramuh in a reimagined encounter. The fight introduces surge orb mechanics and requires careful attention to buff management.

## Ramuh

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Crippling Blow | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster - use defensive cooldowns |
| Judgment Volts | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage - heal and mitigate |
| Stratospear Summons | **AoE** | Large and small spears appear - stand near small spears to avoid lightning from large ones |
| Judgment Jolt | **AoE** | Lightning strikes from large spears - position accordingly |
| Stormcloud | **Spread** | Marked players must separate and drop clouds away from raid |
| Surge Orbs | **AoE** | Collect up to 3 orbs for protection buff - don't take more than 3 |
| Fury's Bolt | **Raidwide** | Buffs Ramuh's next attack - orbs surround him as indicator |
| Divine Judgment Volts | **Raidwide** | Massive damage - requires surge orb buff or players will die |
| Stepped Leader | **AoE** | Lightning AoE patterns - dodge to safe zones |
| Centaur's Charge | **Cleave** | Ramuh charges in a line - avoid his path |
| Chain Lightning | **Stack** | Stack marker with chain damage - group up together |

**Strategy:**
- When Stratospear Summons occurs, move to the small spears for safety
- Collect exactly 3 orbs before Divine Judgment Volts - more will kill you
- Watch for Fury's Bolt indicator (orbs around Ramuh) to know when his next attack is buffed
- For Stormcloud, spread out and drop markers at arena edges
- The arena has lane patterns on the floor to help with positioning

**Notes:** Players can fall off the arena edge but will be raised after a short delay. The surge orb mechanic is the key to surviving this fight.
