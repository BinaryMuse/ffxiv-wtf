The third floor of Omega: Alphascape, facing Omega directly in its combat form. After proving supremacy over the recreated Midgardsormr, you challenge Omega itself.

## Omega

The mechanical entity uses precise directional attacks and devastating lasers.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Atomic Ray | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage at fight start - mitigate and heal |
| Mustard Bomb | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster with fire debuff |
| Starboard Wave Cannon | **AoE** | Right side of Omega unsafe - move left |
| Larboard Wave Cannon | **AoE** | Left side of Omega unsafe - move right |
| Wave Cannon | **AoE** | Frontal line AoE - avoid boss's front |
| Flamethrower | **AoE** | Rotating cone AoE - move with or against rotation |

### Starboard/Larboard Mechanic

Omega casts Starboard or Larboard twice in succession:

| Pattern | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Same skill twice | Switch sides after first hit |
| Different skills | Stay on same side |

After each cast, Omega turns 180°, so track the boss's facing.

### Pantokrator

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Pantokrator | **AoE** | Complex phase with ground AoEs, stacks, and flame positions |
| Ground AoEs | **AoE** | Bait south, then dodge clockwise around boss |
| Line Stack | **Stack** | Stack north after 4 ground AoEs |
| Flame Spots | **AoE** | Take assigned positions based on cardinal/diagonal |
| Proximity AoE | **Knockback** | Sprint to corner for proximity damage at end |

**Strategy:**
- For Starboard, move left; for Larboard, move right
- Track Omega's facing - it turns 180° after each directional attack
- If same skill twice, switch sides; if different, stay put
- During Pantokrator, follow the dodge pattern clockwise and take assigned positions
- Use heavy mitigation during Atomic Ray and Pantokrator proximity phases

**Notes:** The Starboard/Larboard mechanic is simple in concept but easy to mess up in practice. The AoE covers slightly more than 180°, so position clearly to one side.
