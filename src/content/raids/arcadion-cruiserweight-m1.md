The first fight of the AAC Cruiserweight raid tier in Dawntrail. Dancing Green is a shameless playboy from Solution Nine who wields the soul of a terrible toad and fights using dance-themed mechanics on a disco stage.

## Dancing Green

The arena is a disco stage with glowing square panels and a bleed wall outside the arena.

### Phase One Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Celebrate Good Times | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magical damage |
| Deep Cut | **Tankbuster** | Conal magical tank buster on both tanks |
| Do the Hustle | **Cleave** | Dancing Green points to one side of the arena, cleaving that side |
| X-snap Twist | **Cleave** | Half-room cleaves equal to the number in the ability name on indicated side, then hits the opposite side |
| Funky Floor | **AoE** | Checkerboard pattern AoE on floor tiles; tiles swap between each instance |
| Full Beat | **Stack** | Stack marker on a healer |
| Disco Infernal | **Raidwide + Debuff** | Raidwide damage plus Burn Baby Burn debuffs; players must be inside moving spotlights when debuff expires |
| Ride the Waves | **AoE** | Sets of three AoE lines travel from the north; find and stand in safe lines |

### Phase Two Mechanics

Dancing Green jumps to the north side and becomes omni-directional.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ensemble Assemble | **Adds** | Summons eight Frogtourage backup dancers at the front of arena |
| Arcady Night Fever | **Cleave** | Eight backup dancers point towards directions; Dancing Green performs half-room cleaves in those directions with Let's Dance |
| Let's Pose | **Raidwide** | Raidwide magical damage; players not hit by Let's Dance receive a damage buff |
| Frogtourage | **AoE** | Backup dancers slide across the arena creating AoEs |
| Eighth Beats | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on all players |

### Tips

- Count the half-room cleaves correctly before moving to the other side for 2-snap and 4-snap Twist
- Follow the spotlights during Disco Infernal—be inside one when your debuff expires
- For perfect execution of Arcady Night Fever, dodge all eight half-room cleaves to receive Perfect Groove buff
- In later phases, Funky Floor combines with X-snap Twist—stay on safe tiles close to middle on the safe cleave side
