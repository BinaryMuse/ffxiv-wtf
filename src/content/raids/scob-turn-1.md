The first turn of the Second Coil pits adventurers against Rafflesia, a massive carnivorous plant with complex phase-based mechanics involving slimes and briars.

## Rafflesia

A two-phase fight with overlapping mechanics that require precise coordination.

### Phase 1 (100% - 70%)

Rotation: Thorn Whip → Bulbs → Thorn Whip → Devour/Spit → Repeat

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Bloody Caress | **Tankbuster** | Tank swap mechanic - switch tanks at 3 stacks of Vulnerability |
| Thorn Whip | **Spread** | Tethers two or more players - break tethers by moving apart |
| Dark Matter Bulb | **Adds** | Spawns in one of eight locations, creates briar patches |
| Devour/Spit | **AoE** | Suction cone targeting yellow-marked player |
| Blighted Bouquet | **Raidwide** | Taking ANY action near cast end kills you - stop all actions |

### Thorn Whip Strategy

- Players get tethered together
- Stack close initially to make breaking easier
- Then move apart to break the tethers

### Devour/Spit Strategy

- Yellow marker appears on a player
- Don't stand between the marked player and Rafflesia
- Being pulled in grants the boss permanent damage up
- Use briar patches to block the suction

### Phase 2 (70% - 40%)

Rotation: Bulbs → Thorn Whip → Devour/Spit → Honey → Repeat

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Honey/Dark Matter Bee | **Adds** | One player becomes "Honey Glazed" attracting bees |
| Briar Priority | **AoE** | Only NEWEST bulb briars can block Devour - old briars don't work |

**Honey Glazed Strategy:**
- The debuffed player attracts all bees
- Bees attack only the Honey Glazed player until they die
- If the player dies, Honey reapplies to someone else
- Healers must keep the Honey Glazed player alive

**Notes:**
- In Phase 2, you can only use briars from the newest bulbs for Devour
- Old briars from Phase 1 or earlier Phase 2 won't block the mechanic
- Coordinate briar placement carefully
- Tank swap at 3 stacks throughout the fight
