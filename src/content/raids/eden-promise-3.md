The third floor of Eden's Promise features Fatebreaker, an amalgam of Thancred and Ran'jit. The fight uses elemental energy colors to determine mechanics variations.

## Fatebreaker

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Powder Mark | **Tankbuster** | Marked tank buster with follow-up explosion |
| Burnished Glory | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage |
| Burnt Strike | **AoE** | Linear attack - effect changes based on energy color |
| Burnt Strike (Fire) | **Knockback** | Massive knockback - stand close to center of AoE |
| Burnt Strike (Lightning) | **AoE** | AoE expands 50% wider - stand further away |
| Burnt Strike (Light) | **Spread** | Individual AoEs on all players - spread out |
| Turn of the Heavens | **AoE** | Nine halos appear - matching-color halos explode into AoEs |
| Prismatic Deception | **AoE** | Three AoEs around arena - run outside to find safe spots |
| Bound of Faith | **Stack** | Stack marker with additional mechanics |
| Shifting Sky | **AoE** | Arena-wide effect change |
| Floating Fetters | **AoE** | Chains requiring movement |
| Sundered Sky | **AoE** | Follow-up to sky mechanics |

### Energy Color Mechanics

**Fatebreaker's Energy Color** determines attack variations:

| Color | Burnt Strike Effect | Turn of the Heavens |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Fire** (Orange) | Arena-wide knockback - hug center | Fire halos explode |
| **Lightning** (Blue) | Expands 50% - stay far | Lightning halos explode |
| **Light** (White) | Spread AoEs on all players | Light halos explode |

**Turn of the Heavens:**
- Nine halos spawn around arena edge
- All deal damage underneath
- Only halos matching Fatebreaker's current energy color explode into AoEs
- Avoid the matching-color halos specifically

**Strategy:**
- Always watch Fatebreaker's energy color before Burnt Strike
- For Fire: Stand as close as possible to AoE center
- For Lightning: Stand further away from the AoE
- For Light: Spread immediately when circles appear
- During Turn of the Heavens, identify which halos match current color

**Notes:** This fight is more straightforward than previous Eden encounters once you understand the color system. The visual tells are clear and consistent.
