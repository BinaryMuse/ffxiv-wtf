The second turn of the Second Coil features Melusine, a lamia boss with petrification mechanics and multiple add phases.

## Melusine

A fight with heavy emphasis on gaze mechanics and add management across multiple phases.

### Core Mechanics (All Phases)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cursed Voice | **Gaze** | Three random players get debuff - frontal cone of petrification when it expires |
| Cursed Shriek | **Gaze** | One player gets debuff - petrifies everyone in their line of sight |
| Circle Blade | **AoE** | Circular AoE from Melusine - move away when cast begins |

### Cursed Voice Strategy

- Three players receive the debuff
- When it expires, a frontal cone of white smog fires
- Move to arena edge and face away from raid
- Stay positioned until debuff wears off

### Cursed Shriek Strategy

- One player receives the debuff
- They will petrify everyone who can see them
- Move to the back of the arena near the Renauds
- Position outside hitboxes and away from other players' line of sight

### Phase Breakdown

**Phase 1 (100% - 80%)**
- Attack Melusine directly
- Handle Cursed Voice and Cursed Shriek mechanics

**Phase 2 (~80%)**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lamia DeathDancers x3 | **Adds** | Three adds spawn sequentially - use cooldowns on second one |
| Circle Blade | **AoE** | DeathDancers also use this ability |

**Phase 3 (~60%)**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lamia Fatedealers x4 | **Adds** | Spawn in corners - focus them down quickly |
| Purple AoEs | **AoE** | Ground circles appear - avoid standing in them |

**Phase 4 (~35%)**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lamia Prosecutor | **Adds** | Spawns with Petrification ability |
| Petrification | **Gaze** | 3-second cast - look away during cast or be petrified |
| Inherited Petrification | **Gaze** | Melusine gains Petrification after Prosecutor dies |

**Final Phase Strategy:**
- Let the first Petrification cast finish
- Use Limit Break to quickly defeat the Prosecutor
- After Prosecutor dies, Melusine gains Petrification
- Look away during her Petrification casts and burn her down

**Notes:**
- Gaze mechanics are the primary wipe cause - always watch debuffs
- Resume attacking Circle Blade once the visual circle disappears (delayed animation)
- Coordinate add priorities across phases
