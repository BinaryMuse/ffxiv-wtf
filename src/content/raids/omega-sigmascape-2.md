The second floor of Omega: Sigmascape, featuring Demon Chadarnook from Final Fantasy VI. A unique encounter using painting mechanics and a special Paint duty action.

## Demon Chadarnook

A demon that possesses paintings, requiring players to use canvas mechanics to counter attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Demonic Shear | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster - use cooldowns |
| Demonic Howl | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage - heal through |
| Flash Gale | **Stack** | Stack marker on one player - group together |
| Prey | **AoE** | Circular AoEs on marked players - avoid overlapping |
| Demonic Pain | **AoE** | Pink tether - run away until it breaks |
| Possession (Fire) | **AoE** | Boss possesses fire painting - all paint water canvas for Fire Damage Down |
| Possession (Earth) | **AoE** | Boss possesses earth painting - all paint airship canvas to mount and avoid earthquake |
| Possession (Water) | **AoE** | Boss possesses water painting - one player paints boulder; others hide behind it |
| Possession (Wind) | **Adds** | Boss possesses wind painting - one player paints mount to knockback Easterly adds |

**Paint Mechanic:**
- Stand on colored canvas puddles to pick up paint color
- Use the Paint duty action on corresponding canvases to activate defenses
- Cannot use Paint while mounted - apply buffs before mounting

**Strategy:**
- Learn which painting corresponds to which canvas response
- Fire painting = water canvas (buff)
- Earth painting = airship canvas (mount to avoid ground)
- Water painting = boulder canvas (line of sight protection)
- Wind painting = mount canvas (knockback adds)
- Coordinate with team on who paints what

**Notes:** The painting possession mechanic is the core of this fight. Pay attention to which painting glows and quickly paint the correct response canvas. Communication helps assign who handles boulder/mount duties.
