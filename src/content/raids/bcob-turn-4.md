Turn 4 is a DPS-intensive raid taking place on a descending lift with six phases, each lasting approximately one minute. Failing to clear a phase in time causes the next phase to begin on top of it.

## General Strategy

**Optimal Composition:** 2 Tanks, 2 Healers, 2 Magical DPS, 2 Physical DPS

Different enemy types have resistances:
- **Knights** have Stoneskin (reflects magical damage) - kill with physical DPS
- **Soldiers** absorb physical damage - kill with magical DPS
- **Dreadnoughts** deal high frontal damage - never stand in front

## Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clockwork Bugs x6 | **Adds** | Six bugs spawn - tanks round them up, use AoE damage |

**Notes:** Physical DPS should conserve TP to allow mages to deal bulk damage.

## Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clockwork Soldiers x2 | **Adds** | Absorb physical damage - magical DPS priority |
| Clockwork Knights x2 | **Adds** | Have Stoneskin reflecting magic - physical DPS priority |

**Notes:** Each tank takes one Soldier and one Knight. Coordinate DPS accordingly.

## Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clockwork Dreadnought | **Adds** | High priority target - heals by consuming bugs |
| Clockwork Bugs x4 | **Adds** | Secondary priority - Dreadnought feeds on them |

**Notes:** Kill the Dreadnought before Phase 4 begins or it may heal to full. Never stand in front of Dreadnoughts.

## Phase 4

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clockwork Bugs x6 | **Adds** | Similar to Phase 1 |
| Spinner-rooks x2 | **Adds** | Reduce maximum HP of players |

**Notes:** Use heavy AoE damage. Black Mages should save Convert for Double Flare with Quelling Strikes.

## Phase 5

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Clockwork Soldier | **Adds** | Magical DPS priority |
| Clockwork Knight | **Adds** | Physical DPS priority |
| Clockwork Dreadnought | **Adds** | High priority - handle frontal cleave |

**Notes:** Combines earlier phase mechanics. Coordinate tank positioning and DPS targeting.

## Phase 6

Final phase with the remaining adds. Clear everything before the lift reaches the bottom.

**Notes:**
- This fight is a pure DPS check with no traditional boss
- Communication between physical and magical DPS is critical
- If behind on DPS, use Limit Break on Dreadnoughts
