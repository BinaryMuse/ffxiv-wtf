The first floor of Omega: Alphascape, featuring Chaos from the original Final Fantasy. The mightiest entity from Omega's test world uses elemental attacks in a structured rotation pattern.

## Chaos

An ancient entity that cycles through Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind phases with devastating combination attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Chaotic Dispersion | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster - use cooldowns |
| Bowels of Agony | **Raidwide** | Reduces all HP to 1 and applies debuff - healers heal immediately |
| Damning Edict | **Knockback** | Knockback damage - stand behind the boss to avoid |
| Longitudinal Implosion | **AoE** | Two conal AoEs - hits front/back first, then sides |
| Latitudinal Implosion | **AoE** | Two conal AoEs - hits sides first, then front/back |
| Cyclone | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide AoE with debuff - heal through |

### Fire Phase (Blaze)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Blaze | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE setting arena on fire except center |
| Fire Arena | **AoE** | Standing in fire deals heavy DoT - stay in center |

### Water Phase (Tsunami)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tsunami | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE applying delayed donut AoE debuff |
| Dynamic Fluid | **Stack** | Donut AoE on players - stack to avoid overlapping |
| Fiendish Orbs | **AoE** | Orbs spawn - tanks can intercept to soak damage |

### Earth Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Earthquake | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage - heal through |

### Wind Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cyclone | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide AoE applying debuffs |

**Rotation Patterns:**
- Pattern 1: Water → Earth → Interlude → Fire → Wind → Enrage
- Pattern 2: Fire → Wind → Interlude → Water → Earth → Enrage

**Strategy:**
- During Blaze, stay in the center safe zone to avoid fire damage
- For Longitudinal, dodge sides then front/back; for Latitudinal, reverse
- Healers must be ready for Bowels of Agony - everyone hits 1 HP
- Stand behind boss during Damning Edict to avoid knockback

**Notes:** The fight tests healing and positioning awareness. Watch for the Implosion cast names to know which safe spots to use first.
