The first floor of Alexander: Midas, featuring multiple boss encounters culminating in a fight against Ratfinx Twinkledinks. Introduces the transformation puddle mechanics unique to this tier.

## Faust

Simple miniboss encounter at the start.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Basic Attacks | **Tankbuster** | Tank and spank - straightforward DPS check |

## Hummelfaust

Second miniboss with slightly more mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Add Phase | **Adds** | Additional enemies spawn - AoE them down |

## Ratfinx Twinkledinks

The main boss of A5, featuring transformation mechanics via colored puddles.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tower Activation | **AoE** | Stand near towers to activate side puddles |
| Puddle Damage | **AoE** | Don't linger in puddles - they deal damage over time |
| Bombs Away | **AoE** | Bombs spawn with large blast radius |
| Red Puddle | **Adds** | Transforms you into a gorilla - use to knock bombs away |
| Boost | **Raidwide** | Lethal if not in bird form - move to violet puddle before cast |
| Violet Puddle | **Adds** | Transforms you into a bird - survive Boost mechanic |
| Bird Form Exit | **AoE** | After Boost resolves, use skill button #2 to return to normal |

**Strategy:**
- Tank the boss in a far corner of the arena
- Stand near towers to activate side puddles, but don't stay in them
- When Bombs Away is cast, 1-2 players step in the red puddle to become gorillas
- Gorilla form allows you to knock bombs far away (they have very large blast radius)
- When Boost is cast, everyone moves to the violet puddle to transform into birds
- Bird form prevents death from Boost
- After Boost resolves, press skill button #2 to return to normal form
- Repeat transformation mechanics as needed throughout the fight

**Notes:** The key to this fight is understanding when to use each transformation. Gorilla form for bombs, bird form for Boost. Don't linger in puddles or you'll take unnecessary damage.
