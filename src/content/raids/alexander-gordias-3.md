The third floor of Alexander: Gordias, featuring Living Liquid - a shape-shifting boss that transforms between human and hand forms. The arena is surrounded by an electric field, so avoid the edges.

## Living Liquid

A multi-phase boss that alternates between forms. Stay away from the arena edges to avoid stun, electrocution DoT, and paralysis.

### Phase 1 - Human Form

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Frontal Cleave | **Cleave** | Tank faces boss away from raid |
| Protean Wave | **Knockback** | Conal AoE in multiple directions - deals damage and knockback |
| Sluice | **Spread** | 4 players get markers - spread out to avoid overlapping AoEs |
| Splash | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage - heal through |

**Rotation:** Protean Wave → Sluice → Splash ×3

### Phase 2 - Hand Form

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Heavy Cleave | **Tankbuster** | Both tanks stack in front to share splash damage |
| Wash Away | **Knockback** | AoE knockback from center - stand mid to avoid walls |
| Liquid Limb | **Adds** | Hand splits - off-tank picks up add and keeps separated |

**Rotation:** Cleave → Wash Away → Cleave → Cleave → Split → Cleave (both adds) ×7

### Phase 3 - Tornado

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tornado | **AoE** | Boss is invulnerable, deals AoE from center |
| Gear Lubricant | **Adds** | Low HP adds moving toward edge - kill quickly |
| Piston Lubricant | **Adds** | Higher HP adds that rush to edge - stun to buy time |
| Orange Tether | **Tankbuster** | Random player gets tether - pass to tank who isolates |
| Positive/Negative Debuff | **Stack** | Players get + or - icons: same icons stack, opposite icons spread |

**Strategy:**
- Tank the boss in the center of the arena, away from electric walls
- In Human Form, spread for Sluice markers and heal through Splash
- In Hand Form, both tanks stack for the shared tankbuster
- When Liquid Limb spawns, off-tank grabs it and keeps both hands separated
- During Tornado, kill adds before they reach the edge (stun Piston Lubricant)
- Pass orange tether to a tank who moves away from the group
- Match positive/negative debuffs: same symbols stack together, opposite symbols stay apart
- These three phases repeat twice throughout the fight

**Notes:** The key mechanics are managing the shared tankbuster in Hand Form and correctly handling the positive/negative debuffs during Tornado phase.
