The first fight of the Pandaemonium raid series. Erichthonios, warden of Asphodelos, has been corrupted and must be stopped.

## Erichthonios

A straightforward introduction to Endwalker's raid mechanics, teaching positioning and AoE awareness.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Gaoler's Flail | **Cleave** | Watch which hand glows - cleaves that half of arena. Move to safe side |
| Warder's Wrath | **Raidwide** | Heavy party-wide damage - shield and heal |
| Heavy Hand | **Tankbuster** | Physical tankbuster - tank cooldowns required |
| Intemperance | **AoE** | Colored crystals appear - match or contrast colors to avoid damage |
| Shining Cells | **Raidwide** | Arena-wide damage that leaves colored floor tiles |
| Aetherflail | **Cleave + AoE** | Combination of flail cleave and additional AoEs |
| Pitiless Flail | **Tankbuster + Knockback** | Tankbuster with knockback - position carefully |
| Slam Shut | **Raidwide** | Damages entire party when cells activate |

### Intemperance (Colored Cubes)

The signature mechanic of this fight:

1. **Red cubes** = Fire damage
2. **Blue cubes** = Ice damage  
3. Cubes stack in 3 layers and resolve from bottom to top
4. **Standing on matching color** = Small damage
5. **Standing on opposite color** = Neutralizes, no damage
6. **Standing on same color 3 times** = Heavy damage/vuln stacks

**Strategy:** Alternate between red and blue tiles as cubes resolve. Watch which color is about to explode and stand on the opposite.

### Tips

- For Gaoler's Flail, watch the glowing hand - move to the opposite side
- During Intemperance, count the cube colors from bottom to top
- Tanks should communicate for Pitiless Flail knockback positioning
- Healers prepare for Warder's Wrath with shields and regens
- Spread loosely to make positional mechanics easier
