The second floor of Alexander: The Creator, featuring Lamebrix Strikebocks in an arena filled with traps. Requires awareness of environmental hazards and boss tells.

## Lamebrix Strikebocks

A goblin boss who uses the arena's traps to his advantage. Avoid the buttons/circles in the middle of the arena.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Gobrush Rushgob | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster - use defensive cooldowns |
| Gobsway Rumblerocks | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE - heal through |
| Ice Trap (North) | **AoE** | Blasts around edges - stand in center to avoid |
| Ball Trap (Middle) | **AoE** | Wrecking Balls roll through center - stand outside |
| Spike Trap (South) | **AoE** | Unavoidable freeze for 5 seconds - just wait it out |
| Prey | **Spread** | Red marker on player - move away from boss and others |
| Steamroller | **Cleave** | Boss charges - melee move back before cast completes |
| Single Charge | **AoE** | Boss marker: Green = move IN, Red = move OUT |
| Gobslash Slicetops | **Cleave** | Frontal cleave - don't stand in front |

**Trap Strategy:**
- **Ice Trap (North)**: Stand in the center of the arena
- **Ball Trap (Middle)**: Stand on the outside edges
- **Spike Trap (South)**: Cannot avoid - you'll be frozen briefly

**Strategy:**
- Watch for trap activations and position accordingly
- For Single Charge, watch the boss's marker color
- Green marker = move close to boss
- Red marker = move away from boss
- Prey-marked players run away from the group
- Melee must retreat before Steamroller completes

**Notes:** The key to this fight is recognizing which trap is activating and reacting with correct positioning. The arena hazards are more dangerous than the boss's direct attacks.
