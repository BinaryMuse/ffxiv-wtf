The second floor of Eden's Promise features the Shadowkeeper, a shadowy beast with directional attacks and clone mechanics. The fight emphasizes watching the boss's shadow rather than the boss itself.

### Shadow Mechanics

**Shadow Implosion:** The key mechanic of this fight.
- Boss casts Forward or Backward Implosion
- Mid-cast, transforms into a shadow and may change position
- The attack originates from the shadow's position, not the original
- Wait in the middle until shadow appears, then move to safety

**Shadow Warrior:**
- Three clones spawn, one tethered to boss
- Clones shift positions and tether disappears
- Track which clone was originally tethered
- Position behind that specific clone - they perform large conal AoEs

**Strategy:**
- Stay center initially to react to directional attacks
- For Shadow Implosions, don't move until you see where the shadow appears
- During Shadow Warrior, identify the tethered clone before it untethers
- Fade to Shadow requires moving to the add-free corner
- React time is limited - learn patterns quickly

**Notes:** Shadow spells always originate from the shadow's position. This concept is central to all mechanics in this encounter.
