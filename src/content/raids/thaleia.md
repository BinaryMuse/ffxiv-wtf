The third and final 24-player alliance raid of the Myths of the Realm series. Face the remaining deities of the Twelve and uncover the ultimate truth behind the gods.

## Thaliak

The Scholar, god of knowledge and rivers. Features water-based attacks and geometric arena mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Katarraktes | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide water damage that applies bleed DoT |
| Thlipsis | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |
| Rheognosis | **AoE** | Creates clones that launch waves across the arena |
| Tetraktys | **AoE** | Triangle-based arena segments become dangerous |
| Tetraktuos Kosmos | **AoE** | Expanded triangle mechanic - navigate explosive segments |
| Hieroglyphika | **AoE** | Knowledge-based attack patterns |
| Left Bank / Right Bank | **Cleave** | Half-room water cleave - move to safe side |

**Notes:** 
- Triangle geometry determines safe zones during Tetraktys mechanics
- Watch for clone positions during Rheognosis to avoid wave attacks
- Healers prepare for bleed DoT from Katarraktes

## Llymlaen

The Navigator, goddess of the seas and patron deity of Limsa Lominsa. Features nautical mechanics with positioning and movement requirements.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tempest | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide storm damage |
| Seafoam Spiral | **AoE** | Dodge into the center circle to avoid |
| Navigator's Trident | **Cleave** | Cleave attacks requiring position switches |
| Surging Wave | **Knockback** | Pushback attack - position to avoid being pushed off |
| Torrential Tridents | **AoE** | Explosions in sequence order - dodge in correct order |
| To the Last | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |
| Deep Dive | **AoE** | Dive attacks creating impact zones |

**Notes:** 
- For Seafoam Spiral, quickly move to the center safe zone
- Surging Wave knockback can push players off the arena - position carefully
- Torrential Tridents explode in numbered order - memorize sequence
- Navigator's Trident requires swapping positions between cleaves

## Oschon

The Wanderer, god of travelers and vagrants. Features ranged attack patterns with extensive movement requirements.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Trek Shot | **Cleave** | Line attacks across the arena |
| Soaring Minuet | **AoE** | Musical attack creating AoE patterns |
| Lofty Peaks | **AoE** | Mountain-themed environmental hazards |
| Piton Pull | **Knockback** | Tether-based movement mechanic |
| Wandering Shot | **AoE** | Moving projectiles across the arena |
| Arrow Trail | **AoE** | Arrow-based line AoEs |
| Sudden Downpour | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide storm damage |
| Altitude | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |

**Notes:** 
- Oschon's attacks emphasize constant movement
- Watch for arrow indicators showing attack trajectories
- Piton Pull mechanics may require using knockback to reposition
- Keep mobile and track multiple moving projectiles

## Eulogia

The culmination of the Myths of the Realm story. This final boss combines elements from previous encounters in a challenging finale.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dawn of Time | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage |
| Quintessence | **AoE** | Elemental attacks combining previous boss themes |
| The Whorl | **AoE** | Spiral AoE patterns |
| First Light | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |
| Solar Fans | **Cleave** | Fan-shaped cleave attacks |
| Hydrostasis | **AoE** | Water-based sequenced attacks |
| Hieroglyphika | **AoE** | Pattern-based attacks |
| Love's Light | **Stack** | Stack marker |
| Eudaimon Eorzea | **Enrage** | Ultimate attack if not defeated in time |

**Notes:** 
- Eulogia combines mechanics from all twelve deities throughout the fight
- Watch for callbacks to previous boss mechanics you've learned
- This is the most mechanically complex encounter in the series
- Rewards item level 650 gear upon completion
