The second fight of the AAC Cruiserweight raid tier in Dawntrail. Sugar Riot is a rebellious artist who utilizes the soul of a pudding, manifesting her imaginings with an augmented palette.

## Sugar Riot

Sugar Riot is fought on a canvas with a screen on the north showing certain mechanics. The arena changes throughout the fight.

### Phase One Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mousse Mural | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magical damage; returns arena to normal |
| Color Riot | **Tankbuster** | AoE magical tank buster on both tanks |
| Pudding Party | **Stack** | Multi-hit stack marker on a healer |
| Single Style | **AoE** | Sugar Riot draws objects and tethers them to areas on ground |

**Single Style Objects:**
- **Regular Bomb**: Giant circle AoE where it lands
- **Winged Bomb**: Jumps horizontally to opposite side, then explodes in giant AoE
- **Arrows**: Line AoE across the arena
- **Target**: Makes all arrows fire towards it instead

### Phase Two Mechanics (Desert)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sugarscape | **Arena Change** | Sugar Riot draws a landscape, changing the arena to a desert |
| Layer: Sun | **DoT** | Inflicts Sweltering, dealing damage over time |
| Layer: Cacti | **AoE** | Spawns cacti creating large AoEs |
| Layer: Quicksand | **Hazard** | Creates quicksand in the middle that pulls players in |
| Mousse Touch-up | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on all players |
| Double Style | **AoE** | Sugar Riot draws two objects at a time |

### Phase Three Mechanics (River)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sugarscape | **Arena Change** | Creates a river with bridges connecting islands |
| Taste of Thunder | **Spread** | Marked thunder AoEs on all players with magic vulnerability |
| Taste of Fire | **Stack** | Stack markers on both healers dealing fire damage |
| Flood | **Hazard** | Floods the river with perpetual lightning strikes |
| Storm | **AoE** | Giant lightning cloud moves across islands, striking each with lethal AoE |

### Tips

- Standing in the river gives fire resistance and lightning weakness
- Dodge cacti, then move to corners to avoid quicksand pull
- For Double Style with bombs and arrows, be in the safe lane away from the bomb side
- Move from island to island on bridges after the storm starts
