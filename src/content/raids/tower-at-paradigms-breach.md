The final alliance raid in the YoRHa: Dark Apocalypse series, concluding the NieR: Automata crossover storyline. This raid features intense mechanics and visual effects - players susceptible to motion sickness or photosensitivity should exercise caution.

### Clone Mechanics

**Replicate** spawns clones on both arena sides with dice markers showing numbers 1-3:
- Dice indicate attack sequence order
- Track which clone attacks first, second, third
- Position safely between each sequential attack
- Clones execute attacks in numbered order

**Colossal Impact tells:**
- **Open mouth:** Attack from front
- **Closed fists:** Attack from fists/sides

**Notes:** This boss teaches fundamental mechanics that reappear throughout the raid.
