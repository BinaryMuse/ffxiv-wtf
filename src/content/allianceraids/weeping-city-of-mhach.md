The second alliance raid in the Shadow of Mhach series, exploring the ruins of the ancient city of Mhach. Features the iconic Ozma encounter with its shape-shifting mechanics.

## Arachne Eve

A voidsent arachne guarding the city entrance.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Silken Spray | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack - don't stand in front |
| Dark Spike | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage - use cooldowns |
| Sticky Wicket | **AoE** | Web puddles slow movement - avoid standing in them |
| Spider Adds | **Adds** | Small spiders spawn - AoE them down |
| Shadow Burst | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage |
| Tremblor | **AoE** | Ground-based AoE - move out of marked areas |

**Strategy:**
- Tank faces boss away from raid
- Avoid web puddles that slow movement
- Kill spider adds quickly
- Move out of ground AoEs

## Forgall

An ancient Mhachi mage wielding dark magic.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Brand of the Fallen | **Spread** | Markers on players - spread to avoid overlap |
| Hell Wind | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage - heal through |
| Mana Drain | **Adds** | Adds drain mana from healers - kill quickly |
| Mega Death | **AoE** | Doom-style mechanic - must be above HP threshold |
| Dark Eruption | **AoE** | Ground eruptions - avoid marked areas |
| Necropurge | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raid damage |

**Strategy:**
- Spread for Brand of the Fallen markers
- Kill Mana Drain adds before healers run dry
- For Mega Death, ensure party is above HP threshold
- Healers prepare for heavy damage during Hell Wind

## Ozma

A legendary geometric entity that shifts between three forms.

### Sphere Form

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Meteor | **Spread** | Red markers - spread to halfway between alliances |
| Cube Adds | **Adds** | Kill quickly after meteors |
| Acceleration Bomb | **AoE** | Do NOT move or attack when debuff expires |

### Pyramid/Triangle Form

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Massive Laser | **AoE** | Fires at main platform - move to walkway immediately |
| Acceleration Bomb | **AoE** | Stop all movement and attacks when debuff timer expires |

### Cube Form

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tank Lasers | **Tankbuster** | Repeated small lasers on tank |
| Tornado | **AoE** | Donut-shaped AoE when adds appear - stay close to Ozma |
| Holy | **Knockback** | Knocks players back - risk of falling off |
| Ball Soak | **Stack** | Balls drop - players must soak them |

### Black Hole Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Teleport | **AoE** | Players teleported to Atomos encounter |
| Yellow Circle | **Stack** | One player stands on circle to keep portals open |
| Assimilation | **Gaze** | Turn away from Ozma or be trapped in Gaol |
| Atomos | **Adds** | DPS adds and Atomos to return to main arena |

**Strategy:**
- Each alliance stays in their designated section
- In Sphere form, spread red markers between alliances, then kill cube adds
- In Pyramid form, immediately move to walkway to avoid laser
- In Cube form, stay close to Ozma for Tornado, brace for Holy knockback
- During Black Hole, one player keeps yellow circle active while others DPS
- Always turn away from Ozma during Assimilation

## Calofisteri

A powerful Mhachi sorceress who achieved immortality through dark rituals.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dancing Mad | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage |
| Haircut | **Cleave** | Various hair-based attacks with different patterns |
| Extension | **AoE** | Hair extends across arena - avoid the lines |
| Penetration | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |
| Evil Curl | **AoE** | Circular AoEs spawn around arena |
| Depth Charge | **Stack** | Stack marker - group up to share damage |
| Living Lock | **Adds** | Hair adds spawn - kill before they reach boss |
| Grand Cross | **Gaze** | Look away or be inflicted with various debuffs |

**Strategy:**
- Watch for hair attack patterns - each has different safe zones
- Avoid hair Extension lines across the arena
- Kill Living Lock adds before they empower the boss
- Stack for Depth Charge markers
- Turn away for Grand Cross gaze attack
- Healers prepare for heavy Dancing Mad damage

**Notes:** The Weeping City features Ozma, one of the most memorable alliance raid bosses due to its unique shape-shifting mechanics. The fight requires alliance coordination and awareness of form changes.
