The first alliance raid of the Shadow of Mhach series, set aboard a massive ghost ship from the void. A 24-player raid introducing the story of the ancient Mhachi civilization.

## Cetus

A massive stingray-like voidsent guarding the ark's entrance.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Divebomb | **AoE** | Green marker - boss charges toward player dropping water bombs |
| Water Bombs | **AoE** | Exploding bombs scatter around arena - avoid them |
| Donut AoE | **AoE** | Safe spot is close to the boss |
| Electric Whorl | **Raidwide** | Run toward boss to avoid raid-wide AoE |
| Hydrosphere/Hydrocore | **Adds** | One alliance pulled to center - inside kills Hydrocore, outside kills Hydrospheres |
| Line AoE Adds | **Adds** | Adds with line AoEs - walk toward them to reduce damage |

**Strategy:**
- Player with green Divebomb marker creates a safe path away from the group
- For Donut AoE, move close to the boss
- During Hydrocore phase, inside team kills the core while outside teams kill all spheres
- Line AoE adds deal reduced damage the closer you are to them

## Irminsul & Sawtooth

A pole and dog duo requiring careful tank positioning and damage type awareness.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Separation Required | **AoE** | Keep bosses apart - if pole pulse hits dog, it gains permanent damage buffs |
| Damage Immunity | **Adds** | One boss immune to magic, one to ranged physical |
| Mucus Tethers | **Adds** | Break chains by walking across them |
| Muddy AoE | **AoE** | Avoid grey bubbling AoEs - too many hits triggers enrage |

**Damage Type Rules:**
- One boss is always immune to magic, one to ranged physical
- Melee DPS can hit either boss
- Ranged physical DPS targets the magic-immune boss
- Casters target the ranged-immune boss
- If pole hits Sawtooth 16 times, the raid wipes

**Strategy:**
- Tanks keep bosses separated at all times
- Watch immunity and assign DPS accordingly
- Break Mucus tethers quickly by running through them
- Avoid grey bubbling AoEs on the ground

## Cuchulainn

A grotesque voidsent with poison mechanics and tentacle attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Bleed Debuff | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide bleed increases throughout fight |
| Vomit Attack | **Cleave** | Extremely dangerous with no floor indicator - avoid at all costs |
| Tentacle Attack | **AoE** | Position between two raised tentacles to avoid damage |
| Poison Floor | **AoE** | Floor becomes poison - find safety platforms in corners |
| Slime Adds | **Adds** | Kill quickly - if boss cone hits them, it gains permanent damage buff |
| Black Ball | **Stack** | Run into it to trigger delayed raid-wide damage |

**Strategy:**
- Healers manage increasing bleed damage throughout fight
- Watch for vomit attack - it's deadly and has no ground marker
- When tentacles raise, stand between them
- Find corner platforms when floor turns to poison
- Kill slimes before boss's cone AoE hits them
- One player triggers black ball to control the raid damage

## Echidna

A lamia-like (serpentine humanoid) voidsent similar to Melusine from the Second Coil of Bahamut.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Petrifaction | **Gaze** | Face away from boss - inflicts stun and vulnerability |
| Flame Wreath | **AoE** | Ring of fire around arena - stay inside |
| Abyssal Reaper | **AoE** | Large AoE centered on boss - move away |
| Adds | **Adds** | Lamia-type adds spawn - kill before they empower boss |
| Tail Swipe | **Cleave** | Rear cleave - don't stand directly behind boss |

**Strategy:**
- Turn away during Petrifaction - debuffs wear off after a few seconds
- Stay inside the Flame Wreath boundary
- Move out for Abyssal Reaper
- Kill adds quickly before they reach the boss
- Melee avoid directly behind boss due to tail swipe

**Notes:** The Void Ark is the introductory alliance raid for the Shadow of Mhach series, unlocking the story that continues in Weeping City and Dun Scaith.
