The second 24-player alliance raid of the Echoes of Vana'diel series. Continuing the FFXI crossover, face Kam'lanaut, the Archduke of Jeuno and one of the last survivors of the ancient Zilart race.

## Faithbound Kirin

The first boss. Clear the trash mobs and use the portal to enter.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Stonega IV | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magical damage |
| Synchronized Sequence | **AoE** | Boss shows two red AoEs that Arms copy; casts first then second |
| Double Wringer | **AoE** | Shows two red AoEs that Arms copy, then does Wringer at end of cast |
| Wringer | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE copied by Arms as donut AoE around boss |
| Towers | **Soak** | Four towers that tanks must soak; NPC takes one |
| Mighty Grip | **Adds** | Arena becomes square; kill two Chiselled Arm adds before Kirin Captivator finishes |
| Striking Right | **AoE** | Boss faces and tethers to an Arm, striking right side in circle AoE; tethered Arm copies with giant circle |
| Summon Shijin | **AoE** | Summons Byakko add that jumps to a panel, dashes across room, and does large circle AoE |
| Crimson Riddle | **Cleave** | Boss cleaves in front, then behind with tail; watch for fiery mouth/tail |
| Synchronized Strike | **AoE** | Two large line AoEs copied by Arms |
| Wrought Arms | **Adds** | Summons arms at edge that copy boss AoEs when tethered |

**Tips:** Double Wringer always has three AoE sets. Watch Byakko's jump destination during Summon Shijin—sides are always safe.

## Ultima and Omega

Dual-boss encounter on a floating airship. Both must be tanked with separate health bars.

### Ultima, the Feared

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Citadel Buster | **Raidwide** | Heavy raidwide damage |
| Antimatter | **Tankbuster** | Magic tankbuster on Ultima's tank and non-Omega tank |
| Chemical Bomb | **Proximity** | Four proximity AoEs around edge in succession; rotate to mitigate |
| Energy Orb | **AoE** | Beams fire from circles at edge where Ultima is |
| Mana Screen | **Mechanic** | Summons screens that extend Energy Orb beams perpendicular when hit |
| Tractor Beam | **Pull** | Pulls all players toward Ultima; airship spawns on one side with AoE |

### Omega, the One

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ion Efflux | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magical damage |
| Hyper Pulse | **Proximity** | Omega lands in middle dealing proximity damage |
| Multi-missile | **Pull + AoE** | Ultima pulls and stuns players; navigate AoEs to other side before airship explodes |
| Surface Missile | **AoE** | Three sets of AoEs firing in succession |
| Anti-personnel Missile | **Spread** | Marked AoEs on several players |
| Aft-to-fore Fire | **Cleave** | Moves near Ultima; AoE behind then in front after cast finishes |
| Trajectory Projection | **AoE** | Players receive chaser AoEs with countdowns |

**Tips:** Focus target helps track both bosses. Energy Orb beams hit only two of three Mana Screens—find the safe spot. Use mobility abilities during Multi-missile stun escape.

## Kam'lanaut

Uses elements and swordplay. The arena changes at certain points.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Transcendent Union | **Raidwide** | Six instances of raidwide magical damage plus one heavy hit |
| Enspirited Swordplay | **Raidwide** | Magical raidwide damage |
| Empyrean Banish IV | **Stack** | Stack marker on random player |
| Princely Blow | **Tankbuster** | Physical line AoE tankbuster on all three tanks |
| Shield Bash | **Knockback** | Knockback from center; use prevention abilities or get knocked to platforms |
| Illumed Facet | **Adds** | Summons three adds casting Illumed Estoc line AoE toward platforms |
| Light Blade | **AoE** | Line AoE across middle; spawning swords do line AoEs; boss cleaves side where sword glows |
| Elemental Blade | **Mechanic** | Boss casts elemental rings indicating which elements expand next |
| Sublime Elements | **AoE** | Elemental line/conal AoEs spawn; elements from Elemental Blade expand and widen |
| Esoteric Palisade | **AoE** | Summons elemental crystals; expanding elemental rings trigger matching crystals for large circle AoEs |
| Proving Ground | **Hazard** | Creates bleed puddle in middle |
| Esoteric Scrivening | **Mechanic** | Tethers players; arena floats up with three side platforms |

**Elements:** Fire (red), Water (dark blue), Wind (green), Earth (brown), Ice (white), Lightning (purple).

**Tips:** Remember elements from Elemental Blade—move away from those elements in the next mechanic. Light Blade has three parts including a half-room cleave.

## Eald'narche

Final boss. Players can fall off the arena.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Stellar Burst | **Stack** | Grabs one player and chains them with stack AoE |
| Uranos Cascade | **Tankbuster** | AoE magical tankbuster on all three tanks |
| Empyreal Vortex | **Raidwide + AoE** | Multi-hit raidwide with marked and ground AoEs |
| Duplicate | **AoE** | Summons giant cards that explode on tile and four adjacent tiles |
| Visions of Paradise | **AoE** | Like Duplicate but swaps two tiles; dodge card explosions and avoid moved tile location |
| Excelsior | **Transition** | Stuns players, removes one tile, transitions to phase two |
| Gaea Stream | **AoE** | Line AoEs move in indicated direction; two sets after phase transition |
| Warp | **Cleave** | Boss jumps to corner facing center; Sleepga is frontal AoE that puts players to sleep |
| Cronos Sling | **AoE** | Cards form sphere (point-blank) or cylinder (donut); cards then move to indicate side cleave |
| Ancient Triad | **AoE** | Ice AoEs cover three tiles, fire circles spawn; either proximity damage with water rings or draw-in with wind tornadoes |
| Omega Javelin | **AoE** | Marks players with AoEs leaving persistent puddles that explode |

**Tips:** Two safe tiles for Duplicate initially, but only one after phase transition. Follow Gaea Stream AoEs as a second set follows. Watch card formation around boss for Cronos Sling in/out mechanic.
