The second Crystal Tower alliance raid, continuing the story of the Allagan Empire's legacy. Features more complex mechanics than Labyrinth of the Ancients.

## Scylla

A boss with elemental orb mechanics and platform-based survival.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Elemental Orbs | **Adds** | Red (fire), Blue (ice), Purple (lightning) orbs spawn |
| Light Pillars | **AoE** | Generators on pillars need one of each orb type |
| Daybreak | **Raidwide** | Stuns everyone if generators not properly charged |
| Ancient Flare | **Raidwide** | Stand in water puddles for damage reduction |
| Elder Staff | **Adds** | Kill adds that spawn - they do ring AoEs |

**Orb Mechanics:**
- Red orbs: Single-target damage, melts ice
- Blue orbs: Freezes players
- Purple orbs: Small AoE damage
- Each generator needs one of each charge type
- Improper charging causes Daybreak stun

**Strategy:**
- Coordinate orb delivery to generators on light pillars
- During Ancient Flare, stand in water puddles
- During Daybreak, return to your team platform to activate barriers
- Kill Elder Staff adds that appear

## Glasya Labolas

A mechanical boss with laser and robot mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Colorful AoEs | **AoE** | Dodge the various colored ground markers |
| Robot Lasers | **AoE** | Robots must block lasers to prevent boss power-up |
| Spanner Rooks | **Adds** | When robots disabled, DPS these on side platforms |
| Teleporters | **AoE** | Access teleporters after killing Spanner Rooks |

**Strategy:**
- Dodge colorful AoEs throughout the fight
- Robots around arena block lasers aimed at boss
- When robots are disabled, DPS goes to side platforms
- Kill Spanner Rooks to access teleporters
- Return to main platform once side objectives cleared

## Amon

A whimsical boss with transformation and add mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Slime Adds | **Adds** | Kill quickly before they reach boss |
| Kumkum Adds | **Adds** | Feed purple shrinking orbs to kill faster |
| Ice Block | **Gaze** | Hide behind frozen players during big attack |
| Toad Transformation | **AoE** | Being transformed is harmless - keep attacking |

**Strategy:**
- Kill slime adds quickly before they reach Amon
- Purple shrinking orbs should be fed to Kumkum adds
- When ice blocks appear (frozen players), hide behind them
- Amon's big attack coming requires line-of-sight block
- Toad transformation is harmless - continue dealing damage

## Xande

The final boss with tower mechanics and add phases.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Yellow Circles | **Stack** | At least one person must stand in each |
| Disappearing Phase | **Adds** | At 70% and 30%, Xande disappears for DPS check |
| Black Circles | **Stack** | Enter to split damage and gain protective barrier |

**Tower/Circle Strategy:**
- Yellow circles spawn on ground - at least one player per circle
- Failure to stand in circles causes raid damage

**Add Phase Strategy:**
- At 70% and 30% HP, Xande disappears
- DPS check on adds (sides first, then middle)
- Kill adds before Xande returns

**Black Circle Strategy:**
- Black circles appear on random players
- Enter circles to split damage
- Provides protective barrier to players inside

**Notes:**
- Like other Crystal Tower raids, lacks item level sync
- Many mechanics can be ignored or skipped with modern gear
- Still important to handle major mechanics (towers, add phases)
