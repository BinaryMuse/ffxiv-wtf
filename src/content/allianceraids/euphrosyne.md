The second 24-player alliance raid of the Myths of the Realm series. Continue the journey to discover the truth of the Twelve by facing four more deities.

## Nophica

The Matron, goddess of the harvest and patron deity of Gridania. Features arena transformation mechanics based on nature themes.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Matron's Plenty | **AoE** | Thorns appear at arena edges - stay away from borders |
| Floral Haze | **Gaze** | Applies "About Face" debuff - watch facing direction |
| Sowing Circle | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide nature damage |
| Reaper's Harvest | **AoE** | Sequenced criss-cross attacks across the arena |
| Landwaker | **AoE** | Ground-based eruptions |
| The Giving Land | **AoE** | Arena transforms - behavior depends on terrain type |

**Notes:** 
- When arena is a **flower field**, stay **away from center**
- When arena is **grassy forest**, get **close to center**
- Watch for terrain changes to position correctly

## Althyk and Nymeia

The Keeper and The Spinner, deities of time and fate. This dual boss fight requires two tanks to separate the bosses.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Mythril Greataxe | **Tankbuster** | Althyk's tank buster |
| Spinner's Wheel | **Tankbuster** | Nymeia's tank buster |
| Axioma | **Raidwide** | Combined raid-wide damage |
| Spinner's Fate | **Gaze** | Debuff requiring you to either look away OR stand still to avoid fire damage |
| Hydrorythmos | **Knockback** | Water attacks that push players back |
| Inexorable Pull | **AoE** | Gravitational attacks from Althyk |
| Petrai | **AoE** | Stone-based AoE patterns |

**Notes:** 
- Both tanks must grab a boss immediately and keep them separated
- Spinner's Fate **reverses its effect** midway through the fight
- First half: Look away to avoid damage
- Second half: Stand still to avoid damage
- Watch for water knockbacks near arena edges

## Halone

The Fury, goddess of war and patron deity of Ishgard. A combat-focused encounter with sequential dodging requirements.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tetrapagos | **AoE** | Four telegraphed AoEs that must be dodged in order |
| Will of the Fury | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage |
| Spears Three | **Cleave** | Triple spear attacks cleaving portions of arena |
| Thousandfold Thrust | **AoE** | Rapid thrust attacks creating danger zones |
| Wrath of Halone | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |
| Glacial Spear | **Adds** | Add phase with rotating line AoEs |
| Chalaza | **AoE** | Ice-based AoE patterns |

**Notes:** 
- Most attacks cleave large portions of the arena
- Identify safe zones quickly between cleave patterns
- During add phase, watch for rotating line AoEs from ice spears
- Tetrapagos requires sequential dodging - learn the order

---

## Menphina

The Lover, goddess of the moon. The final encounter features her loyal hound Dalamud alongside lunar-themed mechanics.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Loving Light | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide moon damage |
| Blue Moon | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage |
| Playful Orbit | **AoE** | Dalamud (the hound) performs attacks |
| Midnight Frost | **AoE** | Ice-based spreading AoE |
| First Blush | **Cleave** | Crescent-shaped cleaves across the arena |
| Full Bright | **Gaze** | Gaze attack - look away from the source |
| Lunar Kiss | **Stack** | Stack marker requiring group stacking |
| Winter Halo | **AoE** | Donut AoE around the boss - stay close |

**Notes:** 
- Dalamud (the hound) assists Menphina with additional attacks
- Track both Menphina and Dalamud's positions for overlapping mechanics
- Lunar-themed attacks often have crescent or circular patterns
