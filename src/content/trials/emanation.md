A trial against Sri Lakshmi, the Lady of Bliss, a primal summoned by the Ananta. This fight introduces the Vril mechanic, a duty action essential for survival.

## Lakshmi

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dreaming Kshatriya | **Adds** | Two adds spawn at the start. Focus one at a time to prevent healing between them. |
| Pull of Light | **Tankbuster** | Targets the highest aggro target. Tank should use cooldowns. |
| Stotram | **Raidwide** | Party-wide unavoidable AoE that spawns Vril orbs. Collect orbs to gain Vril charges. |
| Hand of Grace | **Spread** | Marks 2+ players with cross-shaped AoEs. Move to arena edges without overlapping. |
| Hand of Beauty | **AoE** | Marks 2 players with circle AoEs that follow them. Move away to avoid growing puddles. |
| Pall of Light | **Stack** | Stack marker on a player. Non-marked players group with the marked player. |
| Blissful Kiss | **Raidwide** | Lakshmi becomes untargetable and blows a kiss. **Immediately use Vril** or you will die. |

**Notes:** Collect Vril bubbles whenever they appear. Always maintain at least 1 Vril charge.

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Divine Denial | **Knockback** | Lakshmi's ultimate attack that glows across the platform. **Use Vril immediately**—knockback prevention abilities do NOT work. |
| Chanchala | **Raidwide** | Buff that increases Lakshmi's damage slightly. |
| Path of Light | **Cleave** | Blue spinning marker on second-highest aggro. Turn boss away from party. |
| Alluring Arm | **Spread** / **AoE** | Combines Hand of Grace and Hand of Beauty simultaneously. Quick positioning required. |

**Notes:** Divine Denial will kill you if you don't use Vril. Always save at least one charge for this mechanic.
