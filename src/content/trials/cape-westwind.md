A solo duty as of patch 6.1 (previously an 8-player trial). Face the Imperial praefectus Rhitahtyn sas Arvina during Operation Archon.

## Rhitahtyn sas Arvina

Imperial praefectus defending the Garlean stronghold at Cape Westwind.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Fire Puddle | **AoE** | Random fire puddles spawn around the arena - avoid standing in them |
| Magitek Missile | **AoE** | Large AoE attack targeting the boss's location - move away when text appears |
| Cohort Optio | **Adds** | Two adds spawn at ~66% HP - defeat them to continue |

**Notes:**
- This duty was converted to a solo instance in patch 6.1
- The fight is relatively straightforward and burns down quickly
- NPC allies assist you throughout the encounter
- Included for historical reference - original trial mechanics were minimal
