The Minstrel's Ballad: Zodiark's Fall is the Extreme version of The Dark Inside trial, unlocked by speaking to the Wandering Minstrel in Old Sharlayan (X:12.7, Y:14.2) after completing the Endwalker main scenario. This fight features four phases with increasingly complex mechanic combinations and a 60-minute time limit.

## Zodiark

### Phase 1 - Opening

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Kokytos | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raidwide that leaves all party members at 1 HP. Healers must immediately restore health. |
| Paradeigma (Bird) | **AoE** | Bird tokens create donut AoEs with only 2 safe spots: melee range and far away from the token. |
| Paradeigma (Behemoth) | **AoE** | Behemoth tokens create point-blank circle AoEs. Move away from them. |
| Paradeigma (Snake) | **AoE** | Snake tokens create line AoEs covering one row or column of the arena. |
| Styx | **Stack** | Group stack attack hitting multiple times. Starts with 6 hits and increases by one hit each subsequent cast. Heavy mitigation required. |
| Ania | **Tankbuster** | Heavy magic damage tankbuster inflicting Umbral Rays debuff, forcing a tank swap. Small circle AoE—tanks must not clip others. |

### Phase 2 - Exoterikos

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Exoterikos (Red/Rectangle) | **AoE** | Arcane Sigil with rectangle shape covers the entire half of the arena on that side. |
| Exoterikos (Blue/Triangle) | **Cleave** | Arcane Sigil with triangle shape fires a large cone AoE across the arena. |
| Exoterikos (Green/Diamond) | **AoE** | Arcane Sigil with diamond shape creates a large line AoE covering roughly one-third of the arena. |
| Astral Flow | **AoE** | Arena rotates 90 degrees. Adjust your mental orientation for subsequent mechanics. |

### Phase 3 - Combinations

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Algedon | **Cleave** | Zodiark moves to a front corner and uses a large line AoE that knocks players off. Safe spots are the two adjacent corners and the opposite corner. |
| Triple Esoteric Ray | **AoE** | Sequential line AoEs. Dodge into the first one that fires as it becomes safe. |
| Adikia | **AoE** | Two large semi-circular AoEs in succession. Move in or out relative to Zodiark to dodge. |
| Phlegethon | **Spread** | Ground AoEs placed under all players. Spread out and reposition quickly. |

### Phase 4 - Full Combinations

All previous mechanics combine with arena rotations and overlapping patterns. Be prepared for:
- Paradeigma + Exoterikos combinations
- Astral Flow + immediate follow-up mechanics
- Styx with increasing hit counts (up to 9+ hits)

**Notes:** 
- Assign clock positions for spread mechanics.
- Tanks must swap after Ania due to the Umbral Rays debuff.
- Styx damage increases significantly as the fight progresses—save mitigation for later casts.
- No Phoenix Down is available in this trial.
