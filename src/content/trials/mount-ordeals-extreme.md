Mount Ordeals (Extreme) is the Extreme version of the Rubicante trial, unlocked by speaking to the Wandering Minstrel in Old Sharlayan after completing the 6.3 MSQ "Desires Untold." This fight features complex rotational ring mechanics that require careful observation and pre-positioning.

## Rubicante

### Pre-Fight Setup

Split into two light parties taking East/West sides. Pair each DPS in quadrants using color-coded markers (ABCD on cardinals, 1234 on intercardinals) with assigned clock positions.

### Phase 1 - Ring Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Inferno | **Raidwide** | Heavy raidwide fire damage. Mitigate and heal through. |
| Ordeal of Purgation | **AoE** | Three rings appear with lines that shoot fireballs to activate runes on the arena edge. Blue triangles = conal AoEs. Orange squares = large square AoEs covering one side. Rings may rotate, changing fireball trajectories. Players are rooted—find the safe spot before the cast completes. |
| Shattering Heat | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster with a small AoE. Tank should move away from the party. |
| Arch Inferno | **AoE** | Rotating circle AoEs sweep around the arena with a knockback tornado in the center. Stay near edges and move with the rotation. |
| Fourfold Flame | **Stack** | Stack markers appear on healers. Light parties stack on their healers. |
| Twinfold Flame | **Stack** | Stack markers appear on same-role players. Role pairs stack together. |
| Spike of Flame | **Spread** | Prey markers with circle AoEs. Spread to assigned positions. |
| Conflagration | **AoE** | Line AoE through Rubicante's hitbox. Move to the sides. |
| Radial Flagration | **Spread** | Conal AoEs toward all players. Spread around the boss evenly. |

### Phase 2 - Add Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Greater Flamesent | **Adds** | Large add that must be killed before the gauge fills. |
| Flamesent Adds | **Adds** | Smaller adds spawn alongside the Greater Flamesent. |
| Ghastly Torch | **AoE** | Add-phase AoEs that must be avoided while killing adds. |
| Ghastly Wind | **AoE** | Cone AoEs from adds. Position behind them. |

### Phase 3 - Uncloaked

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Flamespire Brand | **AoE** | Players receive brand debuffs that resolve into different AoE patterns. |
| Sweeping Immolation | **Cleave** | Large sweeping cleave across the arena. Identify safe zones based on Rubicante's position. |
| Flamespire Claw | **Tankbuster** | Enhanced tank buster during the uncloaked phase. |
| Dualfire | **Tankbuster** | Hits both tanks simultaneously. Both need cooldowns. |
| Scalding Ring/Signal | **AoE** | In or out mechanic based on visual cues. |

### Ordeal of Purgation Tips

The ring mechanic is the core challenge:
1. Identify which lines will activate which runes
2. Watch for ring rotation arrows and direction
3. Position in the safe zone before Penance roots you
4. Second cast always involves rotation—calculate the new trajectory

**Notes:**
- Ordeal of Purgation repeats multiple times with increasing complexity.
- The rotation direction of the rings is indicated by arrows—watch carefully.
- During add phase, prioritize the Greater Flamesent while dodging mechanics.
- Save mitigation for the uncloaked phase as damage ramps up significantly.
