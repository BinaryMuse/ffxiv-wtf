A trial against Suzaku, the vermilion bird of the Four Lords. This fight takes place on a circular platform and features several positioning-based mechanics.

## Suzaku

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cremate | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster attack. Use defensive cooldowns. |
| Screams of the Damned | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage. Healers prepare. |
| Southron Star | **Raidwide** | Additional group-wide AoE damage. |
| Eternal Flame | **Adds** | Summons Scarlet Ladies (adds). Stack them in the center and kill them there. |
| Phoenix Down | **AoE** | Creates a safe zone by killing a plume in the middle. Adds may revive if not killed in center. |
| Ruthless Refrain | **Knockback** | Knockback attack. Stay close to center to avoid being knocked off the arena. |

**Notes:** Kill adds in the center during Eternal Flame or they may be revived during Phoenix Down.

### Positioning Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Orbs | **AoE** | One player stands in each circle, facing the direction indicated by arrows. |
| Runes | **AoE** | Move next to the section Suzaku is targeting, then move into it after it explodes. |
| Incandescent Interlude | **Stack** | Meteors that require at least one player per meteor to soak. |

**Notes:** Communication is helpful for orb and meteor assignments in pickup groups.

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Phantom Flurry | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster attack. Tanks prepare cooldowns. |

**Notes:** All Phase 1 mechanics continue with increased intensity. Watch for the knockback especially.
