The climactic trial of the Endwalker Hildibrand questline. Face Asura, a multi-armed deity with complex rotational mechanics.

## Asura

Asura is a fearsome multi-armed boss that uses a variety of sweeping attacks and summons giant clones for combination mechanics. This fight emphasizes watching the boss's positioning and the arena carefully.

### Raid-wide Attacks

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lower Realm | **Raidwide** | Roomwide AoE - arena edges become dangerous after cast |
| Cutting Jewel | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage - use defensive cooldowns |

### Positioning Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Asuri Chakra | **AoE** | Consecutive ring AoEs - stand between the rings to avoid damage |
| Ephemerality | **AoE** | Summons phantom clones that leave afterimages - dodge the circular AoEs |

### Iconography Attacks

Asura tethers to a giant clone on the arena's outer edge. Both the boss and clone cast attacks simultaneously:

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Pedestal Purge + Iconic Execution | **AoE** | Circular AoE from boss, half-circle from clone |
| Wheel of Deincarnation + Iconic Execution | **AoE** | Donut AoE from boss - safe spot is near the clone |
| Bladewise + Iconic Execution | **AoE** | Line AoE from boss - safe spots are on arena sides |

### Rotation Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Six-bladed Khadga | **Cleave** | Six sequential half-circle cleaves - stay close and move minimally between safe spots |
| Myriad Aspects | **AoE** | Two sets of cone AoEs - dodge into resolved cones to avoid second set |
| The Face of Delight | **Mechanic** | Arena splits blue/red - move to the opposite color |
| Many Faces | **Cleave** | Rotating straight-line cleaves - stay close to boss and strafe |
| Bladescatter | **AoE** | Line AoEs that rotate - minimal movement needed |

**Tips:**
- Stay close to the boss during rotational attacks to minimize movement needed
- Watch the giant clone's position and attack tells
- Learn which Iconic Execution pairing creates which safe zones
- Arena edges are dangerous after Lower Realm - don't stand too close
