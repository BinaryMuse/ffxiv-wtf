Face Leviathan, the Lord of the Whorl, on a ship platform. This 8-player trial introduces unique mechanics involving platform management and the Elemental Converter.

## Leviathan

The Lord of the Whorl, primal of the Sahagin beast tribe.

### Core Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Veil of the Whorl | - | Head reflects ranged damage - melee attacks only on head |
| Mantle of the Whorl | - | Tail reflects magic damage - physical attacks only on tail |
| Body Slam | **Knockback** | Water plumes appear, then platform tilts - run to opposite side |
| Tidal Wave | **Raidwide** | Massive attack - use Elemental Converter to survive |

**Tank Split:** One tank on head, one tank on tail at all times.

### Elemental Converter

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Elemental Converter | - | Located at south end - activate before Tidal Wave |
| Gyre Spume | **Adds** | Yellow adds that drain the converter gauge - kill immediately |

**Notes:** Converter gauge must be at least 30 to survive Tidal Wave.

### Add Phases

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wavetooth Sahagin | **Adds** | Casts Dreadstorm (fear puddles) - kill first |
| Wavespine Sahagin | **Adds** | Standard add - lower priority than Wavetooth |

### Other Attacks

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Waterspout | **AoE** | Targets random healers - spread apart |
| Dread Tide | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on tank |
| Tidal Roar | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage |

**Tips:**
- Watch for water plumes indicating which side Leviathan will slam
- Gyre Spumes are top priority - they drain your survival gauge
- Kill Wavetooth Sahagin first to prevent fear mechanics
- Healers should spread to avoid stacking Waterspout damage
