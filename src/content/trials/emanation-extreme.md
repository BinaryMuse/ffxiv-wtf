The extreme version of the Lakshmi trial, requiring careful Vril management across five phases.

## Lakshmi

### Phase 1 (Add Phase)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dreaming Kshatriya | **Adds** | Two adds spawn and shield the boss. Focus down one add before the first Stotram cast. Boss is immune until adds die. |
| Stotram | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raid-wide AoE that also heals the adds. Spawns Vril orbs for collection. |
| Pull of Light | **Tankbuster** | Tankbuster requiring cooldowns. |
| Pall of Light | **Stack** | Stack marker for party damage mitigation. |
| Hand of Grace | **Spread** | Cross-shaped column AoEs on marked players. Place in outer corners. |
| Hand of Beauty | **AoE** | Two players marked with growing circular AoEs. Move away from the group. |

**Notes:** Kill adds before Stotram heals them. Collect Vril orbs whenever they spawn.

### Phase 2 (80% HP)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Chanchala | **Raidwide** | Boss buff that enhances all attacks with black and blue flame telegraphs. |
| Divine Denial | **Knockback** | Ultimate attack requiring Vril activation. **Use Vril or die.** |
| Alluring Arm | **Spread** / **AoE** | Combination of Hand of Grace and Hand of Beauty used simultaneously. |
| Path of Light | **Cleave** | Blue spinning marker on second-highest aggro. Turn boss away from party. |

**Notes:** Vril usage becomes mandatory in this phase. Enhanced attacks are more punishing.

### Vril Management

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Vril Charges | **Stack** | Players have 3 Vril uses before needing to recharge. Conservation is critical. |
| Vril Orbs | **AoE** | Collect orbs during Stotram casts and Vril Restoration to refresh charges. |
| Blissful Kiss | **Raidwide** | Boss becomes untargetable and blows a kiss. **Use Vril immediately** or die. |

**Notes:** Always maintain at least 1 Vril charge for emergency use. Recharge opportunities are limited—don't waste charges on avoidable damage.
