A Dawntrail trial featuring Zelenia, a mechanical guardian forged from Queen Sphene's memories of the royal knight. Unlocked through the MSQ "Descent to the Foundation."

## Zelenia

Zelenia wields a massive sword and uses a combination of lightning-based attacks and geometric AoE patterns. The fight introduces the Roseblood Bloom mechanic that transforms the arena.

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Alexandrian Thunder IV | **AoE** | Inner point-blank and outer donut rings spawn - they resolve in random order |
| Shock | **Spread** | All players receive spread markers dropping persistent AoEs - move out quickly |
| Power Break | **Cleave** | Half-arena slash based on sword angle - watch which way she's facing |
| Specter of the Lost | **Tankbuster** | Double conal tankbuster on both tanks - face away from party |

### Holy Hazard

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Holy Hazard | **AoE** | Two sets of consecutive 120-degree cone AoEs - stand between first set, then move to safe area |
| Thunder Slash | **AoE** | Sequential conal AoEs combined with Alexandrian Thunder IV mechanics |

### Phase 2 - Roseblood Bloom

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Roseblood Bloom | **Mechanic** | Arena transforms with rose-patterned tiles |
| Rose Tiles | **Mechanic** | Tiles copy mechanics that hit them and spread to connected rose tiles |
| Alexandrian Thunder III | **AoE** | Ground circles explode in sequence - different effects on rose tiles |

**Tips:**
- Watch the ring AoEs carefully - inner or outer can resolve first
- Spread out for Shock markers and move to safe areas quickly
- During Roseblood Bloom, avoid standing on rose tiles when mechanics resolve nearby
- Avoidable damage applies stacking Vulnerability Up - dodge carefully
- Stay away from dangerous arena edges
