An Extreme trial featuring Sphene, the Queen Eternal, unlocked by speaking with the Wandering Minstrel in Tuliyollal after completing the Dawntrail MSQ.

## Sphene (The Queen Eternal)

**Wind Phase:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Aethertide | **AoE** | Sphene distorts arena portions three times, firing lasers at healers that others must soak |
| Laws of Wind | **Stack** | Stack markers on both healers; Light Party 1 NW, Light Party 2 SE, then move to center |
| Legitimate Force | **Cleave** | Boss attacks glowing side first; dodge accordingly |

**Earth Phase:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Meteor Impact | **AoE** | Square position formation; DPS outside, tanks/healers inside |
| Royal Domain | **AoE** | Arena-wide mechanics with positional requirements |

**Ice Phase:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Absolute Authority | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage |
| Dimensional Distortion | **AoE** | Arena manipulation mechanics |

**Coronation Phase:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Coronation | **Raidwide** | Phase transition with heavy damage |
| Royal Banishment | **Tankbuster** | Heavy dual tank buster |

**Notes:** Divide into two Light Parties with assigned positions. Use square formations for Meteor Impact mechanics. Strategies may vary by data center—study mechanics beforehand unless doing blind progression.
