The Extreme version of the Shiva trial. An 8-player encounter with complex weapon stance mechanics and a soft enrage timer. Set Shiva as Focus Target to track her stance changes.

## Shiva

### Weapon Stances

Shiva alternates between Staff and Sword modes, each with unique mechanics and tank debuffs.

| Stance | Buff | Tank Debuff |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Staff | Damage Up | Blunt damage, stacking slashing resistance (up to 8 stacks, 2min) |
| Sword | Healing/Defense Up | Different attack patterns |

### Staff Mode Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hailstorm | **Spread** | AoE circles over all players (Phase 1: all 8, later: 4-5) |
| Absolute Zero | **Raidwide** | Four map-wide AoEs - requires AoE healing |

**Positioning:** No cleave attacks in Staff mode - tank can position freely. Spread for Hailstorm.

### Sword Mode Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ice Brand | **Cleave** | High-damage frontal cone |
| Glacier Bash | **Knockback** | Stun attack - move behind Shiva |
| Heavenly Strike | **Knockback** | Low damage knockback |

**Positioning:** Tank faces Shiva away from party.

### Both Modes

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Icicle Impact | **AoE** | Edge circles + center circle - find safe spots |
| Permafrost | **AoE** | Slippery ice floor with Thin Ice and Frostbite DoT |

### Phase 1 (100% - 80%)

Easier phase with 2 weapon changes. Hailstorm targets all 8 players.

### Phase 2 (80% - 70%)

Transition phase - approximately 60 seconds or until HP threshold.

### Diamond Dust (Add Phase)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ice Soldiers | **Adds** | Four adds spawn - kill to reduce Diamond Dust damage |
| Diamond Dust | **Raidwide** | Freezes all players - damage scales with surviving adds |

**Strategy:** Stack in middle. Kill all Ice Soldiers before Diamond Dust completes.

### Phase 3 (70% - 0%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dreams of Ice | **Enrage** | Shiva gains stacking Damage Up over time |
| Icicle Impact (Sequential) | **AoE** | Rings appear one after another - move into cleared spaces |
| Ice Wall | **AoE** | Arena edges freeze players into boulders - destroy to free |
| All previous | - | All mechanics continue |

### Enrage

| Stacks | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 1-10 | Increasing damage |
| 11-12 | Hard enrage - next Diamond Dust kills party |

**Tips:**
- Set Shiva as Focus Target to track weapon changes
- Spread during Hailstorm, stack for Diamond Dust
- Tank swap to manage slashing resistance stacks
- Kill Ice Soldiers quickly to minimize Diamond Dust damage
- Free players frozen in Ice Boulders immediately
- Phase 3 has a soft enrage - push DPS before stacks get too high
- Sequential Icicle Impact: stay near first ring, move into its space
