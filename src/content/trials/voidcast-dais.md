The Voidcast Dais is a level 90 trial introduced in Patch 6.4, where players face Golbez, the main antagonist of the Endwalker post-patch story. The battle takes place on a square arena where falling off the edges results in death.

## Golbez

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Terrastorm | **AoE** | A sphere moves toward an intercardinal edge, marking a circle AoE that covers roughly half the arena. Move to the opposite side. |
| Crescent Blade | **Cleave** | Half-room cleave toward a random player. Move behind Golbez. |
| Arctic Assault | **AoE** | Icicle walls create line AoEs across two arena quadrants. Move to the safe quadrants. |
| Void Meteor | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster hitting both tanks with four comet impacts followed by massive damage. Both tanks stack with cooldowns. |
| Lingering Spark | **AoE** | Baited AoEs on all players. Group up to bait, then dodge together. |
| Binding Cold | **Raidwide** | Raidwide magic damage with a 9-second frost DoT. Heal through the damage over time. |
| Azdaja's Shadow | **AoE** | Transition mechanic into Phase 2. Moderate raidwide damage. |

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Black Fang | **Raidwide** | Ultimate ability dealing pulses of moderate damage followed by a heavy raidwide hit. Save mitigation for this. |
| Shadow Crescent | **Cleave** | Enhanced Crescent Blade with AoE markers on all players, followed by either a point-blank AoE or stack marker. Spread for markers, then react to the follow-up. |
| Dragon's Descent | **Knockback** | Knockback marker on a targeted player. Position so the knockback doesn't push you or others off the arena. |

**Notes:** Watch for the sphere movement during Terrastorm to identify the safe half of the arena. Coordinate mitigation for Black Fang as it deals significant damage across multiple hits.
