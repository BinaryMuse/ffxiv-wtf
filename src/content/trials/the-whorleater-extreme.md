The Extreme version of the Leviathan trial. An 8-player encounter on a ship platform with complex diving patterns and strict add management.

## Leviathan

### Core Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Body Slam | **Knockback** | Platform tilts based on water geyser location - run opposite |
| Spinning Dive | **AoE** | Marked by geyser, Leviathan charges across platform |
| Tidal Wave | **Enrage** | Use Elemental Converter (needs 27-33 power) to survive |

### Dive Patterns

| Pattern | Coverage | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Vertical Dive | 50% of platform | Dodge left or right |
| Horizontal Dive | 33% of platform | Dodge to safe third |

**Note:** Water balls fall on random players before each dive, dealing damage and applying Slow.

### Elemental Converter

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Converter Gauge | - | Must reach 27-33 before Tidal Wave |
| Gyre Spume | **Adds** | Yellow adds that drain gauge and deal AoE when killed |

**Priority:** Kill Gyre Spumes immediately but be aware of their death AoE.

### Add Phases

| Add | Priority | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Wavetooth Sahagin | High | Casts Dreadstorm (fear puddles) - kill first |
| Wavespine Sahagin | Medium | Casts Hydroshot - creates damaging water pools |

### Head & Tail Management

| Target | Reflection | Attack With |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| Head (Veil of the Whorl) | Reflects ranged | Melee attacks only |
| Tail (Mantle of the Whorl) | Reflects magic | Physical attacks only |

**Tank Split:** One tank on head, one on tail.

### Other Attacks

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Grand Fall | **AoE** | Multiple players targeted, creates pools |
| Aqua Breath | **Tankbuster** | Tank-targeted AoE |
| Dread Tide | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on tank |
| Whirlpool | **AoE** | Water drops on random targets |
| Tidal Roar | **Raidwide** | Group-wide damage |

**Tips:**
- After Body Slam destroys railings, positioning becomes critical
- Gyre Spumes are always top priority
- Kill Wavetooth before Wavespine to prevent fear mechanics
- Watch geyser positions to predict dive directions
- Converter gauge of 33 is safer than 27
- Coordinate physical/magical attacks on head/tail properly
