A crossover trial featuring Guardian Arkveld from Monster Hunter Wilds. Unlocked through the sidequest "The White Wanderer" from the Dawn Hunt Tracker in Tuliyollal after completing the Dawntrail MSQ.

## Guardian Arkveld

A massive wyvern-type creature that fights using Monster Hunter-style mechanics. Unlike standard FFXIV fights, this boss has **no cast bars** - all attacks are telegraphed through animations and visual tells only.

### Special Buffs

| Buff | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Well-done, Steak | Passive buff healing ~5,000 HP every 3 seconds and increasing max HP |
| Mega Potion | 10 charges of a duty action that instantly restores HP to full |

### Primary Tells

Watch for two main attack patterns:
- **Roar** - Indicated by roaring animation
- **Chainblade Blow** - Indicated by raising arm

### Phase 1 Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Roar | **Raidwide** | Heavy party-wide magic damage - heal and mitigate |
| Chainblade Blow | **Cleave** | Half-room cleave on one side, followed by large ground AoE - move opposite side |
| Wyvern's Siegeflight | **Cleave** | Boss jumps to edge and charges across with ground cracks |
| Red Cracks | **AoE** | Red ground cracks mean side cleaves - middle is safe |
| White Cracks | **AoE** | White ground cracks mean middle cleave - sides are safe |

### Rush and Stack Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rush | **AoE** | Three red line AoE charges with expanding ring explosions |
| Chainblade Charge | **Stack** | Stack marker on random healer - group together |

### Tower Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Small Towers | **Soak** | Six towers spawn at cardinals requiring players to soak |
| Large Towers | **Tankbuster** | Two larger towers need tanks with mitigation |

**Tips:**
- Pay close attention to animations since there are no cast bars
- Red cracks = stay middle, White cracks = stay on sides
- Use Mega Potion liberally - you have 10 charges
- The Well-done Steak buff provides significant passive healing
- After clearing, interact with Arkveld's head for Guardian Scales
- Rewards Veldian Weapon Coffer (ilvl 745)
