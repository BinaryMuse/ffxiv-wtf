The first trial of Dawntrail, unlocked during the MSQ quest "The Skyruin." Players face Valigarmanda, a powerful three-element dragon atop the frozen peaks.

## Valigarmanda

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Susurrant Breath | **Raidwide** | Party-wide magic damage |
| Ice Talon | **Tankbuster** | Dual tankbuster hitting both tanks with high damage and 15s Frostbite DoT; tanks split apart and use cooldowns |
| Strangling Coil | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE from boss; move away from melee range |
| Slithering Strike | **Cleave** | Frontal cone AoE; move to front corners or behind |
| Skyruin | **AoE** | Arena transforms with phase-specific mechanics (Thunder/Ice phases) |
| Disaster Zone | **AoE** | Ring AoE around arena edges; move to center |
| Thunderous Breath | **Raidwide** | Lightning-infused party damage |
| Calamitous Cry | **Spread** | Spread markers on players |
| Ruinfall | **AoE** | Proximity-based AoEs; move away from impact points |

**Thunder Phase:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Blighted Bolt | **AoE** | Lightning strikes across arena in patterns |
| Chilling Cataclysm | **AoE** | Ice circles expand outward |

**Ice Phase:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Northern Cross | **AoE** | Large cross-shaped AoE |
| Freezing Dust | **AoE** | Arena sections freeze; movement required to avoid freezing |

**Notes:** Watch the boss's animations and text callouts: "Tempestuous wind" = donut AoE (stay center), "Gathers strength" = point-blank (stay back), "Blinding energies" = cone (go to corners). Getting hit by mechanics inflicts Trauma (stacking DoT); two hits causes Concussion (stun + damage down).
