The sequel to Battle on the Big Bridge from the Hildibrand Adventures. This 8-player trial features Gilgamesh and his faithful companion Enkidu fighting together.

## Phase 1: Gilgamesh & Enkidu

Both bosses are active simultaneously. Phase ends when Enkidu is defeated.

### Enkidu's Abilities

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Aero II | **AoE** | Targeted AoE on random players - spread to avoid overlap |
| Web | **AoE** | Single-target Slow + Heavy debuff - removable with Esuna |
| Eerie Soundwave | **Cleave** | Conal AoE on random player |
| White Wind | - | Heals Gilgamesh - interrupt or burn through |

### Gilgamesh's Abilities

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Bitter End | **Cleave** | Frontal cone tankbuster - face away from party |
| Missile | **AoE** | Marks a player, deals 50% of their current HP |
| Pradamante | **AoE** | Untelegraphed circle AoE with moderate damage |
| Whirlwind Slash | **AoE** | Telegraphed circle AoE |
| Minimum | **AoE** | Shrinks two players and tethers them - walk to partner to restore |
| Chicken | **AoE** | Transforms multiple players into chickens (including healers) |

## Phase 2: Gilgamesh Alone

Begins after Enkidu dies. Gilgamesh continues using most previous attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| All Gilgamesh abilities | - | Same mechanics, but debuffs can affect multiple players |

**Tips:**
- Kill Enkidu first to simplify the fight
- Interrupt White Wind if possible to prevent Gilgamesh healing
- Esuna the Web debuff promptly
- Minimum-tethered players should move together to break free
- Prepare for multiple chickened players in Phase 2
