Storm's Crown is a level 90 trial introduced in Patch 6.2, where players face Barbariccia, the Empress of the Winds and one of Golbez's four Archfiends. The fight features wind-based mechanics across two distinct phases.

## Barbariccia

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Void Aero IV | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable party-wide damage. Heal and mitigate. |
| Savage Barbery (Line) | **AoE** | Line AoE down the arena center, followed by her thrown sword creating a circle AoE where it lands. |
| Savage Barbery (Ring) | **AoE** | Large ring AoE with a safe spot close to the boss. Move into her hitbox. |
| Hair Raid | **Cleave** | Conal AoE after Savage Barbery. Run toward Barbariccia's sword to avoid the cleave. |
| Hair Spray | **Spread** | Later versions of Hair Raid include spread markers on all players. Spread out while dodging. |
| Void Aero III | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage with a stacking debuff. Both tanks should group together with defensive cooldowns. |
| Teasing Tangles | **AoE** | Four circle AoEs appear and players get tethered to each. You cannot leave your circle without taking damage and a DoT debuff. Stay inside until the mechanic resolves. |

### Phase 2 - Wind Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wind Border | **AoE** | A wind border appears around the arena edge. Entering it deals damage and inflicts Windburn DoT. |
| Curling Iron | **Knockback** | Transition ability with knockback. Position to avoid being knocked into the wind border. |

**Notes:** Watch Barbariccia's sword position to determine which Savage Barbery variant is coming. Stay aware of your tether during Teasing Tangles—breaking it early results in unnecessary damage and debuffs.
