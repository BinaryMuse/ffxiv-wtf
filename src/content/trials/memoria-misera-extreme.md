An extreme-only trial featuring Varis yae Galvus, the former High Legatus of Garlemald. This is part of the Save the Queen storyline and has no normal mode equivalent.

## Varis yae Galvus

### Pre-Fight Setup

Assign spread positions (cardinal/intercardinal) and stack groups before pulling:
- **Spread positions:** Tanks, healers, and DPS at assigned cardinals/intercardinals
- **Stack North/West:** MT, H1, D1, D3
- **Stack South/East:** OT, H2, D2, D4

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Altius | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE that spawns X marks on player positions during the cast. Bait marks behind the boss with MT staying north to keep the line of fire straight. |
| Citius | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster requiring mitigation and potential tank swap. |
| Alea Iacta Est | **Cleave** | Multi-hit cleave attack in front of the boss. Stay behind or to the sides. |
| Festina Lente | **Stack** | Stack marker requiring the party to group up to split damage. |
| Ignis Est | **AoE** | Fire-based AoE requiring movement to safe zones. |

**Notes:** Phase 1 establishes the baseline mechanics. Focus on positioning and learning the telegraph patterns.

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Magitek Burst | **Raidwide** | Heavy raid-wide damage. |
| Gunshield | **AoE** | Boss deploys a gunshield creating directional attacks. Watch the shield orientation. |
| Loaded Gunshield | **AoE** | Enhanced gunshield attacks with additional AoE patterns. |
| Gunshield Spark | **Spread** | Spread mechanic during gunshield phase. DPS spread to assigned positions with healers and tanks in designated spots. |
| Ventus Est | **Knockback** | Wind-based knockback. Position to avoid being knocked into danger zones or off the platform. |

**Notes:** Gunshield mechanics require watching the boss's shield orientation to determine safe zones.

### Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Combined Mechanics | **AoE** | All previous mechanics combine with shorter windows between them. |
| Terminus Est | **AoE** | X-shaped markers appear on the ground. Move to avoid the resulting cross explosions. |
| Fortius | **AoE** | Enhanced version of earlier attacks with larger AoEs. |
| Enrage | **Enrage** | Must defeat Varis before enrage timer expires. |

**Notes:** The fight requires coordination between all eight players to manage raid-wide damage, positional mechanics, and targeted abilities across all three phases. Communication is key for gunshield positioning.
