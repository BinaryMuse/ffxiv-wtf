The Extreme version of the Zoraal Ja trial, the second Extreme trial of Dawntrail. Unlocked by speaking with the Wandering Minstrel in Tuliyollal.

## Zoraal Ja

**Phase 1:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Actualize | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage; prepare defensive cooldowns |
| Soul Overflow | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide magical damage granting boss Damage Up buff |
| Double-edged Swords | **Cleave** | Half-circle AoEs hitting front/back then opposite; stand in second AoE zone first |
| Patricidal Pique | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster requiring defensive cooldowns |
| Calamity's Edge | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage followed by circular AoEs from NPCs |
| Vorpal Trail | **AoE** | Swords fan inward from edges, meet middle, fan outward |

**Phase 2 (Distorted Arena):**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dawn of an Age | **Raidwide** | Massive damage; arena becomes central platform with four surrounding platforms |
| Smiting Circuit | **AoE** | Point-blank or donut based on blade spawn location |
| Vollok | **AoE** | Square AoEs on half arena; can spawn on other platforms |
| Bitter Reaping | **Tankbuster** | Dual tank buster on both tanks |
| Sync | **AoE** | Blade patterns replicate across platforms via blue diagonal connectors |
| Blade Sync | **AoE** | Combined platform blade mechanics |
| Actualize | **Raidwide** | Returns arena to normal with heavy damage |

**Notes:** Arena edges have uncleansable Bleeding DoT zone—avoid completely. Falling off resets the fight without consuming Willful stacks. Rewards include i710 accessories, mount (rare), music, and totems for exchange.
