A trial against Innocence, the Lightwarden of Kholusia. As Vauthry's true form, this angelic horror must be vanquished to bring darkness back to the First.

## Innocence

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Realmrazer | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raid-wide damage. Healers prepare group heals. |
| Heavenly Host | **Adds** | Spawns Forgiven Shame and Forgiven Venery adds. Kill them quickly before they cause problems. |
| Sinsphere | **AoE** | Meteor towers appear. At least one player must stand in each tower to prevent raid-wide damage. |
| Daybreak | **AoE** | Puddle AoEs spawn on the ground. Move out of the marked areas. |
| Enthrall | **Gaze** | Gaze attack from the boss. Look away to avoid Confusion debuff. |
| Exalted Wing | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE signaling phase transition. Heavy damage. |
| Exalted Plumes | **AoE** | Additional AoEs during phase transition. |

**Notes:** Keep adds under control and handle towers properly. The gaze mechanic is easy to miss during chaos.

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Righteous Bolt | **Tankbuster** | Heavy single-target tankbuster applying a lightning debuff. Tank swap recommended. |
| Winged Reprobation | **AoE** | Two swords target random players with circular AoEs. Targeted players move to arena edges to redirect beam damage away from party. |
| Rightful Reprobation | **AoE** | Blue telegraphed barrage that fires, then returns after a few seconds. Stay just outside the original damage zone to avoid the return volley. |
| Rotating Swords | **AoE** | Line AoEs radiating from boss that slowly rotate around the arena. Keep moving to avoid getting hit. |
| Drop of Light | **Spread** | Two players targeted with large circular AoEs. Spread out to prevent overlap damage. |
| Shadowreaver | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raid-wide damage. |

**Notes:** Watch for the returning projectiles from Rightful Reprobation—they catch many players off guard.

### Add Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Forgiven Shame (x4) | **Adds** | Four Shame adds spawn and must be destroyed. |
| Forgiven Venery | **Adds** | One Venery add spawns with the Shames. |
| Ultimate Blast | **Enrage** | If adds aren't killed before the boss gauge reaches 100%, massive damage occurs. |

**Notes:** AoE down the adds quickly. Use defensive cooldowns for the damage spike when the gauge completes.
