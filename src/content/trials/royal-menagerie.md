The climactic battle against Shinryu, the great wyrm merged with Zenos yae Galvus. This fight takes place on a square platform with no railings—falling off means death.

## Shinryu

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tidal Wave | **Knockback** | A water spout appears on the arena edge. Move close to it to avoid being knocked off. Creates water puddles that give Fire Resist Up but Lightning Resist Down. |
| Hellfire | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage that applies Burns. Stand in water puddles to reduce damage and prevent the burn debuff. |
| Judgment Bolt | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide lightning damage. **Avoid** standing in water puddles as they increase lightning damage taken. |
| Akh Morn | **Tankbuster** | Multi-hit tankbuster targeting the two highest enmity players. Tanks should stack together and use cooldowns. |
| Hypernova | **Stack** | Marks one player with a stack marker. Party stacks to share damage. |
| Spikecycles | **AoE** | Alternating line AoE attacks across the arena. |
| Earth Breath | **Cleave** | Marks two players with cone attacks. Marked players can group in a corner to minimize area affected. |
| Ice Storm | **Raidwide** | Mild raid-wide AoE damage. |

**Notes:** The Corrupted Aether gauge fills throughout the fight, triggering primal-based attacks. Pay attention to puddle placement for fire vs. lightning attacks.

### Phase 2 (Add Phase)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Wave 1 | **Adds** / **AoE** | Three proximity AoEs spawn. Move to the opposite side of the arena. |
| Wave 2 | **Adds** / **AoE** | Four proximity AoEs spawn. Move to a safe corner. |
| Wave 3 | **Adds** / **AoE** | Proximity AoEs on all corners and center. Stand between two corner AoEs. |
| Protostar | **Raidwide** | High-damage raid attack after all adds are defeated. Healers prepare. |
| Active Time Maneuver | **Enrage** | Button-mash sequence required to survive. |

**Notes:** Kill adds quickly between each wave. Failing the Active Time Maneuver is a wipe.

### Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Line Attack | **AoE** | Large line attack across the arena. Move to one of two safe corners. |
| Tail Slap | **AoE** | Marks arena tiles with line attacks. Destroys tiles on second hit—manage your platform space. |
| Burning Chains | **Spread** | Two players are marked with chains. Run away from each other to break them. |
| Levin Bolt | **Spread** | Marked players spread away from others to avoid splash damage. |

**Notes:** Manage arena space carefully as Tail Slap can destroy tiles. Prioritize not falling off the shrinking platform.
