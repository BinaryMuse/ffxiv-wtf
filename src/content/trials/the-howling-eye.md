The third primal trial in the main story. Garuda, Lady of the Vortex, introduces the concept of using environmental cover to survive attacks.

## Garuda

The Lady of the Vortex, primal of the Ixal beast tribe.

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Slipstream | **Cleave** | Frontal line attack - move to sides or behind Garuda |
| Mistral Song | **Raidwide** | Garuda teleports and fires a devastating wave - hide behind rock pillars |
| Razor Plumes | **Adds** | Adds spawn after Shriek that damage pillars - destroy them quickly |

**Notes:**
- Rock pillars around the arena are essential for survival
- Tank should keep Garuda away from pillars to prevent damage
- Garuda performs two Mistral Song casts - one from north, one from south
- Note: Mistral Shriek is a separate PBAoE ability (close-range AoE around Garuda)

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Arena Edge | **AoE** | Purple damaging AoE appears around arena edges - stay away |
| Mistral Song | **Raidwide** | Another wave attack - move to her sides or behind |

**Tips:**
- If Garuda teleports to center, run behind her
- If she teleports to an edge, run to the opposite side
- Keep pillars alive as long as possible for cover
- Don't stand near pillars unless actively using them for protection
