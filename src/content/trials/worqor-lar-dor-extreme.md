The Extreme version of the Valigarmanda trial, the first Extreme trial of Dawntrail. Requires precise execution and coordination.

## Valigarmanda

**Phase 1:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spikecicle | **AoE** | Ice projectiles curve across arena leaving explosive balls; watch blue lines to predict pattern, position opposite first AoE row |
| Mountain Fire | **Tankbuster** | 6-hit tank buster; tank swap after 2 hits due to Fire Vulnerability. DPS/healers stand behind pillar on main tank |
| Triscourge | **Spread** | Three debuffs: Calamity's Bolt (spread to avoid paralysis), Calamity's Chill (keep moving to avoid freeze), Calamity's Inferno (healers run to edges, slowly return) |

**Telegraph Attacks (watch text):**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| "Tempestuous wind" | **AoE** | Donut AoE; pair up in safe zone at center |
| "Gathers in strength" | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE; stay back from boss |
| "Blinding energies" | **Cleave** | Cone AoE; run to northern corners |
| "Volcanic eruption imminent" | **AoE** | One arena side hit; watch erupting volcano side |

**Phase 2:**

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ruins Foretold | **Raidwide** | Phase transition |
| Avalanche | **AoE** | Ice wall mechanics with positional requirements |
| Ice Pillars | **AoE** | Pillars spawn creating safe/danger zones |
| Freezing Dust | **AoE** | Must keep moving or freeze |
| Fire/Ice/Lightning Combos | **AoE** | Combined elemental debuffs with positioning |

**Notes:** Ensure Item Level 690+ fully-melded gear, level 100 raid food, and potions. Being hit by avoidable AoEs inflicts Damage Down, Vulnerability Up, or Sustained Damage stacks. Coordinate tank swaps during Mountain Fire.
