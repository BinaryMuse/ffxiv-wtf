The second primal trial in the main story. Titan, Lord of Crags, introduces platform-based mechanics and the danger of falling off the arena.

## Titan

The Lord of Crags, primal of the Kobold beast tribe.

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rock Buster | **Tankbuster** | Frontal cleave - tank faces Titan away from party |
| Tumult | **Raidwide** | Multiple stomps dealing party-wide damage - healers prepare |
| Landslide | **Cleave** | Long rectangular AoE with knockback - will push players off the platform |
| Geocrush | **Raidwide** | Titan jumps and lands, dealing proximity damage and shrinking the arena |

**Notes:**
- Stand on the inner edge of the red ring during Geocrush to minimize damage
- Getting knocked off the platform by Landslide is instant death

### Phase 2 (Heart Phase)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Granite Gaol | **Adds** | A player becomes trapped in stone - destroy to free them |
| Weight of the Land | **AoE** | Circular puddle AoEs under 1-2 players - move out quickly |
| Titan's Heart | **DPS Check** | Must destroy heart before Earthen Fury or party wipes |
| Earthen Fury | **Enrage** | Instant kill if heart is not destroyed in time |

**Tips:**
- Free gaol'd players immediately
- Use Limit Break on the heart if DPS is tight
- After heart phase, Titan uses all previous mechanics with increased intensity
