Face Shiva, the primal of ice, in this 8-player trial. The Lady of Frost alternates between weapon stances with unique mechanics for each.

## Shiva

Shiva alternates between Staff and Sword forms, each with different abilities.

### Staff Form (Damage Up Buff)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hailstorm | **AoE** | Random players marked with icicle circles - spread out to avoid overlap |
| Absolute Zero | **Raidwide** | Low room-wide AoE - heal through it |

### Sword & Shield Form (Healing/Defense Buff)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ice Brand | **Cleave** | High-damage frontal cone - tank faces Shiva away |
| Glacier Bash | **AoE** | Stun attack - avoid by moving behind Shiva |
| Heavenly Strike | **Knockback** | Low damage knockback |

### Both Forms

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Icicle Impact | **AoE** | 4 circles at edges + 1 center - stand in safe spots between |

### Phase Transition (~60% HP)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ice Soldiers | **Adds** | 4 adds spawn - kill quickly to reduce Diamond Dust damage |
| Diamond Dust | **Raidwide** | Freezes all players - damage scales with surviving adds |

**Notes:** Stack in middle for Diamond Dust. Kill all Ice Soldiers before it casts.

### Phase 2 (Post Diamond Dust)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dreams of Ice | **Enrage** | Shiva gains stacking damage buffs over time |
| Permafrost | **AoE** | Creates slippery ice floor with Thin Ice status and Frostbite DoT |
| Icicle Impact (Wave) | **AoE** | Rings appear sequentially - stay near first ring, move into its space |
| Ice Wall | **AoE** | Arena edges freeze players - destroy Ice Boulders to free trapped allies |

**Tips:**
- Watch Shiva's weapon to anticipate mechanics
- Spread during Hailstorm, stack during Diamond Dust
- Phase 2 has a soft enrage - kill before damage stacks too high
- Free players trapped in Ice Boulders immediately
