The second of the Warring Triad trials, facing Sophia the Goddess. Features the unique balance/tilt mechanic that becomes central to the Extreme version.

## Sophia

The Goddess of balance fights with her Daughter (a floating head) and introduces mechanics based on equilibrium and balance.

### Phase 1 (100% - 70%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Thunder II | **Cleave** | Conal AoE in front inflicting Electrocution DoT - tank faces away |
| Aero III | **Knockback** | Point-blank AoE with knockback - move away from Sophia |
| Thunder III | **AoE** | Ring AoE - safe spots inside her hitbox or far outside the ring |
| Light Dew | **AoE** | Daughter fires a line laser inflicting Vulnerability Up - dodge when head reappears |
| Quasar | **AoE** | Proximity AoE in center - run to arena edges |
| Onrush | **AoE** | Sophia charges from east/west edge - avoid the line |
| Cintamani | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable room-wide damage - healers top off |

**Notes:**
- Watch for Daughter's position to anticipate Light Dew direction
- Stay close for Thunder III (inside hitbox) or far away (outside ring)

### Phase 2 (Cloudy Heavens)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cloudy Heavens | **Mechanic** | Transition cast at ~70% HP |
| Balance Tilt | **Mechanic** | Arena tilts based on meteor/add weight - move to the heavier side |
| Scales of Balance | **AoE** | Adds and meteors appear affecting platform balance |

**Notes:**
- The tilt mechanic is simplified in normal mode
- Move to whichever side has more weight to avoid sliding off

### Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| All Previous | - | Phase 1 mechanics continue with balance mechanics |
| Execute | **Raidwide** | Heavy damage at low HP - burn boss quickly |

**Tips:**
- The arena has no walls - falling is instant death
- For tilt: count meteors/tethers and move to the heavier side
- Keep healing through Cintamani casts
