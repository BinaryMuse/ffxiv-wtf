The Dark Inside is a level 83 trial unlocked through the Main Scenario Quest "The Martyr" on Mare Lamentorum. Players face Zodiark, the god of darkness, on a square arena with no railings. The fight emphasizes mechanic overlap and arena-wide awareness.

## Zodiark

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Kokytos | **Raidwide** | Reduces all party members' HP to 1. Healers must quickly restore health. |
| Exoterikos (Triangle) | **AoE** | Arcane Sigil with triangle symbol fires a conal AoE toward the arena center. Stand near the wall where the sigil appears. |
| Exoterikos (Square) | **AoE** | Arcane Sigil with square symbol covers half the arena. Move to the opposite side. |
| Ania | **Tankbuster** | Heavy damage on the main tank. Use defensive cooldowns. |
| Phlegethon | **AoE** | Ground AoEs placed under all players. Spread out and move. |
| Styx | **Stack** | Multi-hit stack marker dealing heavy damage. Group up and use mitigation. |
| Paradeigma | **AoE** | Summons untargetable behemoths (circular AoEs) or pythons (line AoEs). Dodge accordingly. |

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Astral Flow | **AoE** | Fetters players in place while the arena rotates 90 degrees. Remember your new orientation. |
| Adikia | **AoE** | Two large semi-circular AoEs in succession. Move close or far from Zodiark to dodge. |
| Triple Esoteric Ray | **AoE** | Sequential line AoEs from Zodiark. Dodge into the first one that fires as it becomes safe. |
| Algedon | **Cleave** | Zodiark moves to a corner and charges down the middle of the arena. Move to the opposite corner to avoid. |

**Notes:** The difficulty of this fight comes from frequent mechanic combinations rather than individual attacks. Watch for Exoterikos patterns combined with other mechanics. Stay near the center during knockback-adjacent mechanics to avoid falling off the arena.
