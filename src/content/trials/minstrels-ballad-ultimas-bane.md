The extreme version of the Ultima Weapon fight. This challenging trial combines mechanics from all three primal phases (Garuda, Titan, Ifrit) into an intense encounter.

## Ultima Weapon

The Ultima Weapon in its most powerful form, channeling the abilities of the primals it has absorbed. This fight requires precise coordination and tank swaps.

### Tank Management

Ultima applies a stacking debuff (Viscous Aetheroplasm) that becomes lethal at high stacks. Tanks must coordinate swaps using Provoke before stacks reach dangerous levels (typically 3-5 stacks).

### Phase 1 - Garuda Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Garuda Appearance | **Mechanic** | Garuda appears in arena center |
| Directional Cone | **Cleave** | Garuda casts a cone AoE - position behind her |
| Eye of the Storm | **AoE** | Creates a dangerous ring on the outer edge - move to inner edge |
| Titan Landing | **Raidwide** | After Eye of the Storm, sprint to arena edge as Titan lands center |

### Phase 2 - Titan Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Geocrush | **Raidwide** | Titan lands in center dealing proximity damage |
| Sand Circles | **AoE** | Four circles spawn in center - find the small gap or run to outer edge |
| Weight of the Land | **AoE** | Circular AoEs under random players |

### Phase 3 - Ifrit Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ifrit Charges | **Cleave** | Ifrit performs charging attacks across the arena |
| Targeted Explosions | **Spread** | Random players receive AoE markers - spread out |
| Black Orbs | **Mechanic** | Orbs spawn that must be touched to prevent party wipes |

### Final Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Laser Attacks | **AoE** | Various laser patterns to dodge |
| Magitek Droids | **Adds** | Destructible adds that must be killed |
| Combined Mechanics | **Mechanic** | All previous mechanics combine in rapid succession |

**Tips:**
- Tanks must communicate clearly for debuff swaps
- Learn the visual tells for each primal phase
- Two tanks and two healers are essential
- This is legacy content but still requires coordination when synced
