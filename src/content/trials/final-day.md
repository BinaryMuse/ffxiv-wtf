The Final Day is the climactic level 90 trial of Endwalker's main scenario, where players confront The Endsinger (Meteion's transformed state) at the edge of the universe in Ultima Thule. The arena has no railings, making knockback mechanics particularly dangerous.

## The Endsinger

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Elegeia | **AoE** | Summons two planets orbiting at different speeds. The faster planet collides with the slower one, creating a massive AoE. Stand opposite the slower planet and mirror its movement. |
| Galaxias | **Knockback** | Knockback from the arena center. Stay near the middle to avoid being knocked off the edge. |
| Elenchos (Mouth) | **AoE** | If the Endsinger's mouth glows purple, a center line AoE fires. Move to the sides. |
| Elenchos (Eyes) | **AoE** | If the Endsinger's eyes glow purple, two side line AoEs fire. Stay in the center. |
| Death's Embrace | **Spread** | Cone AoEs target all players. Spread out around the boss. Feathers drop afterward leaving circle AoEs—move away immediately. |
| Aporrhoia | **AoE** | Four heads summon and shoot line AoEs down the arena. Stand between two faces to avoid. |
| Hubris | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster hitting both tanks in an AoE. Tanks should spread apart from each other. |
| Ekstasis | **AoE** | Expanding black puddles appear on the ground. Move to the arena edge to avoid them. |

### Phase 2 - Time Manipulation

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Elegeia Unforgotten | **AoE** | Freezes the planet explosion in time. Remember where the safe spot will be. |
| Fatalism | **AoE** | Rewinds time to trigger the frozen planet explosion again. Return to the safe spot. |
| Galaxias + Fatalism | **Knockback** | Use the knockback to position yourself in the safe zone for the rewound explosion. |

**Notes:** Keep track of planet positions during Elegeia mechanics. The faster planet always collides with the slower one, so watch movement speeds carefully. Stay centered for Galaxias knockbacks to have maximum control over your landing position.
