The concluding trial of the Dragonsong War storyline, facing the enraged Nidhogg. A four-phase encounter with significant healing and DPS checks.

## Nidhogg

The great wyrm Nidhogg, consumed by rage and vengeance, attacks with devastating fire and physical abilities.

### Phase 1 (100% - 70%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Deafening Bellow | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE damage - healers top off the party |
| Horrid Roar | **AoE** | Circular AoEs appear as lines below random players - move away quickly |
| Hot Wing | **AoE** | Line AoEs on both sides of the boss - stay centered under Nidhogg |
| The Scarlet Whisper | **Cleave** | Untelegraphed cone attack - tank faces Nidhogg away |
| Cauterize | **AoE** | Boss flies away, Horrid Roar on players, then center divebomb - dodge circles, then move to sides |

**Notes:**
- Tank should always keep Nidhogg facing away from the party
- During Cauterize, dodge Horrid Roar circles first, then move left or right to avoid the divebomb

### Phase 2 - Add Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Shadow Dragon | **Adds** | Cannot be tanked, moves randomly with sweep attacks - **kill first** |
| Shadow Brobinyak | **Adds** | Can be tanked - pick up and face away |
| Shadow Falak | **Adds** | Can be tanked - pick up and face away |
| Breath Attacks | **Cleave** | All adds have frontal breaths - never stand in front |
| Damage Down | **Debuff** | Shadow Dragon's sweeps inflict Damage Down |

**Notes:**
- Focus the Shadow Dragon first as it cannot be controlled
- Tanks grab and separate Brobinyak and Falak
- Stay behind all adds to avoid breath attacks

### Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Final Chorus | **Raidwide** | Transition damage after add phase |
| Enhanced Mechanics | - | All Phase 1 mechanics continue with increased damage |
| Hot Tail | **AoE** | Line AoE through the center - dodge to sides |

**Notes:**
- Hot Wing (sides) and Hot Tail (center) may alternate - watch the cast bar

### Phase 4

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Akh Morn | **Stack** | Stack marker - party stacks to share damage |
| All Previous | - | Phase 1 and 3 mechanics continue |

**Tips:**
- This fight has heavy healing requirements - healers should use cooldowns throughout
- Watch for Hot Wing vs Hot Tail - they look similar but have opposite safe zones
- Coordinate tank cooldowns for The Scarlet Whisper
