The second trial of the Sorrow of Werlyt questline, featuring the Emerald Weapon—a Garlean warmachina with devastating blade and laser attacks.

## Emerald Weapon

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Emerald Shot | **Tankbuster** | Multi-hit tankbuster that opens the fight. Use mitigation for consecutive hits. |
| Optimized Ultima | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable raid-wide damage. Healers prepare group healing. |
| Emerald Beam | **AoE** | Boss spins clockwise with front/rear cone AoEs while pulsing circle AoEs expand outward. Zigzag while running clockwise to avoid both mechanics simultaneously. |
| Magitek Magnetism | **AoE** | Two mines appear with positive (+) or negative (-) charges. Same charges repel; opposite charges attract. Position based on anticipated mine movement before detonation. |
| Sidescathe | **Cleave** | Hits half the arena. Dodge by standing on the side without the glowing wing. |
| Split | **Knockback** | Boss sheds legs with pushback effect. Position carefully to avoid falling off arena edges. |
| Divide Et Impera | **Tankbuster** / **Raidwide** | Hits the tank first, then everyone else. Spacing required between tank and party. |

**Notes:** For Emerald Beam, run clockwise and weave between the expanding circles. The magnet mechanic requires quick identification of charge polarity.

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Phase Transition | **AoE** | Cutscene plays and arena changes. The new arena no longer has fatal edges. |
| Checkpoint | **AoE** | If the party wipes after Phase 2 starts, you restart from Phase 2, not Phase 1. |
| Tertius Terminus Est | **AoE** | Large X-shaped AoE markers. Avoid the marked zones. |
| Primus/Secundus Terminus Est | **AoE** | Sequential AoE attacks requiring movement between safe zones. |
| Legio Phantasmatis | **Adds** | Phantom soldiers spawn and attack. Handle adds while avoiding ground AoEs. |

**Notes:** Phase 2 is more forgiving due to the arena change. Focus on learning the Terminus Est patterns.
