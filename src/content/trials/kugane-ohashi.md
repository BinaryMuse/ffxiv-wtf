A Hildibrand questline trial where Yojimbo is revealed to be Gilgamesh in disguise, seeking revenge from your previous encounters. This comedic trial brings together attacks from previous Gilgamesh battles.

## Yojimbo (Gilgamesh)

### Phase 1 (Yojimbo)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Bitter End | **Tankbuster** | Tank buster attack. Use defensive cooldowns. |
| Metta-giri | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage. Healers prepare. |
| Inoshikacho | **AoE** | Blue butterflies appear on player positions and explode in circular AoEs. Move out of markers. |
| Gekko | **AoE** | Players receive circular AoEs that become donut-shaped attacks. Move appropriately. |
| Kasha | **AoE** | Similar to Gekko with circular-to-donut transformation. |

**Notes:** This phase uses Yojimbo's samurai-themed attacks.

### Phase 2 (Gilgamesh)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Unveiling | **Raidwide** | Yojimbo transforms into Gilgamesh form. |
| Tiny Song | **Stack** | Players get dark red markers and must stand with at least one other player to avoid being turned tiny. |
| Dragon Night | **Raidwide** | Gilgamesh summons dragon heads that apply vulnerability stacks and deal raid-wide damage. |
| Zantetsuken | **AoE** | Signature Gilgamesh attack. Dodge the indicated area. |

**Notes:** Stack for Tiny Song markers or you'll be shrunk. The fight combines classic Gilgamesh mechanics with new Yojimbo-themed abilities.
