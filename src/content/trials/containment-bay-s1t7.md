The first of the Warring Triad trials, facing Sephirot the Fiend. A two-phase encounter with an add phase transition featuring knockback and tower mechanics.

## Sephirot

The first member of the Warring Triad, Sephirot is a massive tree-like primal who towers over the arena.

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tifferet | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage - use cooldowns |
| Ein Sof | **AoE** | Expanding circle AoE from center - move away |
| Fiendish Rage | **Raidwide** | High party-wide damage - triggers add phase |
| Chesed | **AoE** | Multiple ground AoEs - dodge the marked areas |

**Notes:**
- Keep Sephirot relatively centered
- Phase 1 ends when Fiendish Rage is cast

### Add Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Storm of Words | **Adds** | Sephirot summons adds that must be defeated |
| Ratzon | **AoE** | Ground AoEs during add phase - keep moving |
| Cochma | **Adds** | Larger add that must be focused down |

**Notes:**
- Kill adds efficiently to minimize damage taken
- Dodge ground AoEs while focusing adds

### Phase 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Yellow Flash | **Mechanic** | Floor flashes yellow - move immediately to avoid damage |
| Da'at | **AoE** | Green and orange markers - move to your assigned color position |
| Yesod | **Knockback** | Blue circles indicate knockback - position carefully to avoid falling |
| Pillar of Mercy | **Stack** | Towers appear - at least one player must stand in each |
| Ein Sof Ohr | **Raidwide** | Heavy room-wide damage |

**Notes:**
- Watch for green vs orange debuffs during Da'at - green goes front, orange goes to towers
- Don't stand on damaged (cracked) floor sections during knockbacks

**Tips:**
- The arena has no walls in Phase 2 - falling is instant death
- Coordinate tower soaking - each tower needs at least one player
- Use knockback immunity skills during Yesod if available
