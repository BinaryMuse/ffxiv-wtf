The climactic trial of the Heavensward main scenario, facing King Thordan and his Knights of the Round. Features multiple phases with knight summoning mechanics.

## King Thordan

The corrupted Archbishop wields the power of Nidhogg's eye and commands the Heavens' Ward knights in battle.

### Phase 1 (100% - ~50%)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ascalon's Mercy | **Cleave** | Three frontal cones inflicting Windburn DoT - move behind Thordan |
| Ancient Quaga | **Raidwide** | Room-wide AoE damage - healers prepare |
| Lightning Storm | **Spread** | Random players targeted with circle AoEs - spread apart |
| Meteorain | **AoE** | Additional ground AoEs after Lightning Storm - keep moving |

**Notes:**
- Tank should keep Thordan faced away from the party
- Spread for Lightning Storm, then dodge Meteorain circles

### Phase 2 - Knights of the Round

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ser Janlenoux & Adelphel | **Adds** | Dash around creating light orbs that explode with Bleeding - position between orbs |
| Ser Vellguine | **AoE** | Creates a center line AoE - move to either side |
| Ser Hermenost | **Stack** | Creates three pillars needing at least one player each - failure causes party Paralysis |
| Ser Haumeric | **Spread** | Marks players with ice circles - go to edges and don't overlap |
| The Dragon's Eye | **Gaze** | Thordan gains buff, then uses The Dragon's Gaze - **look away before cast finishes** |

**Notes:**
- Watch for knight spawns and react to their specific mechanics
- Always look away when you see The Dragon's Eye buff appear
- Coordinate for Hermenost's pillars

### Phase 3 (15% HP)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sword Plant | **Mechanic** | Thordan plants his sword and summons multiple knights |
| Comet/Meteor | **Adds** | Destroy these circles before they land |
| Janlenoux & Adelphel | **Adds** | Keep separated - they strengthen each other when close |
| Ser Zephirin | **DPS Check** | Kill before Sacred Cross finishes casting |
| Ser Guerrique | **AoE** | Concentric AoEs - safe spot is behind his cone |
| Ultimate End | **Raidwide** | Massive party-wide damage - stack and use shields/regen |

**Tips:**
- Prioritize Zephirin when he appears - Sacred Cross is a wipe if it completes
- After Ultimate End, Thordan receives Damage Down and can be finished quickly
- Use Limit Break on Comets if DPS is tight
