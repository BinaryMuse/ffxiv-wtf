The climactic trial of A Realm Reborn's main story. Face the Ultima Weapon and its pilot in an 8-player spectacle fight with unskippable cutscenes.

## The Ultima Weapon

### Phase 1: Primal Powers

The Ultima Weapon cycles through abilities absorbed from the three Primals.

#### Titan's Power

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Earthen Fury | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Geocrush | **AoE** | Proximity-based AoE - move away from the marker |
| Landslide | **Cleave** | Line AoE attack |
| Weight of the Land | **AoE** | Multiple circle AoEs to dodge |
| Granite Interment | **Adds** | Locks players in gaols - break them free |

#### Garuda's Power

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Aerial Blast | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Eye of the Storm | **AoE** | Donut AoE - stay in the center safe zone |
| Mistral Shriek | **AoE** | Circle AoE around boss - stay away but inside safe zone |
| Vortex Barrier | - | Boss becomes invulnerable briefly |

#### Ifrit's Power

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hellfire | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Radiant Plume | **AoE** | Circle AoEs to dodge |
| Vulcan Burst | **Knockback** | Knockback from center - move back in or use prevention |
| Radiant Blaze | **Raidwide** | Arena-wide damage followed by cutscene |

### Phase 2: Lahabrea

After the cutscene, face the Ascian with new attacks.

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Homing Lasers | **Tankbuster** | High damage on tank |
| Magitek Ray | **Cleave** | Three line attacks in sequence |
| Homing Ray | **Spread** | Circle markers on players - spread out |
| Tank Purge | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Aetheric Boom | **Knockback** | Knockback attack from boss |

**Notes:**
- This trial was moved from The Praetorium dungeon in patch 6.1
- Contains unskippable story cutscenes
- Damage requirements are only relevant in the final phase
- Focus on dodging mechanics rather than DPS for most of the fight
