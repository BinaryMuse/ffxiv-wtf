The extreme version of the Suzaku trial, featuring the infamous "Simon Says" color platform mechanic.

## Suzaku

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Screams of the Damned | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage. |
| Cremate | **Tankbuster** | Magic-based tankbuster. Tank uses cooldowns. |
| Fleeting Summer | **Cleave** | Conal AoE. Avoid the indicated area. |
| Rout | **AoE** | Suzaku charges at center (line AoE). Players receive Rekindle (circular AoE). |
| Rekindle | **Spread** | Circular AoEs on players. Spread without clipping others or hitting dead birds on the ground. |
| Eternal Flame | **Adds** | Scarlet Ladies spawn. Stack them in center and kill. If not killed in center, they revive during Phoenix Down. |
| Southron Star | **Raidwide** | Group-wide AoE damage. |
| Ruthless Refrain | **Knockback** | Move close to center to avoid being knocked off the arena. |

**Notes:** Don't let Rekindle AoEs hit dead birds or they will revive.

### Phoenix Down

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Position Assignment | **AoE** | H1D1 → W-NW, MT/D2 → N-NE, H2D3 → E-SE, OT/D4 → S-SW |
| Small Feathers | **Adds** | Kill small feathers at assigned positions to create safe zones (Wing and a Prayer). |
| Large Feathers | **AoE** | Drop with big AoEs. Position to avoid. |
| Rekindle (DPS) | **Spread** | All DPS are marked with Rekindle during this phase. |

**Notes:** Coordinate positions before the fight. Kill your assigned small feather before it explodes.

### Arrow/Orb Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Orbs | **AoE** | One player stands in each circle, facing the direction indicated by arrows. |
| Runes | **AoE** | Move next to the section Suzaku is targeting, then move into it after it explodes. |
| Incandescent Interlude | **Stack** | Meteors require at least one player per meteor to soak. |
| Phantom Flurry | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster. Tanks prepare cooldowns. |

**Notes:** Arrow direction matters for orb mechanics—face the correct way.

### Phase 2 (Simon Says)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Color Platforms | **AoE** | Floor displays colored patterns. Move to corresponding safe zones based on the sequence. |
| Mesmerizing Melody | **AoE** | Suzaku displays a sequence of colors. Players must respond by moving to matching safe zones. |
| Pull/Push AoE | **Knockback** | Second Simon Says adds either a pull or push, complicating movement on the small platform. |
| Center Hole | **AoE** | The platform has a hole in the center. Don't fall in while navigating colors. |

**Notes:** Simon Says is latency-sensitive. Chase explosions by running in circles at the appropriate color trigger. The second set is significantly harder than the first.
