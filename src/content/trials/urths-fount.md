Face the legendary primal Odin in this challenging 8-player trial. A high-mobility fight with a strict DPS check.

## Odin

The Dark Divinity. This fight requires constant movement and has a hard enrage mechanic.

### Core Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Shin-Zantetsuken | **Enrage** | Triggers at 15% HP or ~7 minutes - must kill Odin within 30s |
| Gungnir | **Adds** | 3 players receive AoE circles that spawn spears - kill spears immediately |
| Bleed Debuff | - | Active while Gungnir spears exist - incurable, damages all players |

### Raidwide Attacks

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hall of Sorrow | **Raidwide** | Unavoidable party-wide damage |
| Einherjar | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage + 30s bleed debuff (dispellable) |

### Avoidable Attacks

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hall of Stone | **Gaze** | Ground AoEs that petrify for 8s - avoid them |
| Valknut | **AoE** | Point-blank AoE around Odin - move away |
| Gagnrath | **AoE** | Line AoEs from arena edges in two patterns - dodge accordingly |

### Debuffs

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hall of Lead | **AoE** | Targets 3 random players with damage + slow - dispellable |

**Tips:**
- This is a highly mobile fight - keep moving
- Kill Gungnir spears immediately to stop the incurable bleed
- Dispel Hall of Lead slow debuffs when possible
- Use Party Finder - this trial only appears in Mentor Roulette
- Meet the DPS check or Shin-Zantetsuken will wipe the party
- Hall of Stone appears more frequently in later phases
