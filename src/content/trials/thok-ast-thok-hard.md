The first trial of Heavensward, pitting players against Ravana, Lord of the Hive. An 8-player encounter introducing the Seeing Wing/Tail parry mechanic and Bloodlust gauge.

## Ravana

The Lord of the Hive wields multiple blades and uses dance-like combat abilities. Watch his parry buffs to know where to attack from.

### Phase 1 (100% - 60% Bloodlust)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Seeing Wing | **AoE** | Parries attacks from sides - attack from front or back |
| Seeing Tail | **AoE** | Parries attacks from front/back - attack from sides |
| Blinding Blade | **Tankbuster** | Heavy hit on tank - use cooldowns |
| Prelude to Slaughter | **AoE** | Series of attacks: small PBAoE (move out), line AoEs (find gaps), donut AoE (move in) |
| Slaughter | **AoE** | Cross-pattern line AoEs with safe corners, marked players get circle AoEs - spread apart |

**Notes:**
- Pay attention to which Seeing buff Ravana casts and position accordingly
- Line markers during Slaughter inflict Burn DoT if touched

### Phase 2 - Gana Phase (60% Bloodlust)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Spirit Gana | **Adds** | Summoned adds - kill after Moon Ganas |
| Moon Gana | **Adds** | Priority targets - **kill these first** |
| Falling Laughter | **Enrage** | Cast if adds survive too long - spawns extra swords |
| Bloody Fuller | **Raidwide** | Party lifted and attacked with swords - too many swords = wipe |

**Notes:**
- Always prioritize Moon Ganas to prevent sword buildup
- The more swords spawned, the more damage Bloody Fuller deals

### Phase 3 (Post-Gana)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Pillars of Heaven | **Knockback** | Knockback from arena center - position near edges away from fence gaps |
| Surpanakha | **Cleave** | Targets second-highest enmity with red marker - separate from group |
| All Previous | - | All Phase 1 mechanics continue |

**Tips:**
- Tank swap or use invuln for Surpanakha if needed
- Stay away from fence gaps during Pillars of Heaven
- Continue watching Seeing Wing/Tail throughout the fight
