A Monster Hunter World crossover trial featuring Rathalos. This fight uses unique mechanics—Rathalos cannot be tanked traditionally and all players must actively dodge based on animation tells.

## Rathalos

### Phase 1 (Grounded)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Roar | **Raidwide** | Arena-wide damage at regular intervals. |
| Tail Swipe | **Cleave** | 360-degree counterclockwise tail swing. Move away when you see the wind-up. |
| Bite/Swipe | **Cleave** | Frontal attacks. Stay at his left flank to avoid. |
| Fireball | **AoE** | Ranged fire attack. Watch for head movements indicating the attack. |

**Notes:** Rathalos has no auto-attacks and cannot be tanked. All players must actively dodge based on direction and animation tells. Melee should stick to his left flank.

### Phase 2 (Adds)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Add Spawns | **Adds** | Additional enemies spawn during this phase. Prioritize dangerous adds. |

**Notes:** Clear adds while continuing to dodge Rathalos attacks.

### Phase 3 (Flying)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dive Attack | **AoE** | Rathalos takes to the air and performs diving attacks with different patterns. |
| Aerial Fireball | **AoE** | Ranged fire attacks from the air. |

**Notes:** Watch for Rathalos's flight patterns and position yourself to avoid dive attacks.
