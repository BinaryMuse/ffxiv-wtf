Hard mode version of the Garuda trial. An 8-player encounter with emphasis on managing adds and protecting stone pillars.

## Garuda

The Lady of the Vortex with enhanced abilities and stricter mechanics.

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Downburst | **Cleave** | Frontal cone attack - tank faces Garuda away from party |
| Friction | **AoE** | Wind burst at random player that damages nearby pillars |
| Wicked Wheel | **AoE** | Circle attack around Garuda - maintain distance |
| Slipstream | **Cleave** | Line attack with stun risk - move behind then back in front |
| Mistral Song | **Raidwide** | Teleports to north and fires wave - hide behind stone pillars |

### Add Phases

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Razor Plumes | **Adds** | Spawn after Mistral Song - kill quickly to save pillars |
| Satin Plume | **Adds** | Can put party to sleep - prioritize killing |
| Spiny Plume | **Adds** | Builds stacks on tank - swap before 3 stacks |

**Notes:**
- Tank should position Garuda at the north side of the arena
- Keep Garuda away from pillars to prevent Wicked Wheel damage
- Don't stand near pillars unless actively hiding behind them
- Kill Razor Plumes immediately to preserve your cover

**Tips:**
- Stone pillars are your lifeline - protect them
- Focus Satin Plumes first to prevent party-wide sleep
- Tank positioning is critical for this fight
- Main tank keeps Garuda north, facing away from party and pillars
