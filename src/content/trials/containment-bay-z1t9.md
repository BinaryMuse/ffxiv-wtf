The final trial of the Warring Triad series, facing Zurvan the Demon. Features a collapsing arena and multiple phase transitions.

## Zurvan

The last of the Warring Triad, Zurvan is a demonic primal who fights across multiple arenas with increasingly complex mechanics.

### Phase 1 - Circular Arena

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Metal Cutter | **Cleave** | Conal AoE - tank faces Zurvan away from the party |
| Flare Star | **AoE** | Circle AoEs on random players - stack to bait, then move out |
| Arena Collapse | **Mechanic** | Quarter sections fall away (East → North → West → South) - don't stand on falling sections |
| The Purge | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage before transition |

**Notes:**
- Watch for arena sections glowing before they collapse
- Standing on a collapsing section is instant death

### Phase 2 - Soar

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Soar | **AoE** | Two clones dash along line AoE indicators - move to safe zones |
| Flaming Halberd | **Spread** | Two players marked with large circle AoEs - spread far apart |
| Demonic Dive | **Stack** | Stack marker on one player - everyone except marked spread player stacks |

**Notes:**
- Soar pattern indicates safe zones - watch clone positions
- During Flaming Halberd, marked players should be on opposite sides of the arena

### Phase 3 - Ice Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Biting Halberd | **Cleave** | Cone AoE - dodge to sides |
| Southern Cross | **AoE** | Cross-pattern ground AoE |
| Ciclicle | **AoE** | Ice AoEs that leave puddles |
| Tail End | **AoE** | Rear cone attack |

**Notes:**
- An ice pad appears under the boss
- Stack for AoE puddles, then move together

### Phase 4 - Demon Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Tyrfing | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank damage - use cooldowns |
| All Previous | - | Mechanics from earlier phases repeat |

**Tips:**
- Modern gear allows skipping some mechanics with high DPS
- Watch arena edges in Phase 2 - there's a dangerous AoE zone
- Coordinate stack/spread mechanics clearly
