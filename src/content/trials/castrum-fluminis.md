A trial against Tsukuyomi, the primal incarnation of the divinity of night and moon, after Yotsuyu transforms using aether-infused crystals.

## Tsukuyomi

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Torment Unto Death | **Tankbuster** / **Cleave** | High damage frontal cone. Tank should face boss away from party and use cooldowns. |
| Reprimand | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide AoE damage. Healers prepare. |
| Steel of the Underworld | **Cleave** | Large frontal cone AoE. Move to the sides or behind the boss. |
| Lead of the Underworld | **Stack** | Stack marker on a player. All players group up to share damage. |
| Midnight Haze | **Adds** | Summons two adds that tether and move toward each other. Destroy both before they collide. |
| Zashiki-asobi | **AoE** | Fans appear around the arena (one center, six on edges). They explode in sequence starting with center. Stand beside first AoE, then move to center after it explodes. |
| Nightbloom | **Raidwide** | Ends Phase 1 with raid-wide damage. Inflicts "Down for the Count" stun. |

**Notes:** Nightfall randomly selects between Steel of the Underworld, Reprimand, Midnight Haze, or Lead of the Underworld.

### Phase 2 (Add Phase)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Specters | **Adds** | Specter of the Matriarch, Patriarch, Asahi, and Zenos spawn. Defeat them quickly. |
| Suffering Gauge | **Enrage** | Adds fill Tsukuyomi's Suffering gauge. Higher gauge = more damage from next Nightbloom. |
| Zenos Knockback | **Knockback** | Zenos will knockback and stun players before the phase ends. |

**Notes:** Focus down adds as quickly as possible to minimize the Suffering gauge.

### Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Selenomancy | **AoE** | Arena splits into white and black sections. Standing in the wrong color causes damage and debuffs. |

**Notes:** Selenomancy appears twice with increased complexity the second time. Watch for visual cues on which side is safe.
