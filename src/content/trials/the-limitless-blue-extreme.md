Extreme version of the Bismarck fight featuring strict DPS checks, elemental debuffs, and tighter coordination requirements. Recommended i170+ weapon and ~10k HP.

## Bismarck

The whale primal returns with new elemental mechanics and stricter timing on island integrity management.

### Phase 1 - Chitin Carapace

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Cetacean Rage | **Raidwide** | Bismarck charges the island - first hit cannot be blocked, subsequent hits require Field |
| Vuk'maii Vundu | **Adds** | Northern caster add - must be killed quickly |
| Lan'maii Vundu | **Adds** | Southern add - must be killed quickly |
| Dragonkillers | **Mechanic** | Fire both to harpoon Bismarck - coordinate timing |
| Chitin Carapace | **DPS Check** | Board Bismarck and destroy carapace - limited time before falling off |
| Turbulence | **Debuff** | Stacking debuff while on Bismarck's back - 3+ stacks is fatal |
| Whaleback DoT | **Raidwide** | Continuous damage on Bismarck's back |

**Notes:**
- Turbulence stacks require jumping off before 3 stacks
- May need multiple boarding cycles to destroy Carapace
- Use the Magitek Field Generator to block charge damage

### Phase 2 - Sanuwa Phase

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| So'Sanuwa | **Adds** | Tank this add where it spawns - faces away from party |
| Ul'Sanuwa | **Adds** | Tank this add where it spawns - faces away from party |
| Will of the Wind | **Debuff** | Wind debuff - stay away from So'Sanuwa or take reflected damage |
| Will of the Water | **Debuff** | Water debuff - stay away from Ul'Sanuwa or take reflected damage |
| Debuff Reapply | **Mechanic** | Debuffs reapply every 50 seconds - may swap positions |
| Breach Blast | **Raidwide** | High damage after add phase - scales with add HP |

**Notes:**
- **Critical**: Check your debuff and attack the OPPOSITE Sanuwa
- Wind debuffed players attack Ul'Sanuwa (Water)
- Water debuffed players attack So'Sanuwa (Wind)
- Tanks should use heavy cooldowns - both adds hit hard

### Phase 3 - Corona

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Add Waves | **Adds** | More adds spawn - defeat to ready Dragonkillers |
| Corona | **DPS Check** | Board Bismarck and attack the Corona |
| Weather Effects | **AoE** | Tornadoes and weather hazards during phase |
| Island Integrity | **Enrage** | Island destruction = wipe |

**Notes:**
- Field Generator is destroyed after Phase 2 - no more blocking charges
- Must kill Bismarck before island integrity reaches 0

**Tips:**
- Assign players to each Dragonkiller before pull
- Call out debuffs during Sanuwa phase
- Pre-position for add spawns
- Use knockback immunity for weather mechanics when possible
- Healers should pre-shield before Bismarck charges
