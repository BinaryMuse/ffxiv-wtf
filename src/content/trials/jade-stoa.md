A trial against Byakko, the white tiger of the Four Lords. This fight introduces mechanics that become more punishing in Extreme mode.

## Byakko

### Phase 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Storm Pulse | **Raidwide** | Raidwide AoE damage hitting twice. Repeated throughout the fight. |
| Heavenly Strike | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tankbuster requiring mitigation. |
| State of Shock | **Stack** | Byakko grabs the main target and throws them to a stack AoE. Players must stack to soak damage or the grabbed player dies. |
| Sweep the Leg | **Cleave** | 270-degree AoE with only the space behind the boss being safe. Move behind Byakko. |
| Unrelenting Anguish | **AoE** | Byakko shoots rotating energy orbs. Dodge between the orbs. |
| Fire and Lightning | **AoE** | Wide column AoE targeting a single player. Move out of the path. |

**Notes:** Being hit by avoidable attacks gives a Vulnerability Up debuff. Minimize mistakes to reduce incoming damage.

### Phase 2 (Intermission)

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Hakutei | **Adds** | Byakko summons his tiger Hakutei. Tanks should face Hakutei away from the group. |
| White Herald | **Spread** | Spread marker requiring distance from other players. |
| The Roar of Thunder | **Enrage** | DPS must burn Hakutei quickly to reduce damage from this attack. |

**Notes:** Keep Hakutei facing away due to its cleave attack. Burn it down before Roar of Thunder.

### Phase 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Falling Phase | **AoE** | Players fall from the sky while dodging donut AoEs. Safe zone is in Byakko's hitbox. |
| Red Bubbles | **AoE** | Avoid the red bubbles while falling. |

**Notes:** Stay close to Byakko's hitbox during the falling sequence to avoid the donut AoEs.
