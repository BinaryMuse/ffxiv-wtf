A trial required for the Zodiac Relic Weapon questline. Defeat the Dhorme Chimera to obtain Alumina Salts.

## Dhorme Chimera

A fearsome beast with multiple heads, each capable of different elemental attacks. The fight teaches players to watch for voice attacks that require specific positioning.

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Lion's Breath | **Cleave** | Frontal cone dealing moderate damage - tanks face away from party |
| Dragon's Breath | **Cleave** | Front-left cone causing Paralysis |
| Ram's Breath | **Cleave** | Front-right cone causing Heavy debuff |
| Scorpion's Sting | **Cleave** | Rear cone causing Poison - melee avoid standing behind |

### Voice Attacks

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| The Dragon's Voice | **AoE** | Large ring AoE causing Paralysis - move close to boss to avoid |
| The Ram's Voice | **AoE** | Point-blank circular AoE causing Frostbite - move away from boss |
| Cacophony | **AoE** | Summons electrical orb targeting random player - explodes on contact causing Paralysis |
| Ram's Keeper | **AoE** | Ice circles under random players causing Frostbite and Heavy |

**Tips:**
- Position behind or to the side of the Chimera to avoid frontal breaths
- Watch cast bar closely for Voice attacks - Dragon's Voice means get close, Ram's Voice means run away
- Healers should be ready with Esuna to clear status effects
- Both Voice attacks can be interrupted with stuns
- This content can be easily completed unsynced at higher levels
