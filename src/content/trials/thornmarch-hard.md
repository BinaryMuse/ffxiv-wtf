Face Good King Moggle Mog XII and his royal guard in this whimsical but challenging 8-player trial.

## King Moggle Mog XII

### Phase 1: Mogglesguard Waves

The battle begins with waves of Moogle guards that must be defeated.

#### Wave 1

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Whiskerwall Kupdi Koop | **Adds** | Paladin moogle - casts Spinning Mogshield (circle AoE, vulnerability) |

#### Wave 2

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Ruffletuft Kupta Kapa | **Adds** | Warrior moogle - 1000-koponze Swipe (cone AoE, vulnerability) |
| Woolywart Kupqu Kogi | **Adds** | Archer moogle - Mograin of Death targets random players |

#### Wave 3

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Puksi Piko | **Adds** | Bard moogle - Moggleday Night Fever (cone, forces dancing) |
| Furryfoot Kupli Kipp | **Adds** | White Mage moogle - Pom Holy damages all; **kill first** |

**Notes:** Kill the White Mage first to prevent healing the Bard.

#### Wave 4

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Pukna Pako | **Adds** | Rogue moogle - Moogle Thrust targets tank |
| Pukla Puki | **Adds** | Black Mage moogle - Pom Meteor places 4 circles; one player each |

### Phase 2: The King

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Memento Moogle | **Raidwide** | Party-wide damage |
| Good King's Decree | **Adds** | Revives some defeated moogles (untargetable) |
| Pom Stone III | **AoE** | Center circle + outer donut AoE |

**Tips:**
- Focus all attacks on the King - revived moogles are untargetable
- Continue avoiding moogle abilities from Phase 1
- Spread for Mograin of Death
- Stack one player per meteor circle during Pom Meteor
