The climactic trial of the Dawntrail MSQ, where players face Zoraal Ja, the King of Resolve, in his fortress Everkeep.

## Zoraal Ja

**Phase 1** (until ~30% HP):

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Actualize | **Raidwide** | Massive raid-wide damage |
| Soul Overflow | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage granting boss stacking Damage Up buff |
| Patricidal Pique | **Tankbuster** | Heavy tank buster requiring cooldowns |
| Double-edged Swords | **Cleave** | Half-arena cleaves that alternate; position in second AoE zone to dodge first, then move |
| Calamity's Edge | **Raidwide** | Raid-wide damage; NPCs become circular AoEs to avoid |
| Vorpal Trail | **AoE** | Sword line AoEs fan in from edges, meet center, fan outward |

**Phase 2** (floating platforms):

| Mechanic | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Dawn of an Age | **Raidwide** | Transforms arena; central platform with four surrounding inaccessible platforms |
| Smiting Circuit | **AoE** | Point-blank or donut based on blade spawn |
| Vollok | **AoE** | Square AoEs on half arena from sword spawns |
| Bitter Reaping | **Tankbuster** | Dual tank buster |
| Sync | **AoE** | Blade patterns copy across platforms using blue connectors |
| Blade Sync | **AoE** | Combined platform mechanics |
| Actualize | **Raidwide** | Returns arena to normal |

**Notes:** Arena perimeter has uncleansable Bleeding DoT zone. Phase 2 has fall hazards—falling resets the fight. Trusts provide one Willful stack for surviving a lethal hit.
